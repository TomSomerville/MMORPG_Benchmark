# Professions: Cooking, Fishing, First Aid

[← Master Plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md) · Prev: [Crafting](crafting.md) · Next: [Leveling Path](../05-zones/00-leveling-path.md)

Every character can learn all three. Same ranks and cap (225) as primary professions.

## 1. Cooking

Needs a fire (campfire object, or *Basic Campfire* spell: flint + tinder + wood from vendors). Food is eaten seated, out of combat. Cooked food from skill 50+ also grants **Well Fed**: +Stamina and +Spirit for 15 min (values in table).

## 2. Fishing

Needs a Fishing Pole (vendor); lures raise effective skill. Cast into water → wait for the bobber to splash (5–25 s) → click within 2 s. Fish below the zone's skill "get away".
