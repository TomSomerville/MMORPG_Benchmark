## 1. Eyrie routes and fares

Flight speed is 32 yards per second. A player may fly only between eyries they have discovered. Multi-leg flights are automatic; the fare and time are the sum of the legs. Fares get the reputation discount of the continent's capital.

| Solhaven ↔ Millbrook | 50c | 1 min 10 s |
| Solhaven ↔ Harrow's Cross | 70c | 1 min 30 s |

| Harrow's Cross ↔ Cooper's Hollow | 50c | 1 min |
| Millbrook ↔ Harrow's Cross | 50c | 1 min 10 s |
| Harrow's Cross ↔ Thornwick | 1s 20c | 2 min |

## 2. Reputation

Ladder: Neutral 0 · Friendly 3,000 · Honored 6,000 · Revered 12,000 · Exalted 21,000 (cumulative from Neutral). Quests give reputation equal to 25% of their XP (minimum 10) with the reputation of their zone:

| Zone or source | Reputation |
|---|---|
| Brightmead, Harrowfield, Russetwood, Veilmoor, Dawn's Stand quests | Solhaven |
| Embercrag, Stonewake Dale, Stoneheart Deeps, Ironroot, Cinderfall | Hammerdeep |
| Thornwick Marches quests; Gallowmere Crypts bosses (75 each) | Thornwick Wardens (and half as much with Solhaven) |
| Kite's Rest and Spire Camp side quests; Blackspade kills (5 each to Honored); a Pitmaster's Chest (150) | Freeholders of Kite's Rest |
| Main Chain 2 quests in any zone; Hollowspire Keep bosses (100 each); raid bosses (150; Vaelric 300) | The Athenaeum |
| Wildpact contact quests (TM-23, IR-22, VM-19, VM-20, VM-35) and CF-17–20 | No launch reputation; flags are stored for future allegiances |
| Cloth donations: 60 Linen, 60 Wool, 60 Silk, once each per capital | 350 each with that capital |

Humans start Friendly with Solhaven and at 2,000 of Neutral with Hammerdeep; dwarves the reverse.

## 4. Fishing tables

Each cast yields one catch from the pool's table. "Junk" is vendor-grey (weeds, driftwood). Below the pool's skill the fish gets away at a chance of (skill needed − skill) ÷ 100.

| Water | Skill | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Junk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silverrun Creek (Brightmead) | 25 | Silverrun Minnow 60% | Brook Trout 22% | Old Boot 3% (gives *Lucky Boot*) | 15% |
| Millrace and mill-pool (Stonewake) | 25 | Dale Trout 56% | Longjaw Pike 22% | River Pearl 5% | 17% |
| Longstrand (Harrowfield) | 75 | Strand Mackerel 58% | Slitherskin Eel 22% | Sealed Crate 4% | 16% |
| Forest streams (Russetwood) | 75 | Copperleaf Dace 58% | Stream Eel 22% | Sealed Crate 4% | 16% |
| Rimewater Pond (Embercrag) | 75 | Rime Smelt 62% | Frost Perch 23% | — | 15% |
| Blackwater Fen (Thornwick) | 150 | Fen Catfish 56% | Murk Eel 24% | Waterlogged Locket 2% (until TM-34 is done) | 18% |
| Highland streams (Ironroot) | 150 | Highland Salmon 58% | Stonescale Carp 25% | — | 17% |
| Moor pools (Veilmoor) | 225 | Ghost Koi 55% | Lantern Eel 24% | Barrow-ring 2% (random green ring, ilvl 28–33) | 19% |
| Sulfur Pools and Emberwell (Cinderfall) | 225 | Sulfur Snapper 55% | Firefin 24% | Small Ember Core 4% | 17% |

Fishing pools (visible schools, level 10+ waters): 4–6 casts each, always the uncommon fish, respawn 10 minutes, 6–8 pool positions per water with 3 active. Sealed Crate: 3–6 Linen or Wool Cloth, 1–2 healing potions of the zone's tier, 20% chance of a world green. Capital harbours fish as the 75-skill table with no rare.
