# World Layout — The Riftlands

[← World Layout Index](00-world-layout.md) · Zone: [The Riftlands](../05-zones/t4-riftlands.md) · Prev: [Cinderfall Barrens](06-cinderfall-barrens.md) · Next: [Veilmoor](08-veilmoor.md)

Notation and rules are in the [index](00-world-layout.md). Each grid cell is 240 m.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size and offset | 2,400 × 2,400 m · Meridia (3100, 3000) |
| Shape | A dry plateau cut by the Rift, a canyon running from the centre to the south-east that deepens to 250 m. Mesas stand above the plateau; the buried city lies on the Rift floor. No water anywhere |
| Elevation | Plateau 100 m (the zone's reference level) · mesa tops 140–160 m · Kite's Rest mesa 170 m · Spoil Heaps 90 m · Works 80 m · Rift floor −150 m at Spire Camp, −250 m at the Maw-Scar |
| Landmark | Kite's Rest, a free-standing mesa at (1200, 1300) flying kites from its mast; from the Rift floor, the half-buried spire of Tor Vael at (1960, 700) |
| Exits | West pass → Thornwick (0, 1000) · north track → Veilmoor (1100, 2400) · the sealed Imperial Causeway stub at (2350, 60) |
| Vertical travel | Kite's Rest rope-lift (plateau ↔ town) · the Widow's Shaft lift (Works ↔ shaft bottom, needs the chime) · the switchback trail (plateau ↔ Rift floor, 900 m long, starts at (1500, 1050)) |
