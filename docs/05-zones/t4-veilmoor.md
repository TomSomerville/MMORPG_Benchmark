Iron, Gold (rare), Starmetal (barrows) · Wyrmtongue (tors), Veilshade (everywhere in fog hollows), Ghostlily (Hollowspire Grounds) · Medium → Heavy Leather · Silk · Fishing: moor pools (225).

## Flow

Lanternwatch (01–10) → Pilgrim's Cairn (11–22) → Vigil's End (23–28, 33, 34, 36) → Hollowspire Keep (29–31) → (32 → Riftlands for the raid).
