| | |
|---|---|
| Theme | Wind-scoured pine highlands over the richest iron seams in Khazrun. Ogres raid from the east with suspiciously good weapons; miners at Deepseam come up from the dark with empty eyes |
| Hubs | **Ironroot Hold** (mountain-dwarf keep-town, 10–14) · **Ramshead Lodge** (hill-dwarf hunting lodge and steadings, 14–18) · **Deepseam Camp** (mountain-dwarf mine head, 16–20) |
| Cultures | The zone where both dwarven ways of life share one map: Hallborn in the keep and the mines, Dalefolk on the slopes with their herds. The ogre raids fall on the hill steadings first and the Hold's garrison is slow to ride out — a running grievance in the zone's quest text |
| Dungeon | None in zone — players cross to Meridia (ship from Hammerdeep's Seagate, then eyrie) for [Gallowmere Crypts](../07-instances/dungeons.md#1-gallowmere-crypts-1316) (breadcrumb IR-15) |
| Exits | North → Stonewake Dale; Deep Gate rail-tunnel → Stoneheart Deeps; south → Cinderfall Barrens; east → Stonemaw Steppes (blocked: collapsed pass, future Wildpact ogre zone) |

## Gathering & professions

Tin, Silver (rare), **Iron** (Deepseam, Brandforge, peaks — the best iron zone) · Crownleaf, Emberweed (Brandforge vents), Frostcap (peaks, night) · Medium Leather, Rimewing Scale · Wool; Silk at Brandforge · Fishing: highland streams (150).

## Flow

Ironroot Hold (01–09, 12) → Warcamp & Smugglers' Cut (10, 11, 13, 14) → Hammerdeep / ship to the Crypts (15) → Ramshead Lodge (16–22, 26) → Hammerdeep round-trip (23) → Brandforge Pass (24, 25, 35) → Deepseam (27–34) → ship to Brandspire (36).
