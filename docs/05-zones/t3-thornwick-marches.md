**Chain boss:** *Magister Corvin Ashgrave, the Branded Lord* (20 elite, top of Brandspire) — Phase 1: Fireball, Brand of Obedience (mind-controls one player 6 s; solo: 4 s stun), Flame Ring. Phase 2 at 40%: his chest splits to reveal the crown shard — Hollow Pulse (Shadow AoE every 10 s), summons 3 Hollowed Husks. Tuned for 3 players at 20, soloable at 23+.
