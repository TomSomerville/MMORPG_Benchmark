The dam, its sluice-houses and the reservoir behind it are scenery and a friendly post: nothing in the zone is fought on the dam or in water. The dam is the zone's background and its tension. The Dalefolk never wanted it; the Hallborn built it to feed Hammerdeep's forges and the Rootvault, and the Dale-Reeve and the Dammaster have not exchanged a civil word in eleven years.

In this zone a creature's aggression is set by the sub-area it lives in, by the level at which a player arrives there: the first number of the sub-area's level band in the table above, and 5 for the Deep Sink. Where that is 1 to 3 the creatures are not aggressive: they do not attack a player who comes near, they fight back when attacked, and they call for help as usual. Where it is 4 or more they are aggressive. The creature's own level does not decide it, and the rares, the elite and the mini-boss follow the same rule ([NPC AI §2](../02-systems/npc-ai-and-world.md#2-ai-state-machine)). Decided 2026-11-25; to be adjusted when the owner has played the zone.

- **Not aggressive:** Sunbank Steading, the Barley Terraces, Greywall Quarry, the Larch Hangers, the Gravelkin Sinks above the Deep Sink, and the Hop-Yards. A level 4 Hop-Blight Beetle is therefore not aggressive.
- **Aggressive:** the Deep Sink (Chief Gnarr), Daleside Adit, the Old Brewery, and Ridgefold & the High Pastures (Greedy Gund, Old Bitterhorn).

Open: whether Chief Gnarr's two Bonechewer guards, who stand on the ramp down to the Deep Sink, count as creatures of the Deep Sink (aggressive) or of the Sinks (not).

**Rares:** *Greedy Gund* (4 rustler chief, High Pastures — *Gund's Lasso-Belt*, leather waist) · *Old Bitterhorn* (5 elite giant ram, ridge top — quest target).
**Mini-boss:** *Chief Gnarr* (5, the Deep Sink) — Gnaw, Frenzy (self-enrage at 30%), 2 Bonechewer guards.

The Rustler Hound-Master is the zone's hard enemy: his whistle brings every rustler and hound within reach, so the Hound-Masters of the High Pastures stand 30 m apart and are met one at a time.

**Totals:** 18 quests · ≈ 4,060 quest XP — identical structure and XP to Brightmead so neither race levels faster · about 200–280 kills in all, about 75 of them asked for by quests → player leaves at about level 7. The kills beyond the quests' 75 are the creatures that stand between a player and the objectives, and the repeat kills for items that drop by chance; the zone is meant to play this way and its creatures are not thinned (decided 2026-11-25). Measured on 2026-11-25, on the layout before the overhaul: the zone bot run completed 18 of 18 quests in 93 minutes, with 101 kills and 4 deaths, leaving at level 6 (`build/evidence/T-M3-02/zone-run.json`); about a quarter of that time was running, over a quest flow line of 8.9 km. Open: the run must be measured again on the overhauled zone, whose flow line is to be about 4 km; with every camp's count unchanged in smaller camps, camp overlap and respawn timing are checked in that run. (The run measured earlier for the dwarf start, 211 kills in 96 minutes, 4 deaths, level 7, was of Embercrag Hollow as it was at levels 1–5.)

**Story note.** A warm spring with two small troubles: sinkholes in the low fields and rustlers on the ridge. Both turn out to be paid for. The rustlers carry coins with a hand burned into them, and the rock-eaters' chief keeps a slate that tallies fields undermined against lumps of forge-slag paid, every lump pressed with the same hand. The last line on the slate is not yet paid: the dam. The Dale-Reeve will not take that to the Dammaster on rumour, so the slate goes up to the mountain, to Anvilrest or to Lanternhold. The heavier story that grows from it (Slagborn sappers, cinder-charges, Sapper-Chief Dolga Blackmatch) is told in [Embercrag Hollow](t2-embercrag-hollow.md) at levels 5–10, where the charges meant for the dam are found and stopped at their source; the dam itself is never attacked in this zone.

## Gathering & professions

Copper Veins (terrace walls, the quarry face, the adit's spoil banks) · Sunpetal and Hearthroot on field margins and the feet of the walls, Dewleaf under the larches · Light skinning from goats and bears · Linen from Gravelkin and rustlers · Fishing: the Millrace and the mill-pool below Hopstead (skill 25). Apprentice trainers for all professions in Hopstead, with First Aid at Ridgefold, Engineering at the Tinker's sluice hut and Fishing at the mill-pool. Cooking is introduced by quest ST-05.

## Flow

Sunbank (01–06) → the Sinks (07) → Hopstead (08) → Hop-Yards and the herb banks (09, 10) → Ridgefold and the High Pastures (11, 12, 15, 17) → the Old Brewery (13) → Daleside Adit (14) → the Deep Sink (16) → Bouldergate Pass or the Adit (18).

The first seven quests are played inside the starter bowl, where nothing is more than 190 m of walking from the start point: the buzzards are about 65 m away, the first sink 150 m, the billies and the quarry 150–185 m. ST-08 leads out through the Sunbank gate to Hopstead, 290 m from the start. From Hopstead the hop-yards and herb banks are 150–210 m, Ridgefold 180 m, the High Fold 285 m, the ridge top 445 m, Brewmaster Maltby 300 m and the Adit's bears about 340 m; a foot path along the mountain's foot joins the brewery to the Adit, so ST-13 and ST-14 are one round. ST-16 goes back into the bowl, 440 m from Hopstead to the first sink's mouth. The passes are 510–525 m from Hopstead. Walked with its returns, the flow line is to be about 4 km, 8 to 12 minutes of running, where it was 8.9 km and 23 minutes ([World Layout](../13-world/03-stonewake-stoneheart.md#walking-distances)).

## Build notes

Reuses Brightmead's systems one-for-one with the hill-dwarf art kit (reused at Ramshead Lodge, Emberwell and future foothill steadings): turf-roofed longhouse, dry-stone wall, terrace, hop-pole rows, brewhouse, sheep-fold, and the dam as a set piece. The zone keeps its square of 1,400 m a side, where the other starting zone is 1,000 m, and its place and exits on the continent; that is on purpose, and it does not make the zone large. Inside the square the playable dale is compact and enclosed (the Size row above): the layout places everything in about 55 ha, and the rest of the square is sculpted mountain with a gorge to each exit. Skeletons: goat (shared with ram), bird, bear, beetle, ooze, hound, gravelkin, dwarf.

The overhaul of 2026-11-26. The owner played the zone and found it sparse, and asked for it to be made polished. What changed is where things are, how big the space is and what fills it; the 18 quests, their givers and order, the NPCs, the creature list and every camp's count are as they were. The dam is brought into view as the valley's landmark, 554 m from Hopstead's green and drawn on the skyline from the bowl; it stays friendly, and nothing is fought on it. Every quest area is reached by a road or track that runs toward that area's own landmark (the quarry's derrick, the rag poles at the sinks, the hop-kiln's cowl, the brewery's chimney, the beacon cairn, the dam), with signposts at the forks, milestones and lamp posts. Creatures and NPCs stand in places that explain them: buzzards on the terrace walls, Gravelkin among spoil heaps at the sink rims, rustlers in tent camps with a pen of stolen sheep, bears at a den by the spoil banks, the trainers round a practice yard. The four bothies and the High Fold, which the layout named and the first build left out, are built. Bouldergate Pass and the Dalegate road leave through gorges, the first under a hill-dwarf arch; the Adit keeps its timber door-frame. The zone is built to the dressing, tree, terrain, ground, wall and light rules of [World Construction](../13-world/10-world-construction.md) and finished by a hand pass in the zone editor on the starter bowl and Hopstead.

The south road leads straight from this starting zone into Ironroot Highlands, a level 10–20 zone, and it is left open: no warning sign and no guards. It leaves through a gorge with no gate, no signpost arm and no milestone. A curious or adventurous player may wander into the danger and die there, and finds out the hard way. The quests are what lead a player to the right next zone: ST-18 "Word to the Mountain" sends them through Bouldergate Pass to [Embercrag Hollow](t2-embercrag-hollow.md) or through the Daleside Adit to [Stoneheart Deeps](t2-stoneheart-deeps.md), the level 5–10 zones, and no quest of this zone sends a player down the south road. Decided by the owner, 2026-11-25.
