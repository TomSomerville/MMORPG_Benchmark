Grolm Sootbeard is not a rare: he is the named target of EC-07, always in his chamber, and is listed with the rares so that his level and abilities are in the creature data.

Decided 2026-11-25, and checked again for the zone at levels 5–10: the Slagborn Firebrand keeps its Slag Bolt by the caster rule, with nothing taken off. At level 8 that is 33–45 Fire every 2 s, about 19 damage a second, where a creature of its level that fights by hand does about 9: the same proportion as when the zone was levels 1–5 (20–28 Fire, about 12 a second against 5). It is Embercrag Hollow's hard enemy by design, and it is met one at a time: the Firebrands outside Kiln Cave stand 30 m apart. Every other caster of the level 5–10 zones is built by the same rule, so the Firebrand fits its new band; what the band adds is that a player now meets it at level 7 or 8 with a full set of abilities and not at level 4.

**Totals:** 20 quests · ≈ 12,170 quest XP.

**Story note.** Two fires. The first is the forge: the Slagborn exile Grolm Sootbeard carried the Ever-Ember off to Kiln Cave, and the player brings it home and relights the Old Forge (EC-07; a visible, lasting change in the world for that player). The second is why the forge had to be dark. With no glow on the snow, nobody saw the sledges: Sapper-Chief Dolga Blackmatch has been packing cinder-charges at Slagfall Gully to run down Bouldergate by night and set on the **Stonewake Dam**. If the dam goes, the Dale goes with it, and Hammerdeep's ground-level halls after it. This is where the heavy dam content lives (decided 2026-11-25, when Stonewake Dale became the starting zone): the sappers, the powderkegs, Blackmatch Camp, Powderkeg Pol and Dolga herself are here, and the charges are stopped at their source. Nothing is fought on the dam. EC-14 is the moment the two cultures have to trust each other: the Warden sends her runner to the Dale-Reeve and the Dammaster both, which no mountain warden has done before.

Journeyman trainers in Anvilrest. Cooking is introduced by quest EC-04; First Aid is taught at the inn's side-room, with no quest.

## Build notes

The zone was built as the dwarf starting zone and keeps what was built: its ground, its two hubs, the Warrens, the Old Forge and Kiln Cave, at their places and its size of 1,000 m a side. Becoming a level 5–10 zone changes its creatures' levels, its quests, its trainers' ranks, and adds two places to the north wall (Slagfall Gully, Blackmatch Camp) and the siding at Tuskfall foot. Mountain-dwarf art kit (stone lodge, forge, cave), snow terrain shader, forge-works pieces at Blackmatch Camp. Skeletons: wolf, ram, boar, lynx, gravelkin, sprite, dwarf.

Open: Embercrag is 1,000 m a side, where the other three level 5–10 zones are 1,400 m (or the Deeps' three levels); whether it is enlarged when it is rebuilt for its new levels is not decided, and the layout keeps the built size until then.
