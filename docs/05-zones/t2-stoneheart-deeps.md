| Minor hubs | Cartwright's Junction (rail yard); Deep Gate watch-post |
| Exits | Up the Long Stair → Hammerdeep (Deepgate); Daleside Adit (a mine mouth) → Stonewake Dale; the Deep Gate rail-tunnel south → Ironroot Highlands (surfaces near Ironroot Hold) |

| Travel inside | Mine-cart rides between Lanternhold, Cartwright's Junction and the Deep Gate (the zone's flight path equivalent) |

## Gathering & professions

Copper (Upper Galleries), **Tin and Silver** (Crystal Galleries — the best low-level mining zone), gems from Geode Sprites · Herbs are scarce by design: Dewleaf and Glowcap in the Grotto only (Glowcap is a cooking ingredient, not an Alchemy herb) · Light Leather from bears and borers · Linen, first Wool · No fishing. Herbalists are better served in Embercrag Hollow; miners here — the two level 5–10 zones of Khazrun complement each other.

## Build notes

The project's first fully interior zone, and the template for future mountain interiors (Blackanvil Depths, 30–40).

- **No terrain heightmap:** built from modular cavern pieces (floor, wall, ceiling, pillar, bridge) plus hand-placed set pieces; needs a ceiling-aware camera and a layered minimap (one map per cavern level, switched by elevation).
- **Lighting is gameplay:** lit lantern-roads are safe routes; dark side-tunnels hide ambushers.
- **Navmesh:** multi-level, with rail trestles and lifts as off-mesh links.
- **Scale:** the Great Cavern must feel like an outdoor zone — ~120 m ceiling, 800 m across, visible from Lanternhold's balcony.
- New skeletons: bat, rat, cave bear (re-skin), skitterer (spider re-skin), rail-golem, borer, geode sprite.
