| | |
|---|---|
| Theme | Meridia's dry eastern cape: red mesas, wind-cut canyons and one enormous chasm — **the Rift** — where the earth opened in the Sundering and swallowed the imperial gate-city of **Tor Vael** whole. Spire-tops still poke from the canyon floor. The broken stump of the Imperial Causeway points south toward Sundrel |
| Hubs | **Dawn's Stand** (Concord fort, 20–23) · **Kite's Rest** (neutral mesa-town, 23–27; bank, neutral auction house, the Dustpit) · **Spire Camp** (Athenaeum dig, 27–30) |
| Raid | [The Hollow Throne](../07-instances/raid-the-hollow-throne.md) (10 players, level 30) |
| World boss | [Gorrath, the Rift Maw](../07-instances/world-bosses.md#3-gorrath-the-rift-maw) |
| PvP | [The Dustpit](../02-systems/pvp.md#3-the-dustpit--free-for-all-arena) FFA arena under Kite's Rest |
| Access | East road from Thornwick Marches; south track from Veilmoor; eyries |
| Water | None. A dry land zone: canyons, rope-bridges, dig-lifts and switchback trails |
| Future | The designed meeting point of the three allegiances and the land gate to Sundrel — Wildpact and Covenant envoys already keep tents in Kite's Rest |

## Gathering & professions

Iron, Gold (rare), Starmetal (Tor Vael Ruins) · Wyrmtongue (mesa cliffs), Riftbloom (canyon walls), Ghostlily (Tor Vael) · Heavy Leather, Rift Wyrm Hide · Silk · No fishing in this zone; the *Digger's Haversack* (12-slot bag) and Star Sapphires come from Blackspade lockboxes and the Smuggler's Cache instead.

## Flow

Dawn's Stand (01–09) → Kite's Rest (10, 17–24; main chain 11–16) → Spire Camp (25–34) → raid attunement and raid (35, 36).
