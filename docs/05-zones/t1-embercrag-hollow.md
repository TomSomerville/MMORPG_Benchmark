## Gathering & professions

Copper Veins (Tuskfall, Warrens) · Sunpetal and Hearthroot on sheltered south slopes, Dewleaf under frostpines · Light skinning · Linen from Gravelkin and Slagborn · Ice-fishing at Rimewater (skill 25).

## Flow

Coldhearth (01–06) → Old Forge (07) → Anvilrest (08) → Tuskfall (09, 10, 14) → Kiln Cave approach (11, 12) → Rimewater (13) → Kiln Cave (15, 16) → Warrens deep (17) → Bouldergate (18).

## Build notes

Reuses Brightmead's systems one-for-one with a dwarven art kit (stone lodge, forge, cave), snow terrain shader, and 5 new skeletons (ram, lynx, gravelkin, sprite, dwarf).
