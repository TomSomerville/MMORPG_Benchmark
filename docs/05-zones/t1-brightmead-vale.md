**Totals:** 18 quests · ≈ 4,060 quest XP · about 200–280 kills in all, about 75 of them asked for by quests (≈ 4,600 XP from the quests' kills, ≈ 9,000 kill XP in all) → player leaves at about level 7. The kills beyond the quests' 75 are the creatures that stand between a player and the objectives, and the repeat kills for items that drop by chance; the zone is meant to play this way and its creatures are not thinned (decided 2026-11-25). Measured by the zone bot run: about 281 kills, 13,175 XP in all, 4,060 of it from quests, level 7.

## Gathering & professions

Copper Veins (ridge, mine mouth) · Sunpetal, Hearthroot, Dewleaf · Light skinning from wolves/boars · Linen from Burrowkin and Ashhand · Fishing: Silverrun Creek (skill 25). Apprentice trainers for all professions in Millbrook.

## Flow

Larkhill (01–06) → Hartwell Mine (07) → Millbrook (08) → Greenhollow (09, 10, 17) → Burned Mill (11–13) → Silverrun & Ashhand Camp (14, 15) → Hartwell Deep (16) → north road (18).

## Build notes

First zone built; it is the vertical-slice test bed (see [Roadmap](../08-tech/roadmap.md)). Needs: 1 human village kit, mine interior kit, mill (burned variant), 12 creature models (6 unique skeletons: wolf, boar, spider, burrowkin, human, gillkin, turtle).
