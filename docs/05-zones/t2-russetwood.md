| | |
|---|---|
| Theme | A forest in permanent autumn: red, gold and copper canopy, drifting leaves, low sun through mist. Home of woodcutters, coopers and charcoal-burners — and lately of kilns that burn something other than wood |
| Why it exists | A **complete, self-sufficient 5→10** alternative to Harrowfield, directly west of it. A player can do either zone, or mix them |
| Hub | **Cooper's Hollow** (inn *The Split Stave*, eyrie, mailbox, smith, Journeyman profession trainers) |
| Minor hubs | Charcoal-Burners' Camp; Birch's Lodge (foresters) |
| Exits | East → Harrowfield; south → Solhaven / Brightmead; north (the Old Timber Road) → Thornwick Marches |
| Music / mood | Solo cello and woodwind; wind in leaves; mist at dawn, leaf-fall always |

## Gathering & professions

Copper (rocky outcrops), Tin (the kiln quarry) · Dewleaf (everywhere), Bramblethorn (clearings), Marshbloom (stream banks) — the best low-level herb zone · Light Leather/Hides (the best low-level skinning zone) · Linen; first Wool from Kiln Tenders and Guards · Fishing: forest streams (75). Journeyman trainers in Cooper's Hollow.

## Flow

Cooper's Hollow (01–03) → Birch's Lodge (04–07, 19) → High Canopy (08) → Charcoal Clearings (09, 13) → Rootgnaw Burrows (10, 11) → Mossback Stand (12, 18) → Burning Ride (14, 15) → Great Kiln (16, 17) → Old Timber Road (20).

## Build notes

Shares Harrowfield's human village kit and autumn palette. New: a dense-forest tree set with a red/gold leaf shader and falling-leaf particles, rope-walkway kit, brick kiln kit; new skeletons: fox, stag, beetle, owl, treant.
