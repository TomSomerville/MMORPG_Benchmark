| | |
|---|---|
| Theme | A broad green valley between two mountain spurs, farmed by the **hill dwarves** (the Dalefolk): barley terraces, hop-yards, turf-roofed longhouses, dry-stone walls and herds of goats and rams. High above its head stands the mountain dwarves' great **Stonewake Dam**. If the dam goes, the whole dale goes with it |
| Culture | The zone that introduces the hill dwarves, and the friction between them and the Hallborn engineers who built a dam over their heads |
| Why it exists | A **complete, self-sufficient 5→10**; the outdoor counterpart to Stoneheart Deeps next door |
| Hub | **Hopstead** (inn *The Dry Boot*, eyrie, mailbox, smith, Journeyman profession trainers) |
| Minor hubs | Dam-Watch Post (mountain-dwarf engineers); Ridgefold (shepherds' steading) |
| Exits | Dalegate → Hammerdeep; Daleside Adit → Stoneheart Deeps; Bouldergate Pass → Embercrag; south road → Ironroot Highlands |

The reservoir behind the dam is scenery only; nothing in the zone is fought or quested in water.

**Story note.** The Dalefolk never wanted the dam; the Hallborn built it to feed Hammerdeep's forges and the Rootvault. ST-14 is the moment the two cultures have to trust each other: the Dammaster needs the Dale-Reeve's rangers, and the Reeve needs her engineers.

## Gathering & professions

Copper (terrace walls), Tin (Sinks, dam abutments) · **Dewleaf, Bramblethorn, Marshbloom** (field margins, hop-yards, millrace banks — the Khazrun herb zone) · Light Leather (goats, bears) · Linen, first Wool · Fishing: the millrace and the pool at the dam's foot (75; Longjaw Pike → Pike Oil, River Pearls from mussels). Cooking is introduced by quest ST-04; First Aid is taught at Ridgefold, with no quest.

## Flow

Hopstead (01, 02, 04) → Ridgefold (03, 08, 10, 11, 18) → Sinks (05–07) → Hop-Yards & Old Brewery (12, 15) → Millrace (09) → Dam & Slagfall (13, 14) → Blackmatch Camp (16, 17, 19) → south road (20).

## Build notes

Hill-dwarf art kit (reused at Ramshead Lodge, Emberwell and future foothill steadings): turf-roofed longhouse, dry-stone wall, terrace, hop-pole rows, brewhouse, sheep-fold. New skeletons: goat (shared with ram), beetle (shared with Russetwood), ooze, hound.
