## Gathering & professions

Copper (fields, ditch banks), Tin (Dustwater Quarry) · Dewleaf, Bramblethorn, Marshbloom (Longstrand) · Light Leather/Hides · Linen; first Wool from Firestarters and Houseguards · Fishing: Longstrand (75). Journeyman trainers in Harrow's Cross. Cooking is introduced by quest HF-04; First Aid is taught by the militia medic, with no quest.

## Flow

Harrow's Cross (01) → Gleaning Fields & Fenner's (02–04) → Reaver's Row (05–07, 19) → Scarecrow Hill (08, 09, 18) → Longstrand (10, 11) → Quarry (12, 15) → Burning Rows (13, 14) → Vane Manor (16, 17) → Tollbridge (20).
