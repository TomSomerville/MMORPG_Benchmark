# Lore of Halcyra

[← Master Plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md) · Next: [Factions & Races](factions-and-races.md)

## 1. The world in one paragraph

**Halcyra** is a world of three continents divided by the **Sundering Sea**. Three centuries ago the Aurelian Empire tried to chain the source of all arcane power — the **Wellspring** — to a single crown. The Wellspring ruptured, the imperial heartland shattered, and the survivors split into three peoples who each believe they are the rightful heirs of the world: the **Concord of Dawn** (order and faith), the **Wildpact** (the living wild), and the **Ashen Covenant** (the empire that refused to die). The player begins as a citizen of the Concord in year **312 AS** (After Sundering), when pieces of the Emperor's **Hollow Crown** begin resurfacing.

## 2. Cosmology

| Force | What it is | Who wields it | Spell school |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Dawnflame** | The first light; warmth, oath, renewal. Worshipped as the Twin Flames: *Dawn* (the sun, humans) and *Forge* (the hearth-fire, dwarves) | Clerics | Holy |
| **The Verdant Choir** | The countless spirits of living things; older than any god | Druids, the Wildpact | Nature |
| **The Lattice** | The web of star-light and ley-lines through which aether flows from the Wellspring | Druids (Starweave), mages (future) | Arcane |
| **The Hollow** | What is left when aether is forced through a living soul: an emptiness that hungers | Ashen Covenant, the Cinderbrand | Shadow |
| **The Deep Fires / The Long Cold** | Elemental forces of the world's body | Slagborn, elementals | Fire / Frost |

Design rule: every spell school maps to a cosmological force, so future classes slot into existing lore (Warlock → the Hollow, Archer → mundane/Physical with Choir or Lattice flavours, Rogue → Physical/Shadow).

## 3. Timeline

| Era | Events |
|---|---|
| **Age of Roots** (prehistory) | The Verdant Choir shapes the wilds. Elves and ogres live under the Choir's law. Dwarves wake beneath Khazrun and find the Forge-flame. |
| **Age of Thrones** (c. 1,400 years) | Human mage-kings of **Sundrel** found the **Aurelian Empire** and build the **Aether Throne** atop the Wellspring. All races are imperial subjects. The gate-city **Tor Vael**, on Meridia's eastern cape, guards the Imperial Causeway that links Sundrel to the northern provinces. |
| **The Sundering** (year 0) | Emperor **Vaelric the Last** binds the Wellspring to the **Hollow Crown** seeking deathlessness. The Wellspring ruptures. Sundrel shatters; the Causeway breaks; the earth opens beneath Tor Vael and swallows the city whole, leaving the canyon country now called the **Riftlands**. The Crown breaks into five shards. |
| **The Scattering** (0–40 AS) | Refugees flee west to **Meridia** and north-east to **Khazrun**. Those who would not leave Sundrel are sustained past death by leaking aether — the first **Unburied**. |
| **The Hearth-Oath** (41 AS) | King Aldric I of Solhaven and High Thane Brunna Deepanvil swear the Hearth-Oath, founding the **Concord of Dawn**. |
| **The Thorn Wars** (118–131 AS) | The Wildpact halts Concord logging and farming of the western deepwoods. The border freezes at Thornwick. |
| **The Ash Crusade** (203–209 AS) | The Concord invades Sundrel to "lay the dead to rest", fails, and withdraws. The Ashen Covenant formally declares itself the continuing Empire. |
| **The Long Truce** (210–311 AS) | Cold war. Trade resumes through the neutral caravan-town of Kite's Rest in the Riftlands. |
| **Now** (312 AS) | The **Cinderbrand** cult appears in Concord lands. Crown shards surface. Something beneath the Riftlands is waking. |

## 4. The three factions — the power triangle

Only the Concord is playable at launch. The other two exist in the world as NPC forces, envoys and enemies so they can be switched on later without retconning anything.

| | **Concord of Dawn** | **Wildpact** | **Ashen Covenant** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ideal | Order, oath, civilisation | Freedom, balance, the living world | Continuity, knowledge, defiance of death |
| Colour (also BG team) | **Blue** | **Yellow / green-gold** | **Red** |
| Races (launch → later) | Human, Dwarf → Gnome | *(later)* Elf, Ogre → Beastkin | *(later)* Undead (Unburied) → Hollow-touched exiles |
| Power source | Dawnflame, steel, industry | Verdant Choir, beasts, terrain | Aether, the Hollow, undeath |
| Homeland | Meridia & Khazrun | Western deepwoods of Meridia, eastern Sundrel jungle | Shattered Sundrel |
| Capital(s) | Solhaven, Hammerdeep | Eldergrove of Sael'anor | Vael Morrow, the Unsunk City |
| Controls resource | **Steel** (ore, forges) | **Lifebloom** (living essence) | **Aether** (raw arcane) |
| Needs resource | Aether (wards, lamps, eagle-roost beacons) | Steel (cold iron binds spirits and blight) | Lifebloom (staves off decay of the Unburied) |
| Strong against | Covenant — Dawnflame unmakes undeath | Concord — the wild swallows walls, roads and farms | Wildpact — blight withers what lives |
| Weak against | Wildpact | Covenant | Concord |

**How to read the triangle:** each faction *preys on* the one that holds the resource it needs, and is *preyed on* by the one that needs its own. Nobody can win outright: crushing your prey frees your predator.

```
        CONCORD (Steel)
        /            \
  beats, wants      loses to
  Aether            (wants Steel)
      /                \
 COVENANT (Aether) ──beats, wants Lifebloom──> WILDPACT (Lifebloom) ──beats──> CONCORD
```

Each side's self-image (all three are sympathetic, none is "the evil faction"):
- **Concord:** "We rebuilt the world with our hands. Without law, there is only the next Sundering."
- **Wildpact:** "The Empire's sin was thinking the world belonged to it. The Concord is that sin with a prayer on top."
- **Covenant:** "We never fell. The Empire stands wherever its citizens still stand — living or not."

The triangle is expressed in PvP through map objectives and resources rather than flat damage bonuses; see [PvP](../02-systems/pvp.md).

## 5. The Concord of Dawn (player faction)

- **Humans of Meridia.** Kingdom of Solhaven, ruled by Queen **Isolde Marrowind II**. Farmers, soldiers, priests. Human druids are **Hedgewardens**, keepers of the pre-imperial hedge-circles — tolerated by the Church of the Dawn, resented by the Wildpact as "thieves of the old ways".
- **Dwarves of Khazrun** are one people with two ways of life. The **mountain dwarves (the Hallborn)** live in the snow peaks, inside the mountains and at their very feet: miners, smiths and engineers of the Deep Thanedom of Hammerdeep under High Thane **Durek Deepanvil**. The **hill dwarves (the Dalefolk)** live in the valleys between the mountains and on the farmed foothills: farmers, herders and brewers who govern themselves through the **Dale-Moot** of elected reeves and owe the Thane grain and levies, not obedience. Each thinks the other has forgotten what a dwarf is. Dwarven druids are **Stonesingers**, who hear the Choir in root and rock — a calling far commoner among the Dalefolk. Detail: [Factions & Races](factions-and-races.md#52-mountain-dwarves-and-hill-dwarves).
- **The Church of the Twin Flames** trains Clerics: armoured priests who fight with hammer and Dawnflame.
- **The Hearth-Oath** means no race-locked content: a human may be sworn in at Coldhearth and a dwarf at Larkhill.

## 7. Story of 1–30 in brief

1. **Embers of the Brand (1–20).** Petty troubles in both starting valleys — stolen grain, a forge gone cold — trace back to branded cultists. The trail leads through burned farms and a sabotaged dam to a necromantic workshop in the Gallowmere Crypts and finally to Magister Ashgrave's tower. When he dies he is found to be *hollow*: a crown shard sits where his heart was. Full detail: [Main Chain 1](../06-quests/main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md).
2. **The Hollow Crown (15–30).** Scholars of both capitals investigate the "hollowing". Five shards are in play. One is being smelted in the Slagworks, one is being dug out of Tor Vael by salvagers, and the rest are held by Lady Seraphine Vael. The Concord fails to stop the Crown being dragged down into the earth, and ten heroes follow it into the Rift, to the Hollow Throne. Full detail: [Main Chain 2](../06-quests/main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md).

## 8. Naming conventions

- Human places: English compound names (Brightmead, Harrow's Cross). Human names: Anglo/Breton flavour (Elyse Farrow, Alric Dunmoor).
- Dwarven places: stone/forge compounds (Hammerdeep, Anvilrest). Names: Norse flavour; mountain dwarves take craft surnames (Hilda Stonebraid, Durgan Emberhand), hill dwarves take land and larder surnames (Bruna Ramsbell, Eski Maltby).
- Imperial/Covenant: Latinate with *Vael-* / *-ric* / *-ine* (Vaelric, Seraphine, Tor Vael).
- Wildpact: soft elven (Sael'anor, Ysolde Thornwake) and blunt ogrish (Urgoth Stonemaw).
- All names in these documents are original to this project.
