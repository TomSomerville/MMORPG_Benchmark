## 1. Continents

| Continent | Code | Character | Leveling zones planned (1–60) | Built at launch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meridia** | A | Temperate west: farmland, autumn forest, marsh, moor, rift-canyons. Human homeland | 9 | Solhaven + 6 zones (1–30) |
| **Khazrun** | B | Mountains: snow peaks, mountain interiors, farmed dales, highlands, volcanic south. Dwarf homeland | 8 | Hammerdeep + 5 zones (1–30) |
| **Sundrel** | C | Shattered southern continent; Covenant and Wildpact lands | 5 (30–60) | Nothing — see §8 |

**The half rule.** The planned leveling zones fill only **half of each continent's landmass**. The other half is deliberately left as named but empty map space for future zones, additional capital cities, and starting zones for new races and allegiances. Continents are therefore sized for **18 (Meridia), 16 (Khazrun) and 10 (Sundrel)** zone-sized regions.

There is **no water zone**: the sea is scenery and a ship route, never a questing area.

## 4. The two dwarven worlds on Khazrun

Khazrun's zones are built to show two ways of being a dwarf (culture detail in [Factions & Races](factions-and-races.md#52-mountain-dwarves-and-hill-dwarves)).

| | **Mountain dwarves (the Hallborn)** | **Hill dwarves (the Dalefolk)** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Snow peaks, the mountain interiors, and the ground right at the mountain's foot | Valleys between the mountains and the farmed foothills |
| Zones | Embercrag Hollow, Hammerdeep, Stoneheart Deeps, Ironroot Hold and Deepseam, Fort Ashwatch | Stonewake Dale, Ramshead Lodge and the Ironroot foothill steadings, Emberwell |
| Look | Carved stone halls, forges, rails, lantern-light, snow | Turf-roofed longhouses, dry-stone walls, barley terraces, hop-yards, goat and ram herds |
| Work | Mining, smithing, engineering, rune-craft | Farming, herding, brewing, weaving, hunting |
| Sound | Anvils, low brass, echo | Fiddles, pipes, wind, livestock |

## 5. Capitals

Both capitals are **Sanctuary** zones with identical services so neither race is disadvantaged.

**Services in both:** all class trainers; all profession trainers (to Expert 225); bank and **guild bank**; auction house (linked between the two); mailboxes; inn; eyrie (flight master); stable/mount vendor and feed merchant; guild master and tabard vendor; battlemaster (battleground queue); barber; weapon master; reagent, general, trade-supply vendors; repair; a duelling ground just outside the gate; **the harbour for the capital-to-capital ship**.

## 6. Travel

| Method | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Walking / running** | Run speed 7 yd/s. No level or race restriction on any outdoor zone |
| **The capital ship** | Free. *The Hearth-Oath* sails **Solhaven Harbor ↔ the Seagate of Hammerdeep** every 5 minutes: out of the estuary, along the mountain coast of Khazrun, and through the sea-arch into the mountain. Usable from level 1 — this is how a level 1 human reaches Embercrag. The only ship route in the game |
| **Eyries (flight paths)** | Crag-eagle taxis between discovered eyries on the same continent. Cost scales with distance (≈ 50c–8s). One eyrie in every hub |
| **Hearthstone** | Starting item. Returns to bound inn. 60 min cooldown |
| **Mounts** | Riding at level 30: +60% ground speed. Epic riding at level 60: +100% **while the mount is fed** (see [Economy](../02-systems/economy-and-social.md#4-the-level-60-gold-sink-plan)). Horses (Solhaven), rams (Hammerdeep) |
| **Graveyards** | One per hub plus one per ~600 m of wilderness. Ghost run at +25% speed |
| **Swimming** | Swim speed 4.7 yd/s; 60 s breath; fatigue at continent edges. Rivers and ponds exist; no content requires swimming |
| **Future** | Mage portals; the rebuilt Imperial Causeway from the Riftlands to Sundrel; flying is not planned |

## 7. World rules

- **Seamless zones** within a continent; no loading between adjacent zones. Stoneheart Deeps is entered through cave mouths and the Deepgate without a loading screen.
- **Day/night** cycle: 1 game day = 4 real hours. Some creatures and herbs are night-only. The Deeps have no day/night; lighting comes from lanterns, glowcaps and forge-glow.
- **Weather** per zone: rain (Thornwick, Harrowfield), leaf-fall and mist (Russetwood), snow (Embercrag, Ironroot peaks), fog (Veilmoor), ashfall (Cinderfall), dust-storms (Riftlands). Cosmetic at launch.
- **Exploration** reveals the zone map sub-area by sub-area and grants XP (see [Progression](../02-systems/progression.md)).
- **Zone PvP tag:** every launch zone is `Territory(Concord)` except Kite's Rest (`Sanctuary`) and the Dustpit (`Free-for-all`).
