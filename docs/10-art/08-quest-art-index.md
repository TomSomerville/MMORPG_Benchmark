# Art To-Do 8: Quest Art Index

← [Art Master To-Do](00-art-master-todo.md) | Prev: [Named Item Index](07-named-item-index.md)

Every zone quest from level 1 to 30 with the art its objective implies, extracted automatically from the zone documents. **Item icon** = the quest hands the player, or asks them to collect or carry, a named item that needs an inventory icon. **World object / scene** = something in the world the player clicks, defends, follows or visits that must be modelled or staged. Kill and talk objectives need only the creatures and NPCs already listed in [Creatures and NPCs](02-creatures-npcs.md). Regenerate after any quest change.

**Totals:** 296 quests | 135 need at least one quest-item icon | 63 need a world object or staged scene.
