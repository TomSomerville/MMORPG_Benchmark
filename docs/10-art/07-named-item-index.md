← [Art Master To-Do](00-art-master-todo.md) · Prev: [Icons](06-icons.md)

Every named piece of equipment in the level 1-30 design documents, extracted automatically from the zone, instance, PvP, crafting and quest text docs, with the art each one needs. Sources named "quest-text-..." are quest rewards first named in the [Quest Text](../06-quests/text/00-quest-text-index.md) files. "Tier" means the generic model or texture for that slot and level band defined in [Characters, Armor & Weapons](01-characters-armor-weapons.md); "Unique" means its own asset. Regenerate this file after any design change. Unnamed "green boots"-style rewards and random-suffix drops use tier art and need no entry.

**Totals:** 765 named items · 224 need unique art · 541 use tier art with a recolor · every one needs its own icon.
