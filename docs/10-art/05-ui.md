# Art To-Do 5 — User Interface

[← Art Master To-Do](00-art-master-todo.md) · Prev: [Spell & World Effects](04-vfx.md) · Next: [Icons](06-icons.md)

Every UI texture, frame, widget, screen, map and font. Functional design is in [UI & Controls](../02-systems/ui-and-controls.md); this is the art to produce. Style: the stone-and-brass Concord skin described below, painterly, high contrast, readable at 1080p and scalable to 4K (author at 2×). ☐ = not started.

## The look

The interface is **dark carved stone trimmed with polished brass**. The owner chose this direction from concept screens: the stone-and-brass screen for everything except the unit frames, which are the open round frames described below.

Open: the owner has chosen this direction; the interface is drawn in code today and the UI texture set (P03) has not been approved for production.

The tables in §1 to §6 list the parts each element is made of and keep their ids. Where a row's wording differs from this section (a name plate behind a unit frame, a border drawn round the whole target frame, figured end-caps on the action bar, 20 segments on the XP bar), this section is the look.

| Element | How it looks |
|---|---|
| Stone | Dark blue-grey stone, a little lighter at the top of a panel and darker at its foot, with a soft shadow beneath. Recesses in it (lists, text wells, bar tracks, icon slots) are near-black |
| Brass | Warm polished brass with a light glint on the upper-left and a dark underside. Every brass line is drawn between two thin dark lines so that it reads against stone and against the world |
| Panels and windows | A stone panel edged with a brass line, with a **brass corner plate carrying one stud** at each corner. Small panels that a corner plate would crowd carry the plain brass line alone |
| Buttons | Dark stone with a brass edge; lighter stone and bright brass under the pointer; sunk and near-black when pressed; dimmed when disabled. The small buttons on the minimap's ring are round |
| Text | Warm parchment-cream with a dark outline; titles in gold; secondary text in a dimmer grey-cream |
| Tooltips | Near-black, with a thin brass line |
| Icon slots | A dark square with a thin brass edge drawn over the icon. The edge takes the quality colour for an item. Icons carry no frame of their own ([Icons](06-icons.md#the-icon-style)) |
| Bars | A dark recessed track with a flat-coloured fill and a glint along the top of the fill: health green, cast gold, experience violet, a resource in its own colour |
| Unit frames | **Open: no panel behind them.** A round portrait in a brass ring, a small round level badge in its own brass ring at the portrait's lower right, and beside the portrait the unit's name over two slim bars (health, and a slimmer resource bar) with their numbers. The name is coloured by reaction. Effects on the unit are listed beneath |
| Standing | Shown by the portrait's ring and a word beneath the bars. An ordinary unit has a brass ring. A **rare** has a silver ring; an **elite**, a **rare elite** and a **boss** have a gold ring. A ring of standing is slightly wider and carries four studs, at the top, bottom, left and right; the level badge's ring takes the same colour, and the word (Rare, Elite, Rare Elite, Boss) is written in it |
| Action bar | One stone panel of twelve slots with a **pointed stone cap at each end**, edged in brass and set with a brass lozenge. The key for a slot is written in the slot's upper corner, over the icon |
| XP bar | Sits directly on the top edge of the action bar, the same width, touching it with no gap. Its fill is divided into **10 equal sections** ("bubbles") by thin dividers drawn over the fill. Its text is centred vertically and horizontally inside the fill and never overlaps the frame |
| Bottom of the screen | The action bars, the bag bar, the micro-menu and the chat frame sit with their bottom edges on the bottom edge of the screen, with no gap |
| Minimap | **Round, in a polished brass ring** with a brass pointer at each quarter, the north one larger. The zone's name is written above it and its round buttons sit on the ring |
