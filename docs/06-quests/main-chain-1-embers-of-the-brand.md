# Main Chain 1 — Embers of the Brand (levels 1–20)

[← Master Plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md) · [Leveling Path](../05-zones/00-leveling-path.md) · Next: [Main Chain 2](main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md)

**Premise.** Small troubles in two quiet valleys turn out to be the work of the **Cinderbrand**: a cult that burns an ash sigil into its recruits and promises them "a crown for every brow". The player follows the brand from stolen grain to a traitor marshal, into a necromancer's crypt and up a wizard's tower — and finds that the cult's master was only ever a puppet.

**Structure.** Acts I, III and IV exist in two parallel versions, one per continent, and Act II in **four** — one for each of the 5–10 zones, so every one of those zones carries the main story on its own. Together they tell the same conspiracy from two sides (humans see arson and grave-robbing; dwarves see sabotage and arms-smuggling). A player needs **either** version of each act — they are linked as *exclusive groups* in the quest data — and may freely switch continents between acts. Both versions converge for the dungeon and the finale.

## Act I — Sparks (levels 1–5)

### Meridia version: "The Burned Mill" — [Brightmead Vale](../05-zones/t1-brightmead-vale.md)

| Step | Quest | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | BM-07 The Stolen Seed-Grain | Burrowkin have taken the spring seed. In the mine the grain sacks are stacked neatly beside crates stamped with an ash sigil — someone *paid* the Burrowkin |
| 2 | BM-11 Smoke on the Horizon | The mill burns. Miller Cray saw men with ash-smeared hands |
| 3 | BM-12 The Ashen Hand | Drive the "Ashhands" from the mill and orchard |
| 4 | BM-15 Branded Coin | Their purses hold coins struck with the same sigil |
| 5 | BM-16 The Brander | In Hartwell Deep, recruiter Hale Morrow is branding new initiates. His last words: *"It burns clean. You'll beg for it."* |
| 6 | BM-18 Word to Harrowfield | Carry his branding iron north |

### Khazrun version: "The Cold Forge" — [Embercrag Hollow](../05-zones/t1-embercrag-hollow.md)

| Step | Quest | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | EC-07 The Cold Forge | The Old Forge has burned for a thousand years. Its Ever-Ember is gone |
| 2 | EC-11 Tracks in the Snow | Sooty footprints lead to Kiln Cave |
| 3 | EC-12 Soot and Shadow | Slagborn exiles — kin who were cast out for hollow-fire smithing |
| 4 | EC-15 Branded Ingots | They carry ingots stamped with an ash sigil |
| 5 | EC-16 Sootbeard's Reckoning | Grolm Sootbeard was using the Ever-Ember to "wake" the brand in cold iron. Recover it and relight the forge (a visible, permanent world-state change for the player) |
| 6 | EC-18 Word to Stonewake | Carry an ingot down to the Dale-Reeve in Hopstead, or into the mountain to the Hallwarden at Lanternhold |

## Act II — Kindling (levels 5–10)

### Meridia: "Burning Season" — [Harrowfield](../05-zones/t2-harrowfield.md)

HF-01 → HF-05 Reaver's Row → HF-07 Paid in Ash → HF-09 Cinders in the Stuffing → HF-14 Burning Season → HF-16 The Marshal's Seal → HF-17 Traitor's Due → HF-20 Letters Signed "C.A."

The Harrow Reavers are being paid in branded coin to ruin the harvest; living cinders have been sewn into scarecrows to terrorise farmers off their land. The payroll leads to **Marshal Edrik Vane**, the man charged with defending Harrowfield, who was promised a lordship "when the crown is whole". In his study are letters signed only **"C.A."**.

### Meridia: "Fire in the Russetwood" — [Russetwood](../05-zones/t2-russetwood.md)

RW-01 → RW-05 Poachers' Trails → RW-07 Paid in Charcoal → RW-09 What the Kilns Burn → RW-14 Fire in the Russetwood → RW-16 The Kilnmaster's Ledger → RW-17 Ashes to Ashes → RW-20 Ledger Signed "C.A."

Poachers are being paid in sacks of oddly heavy charcoal to keep the foresters busy while Ashhand charcoalers fire something else in the clearings: the **cinders** themselves, the living coals that will later be sewn into scarecrows and corpses. When the foresters get close, the cult sets the wood alight to cover its tracks. **Kilnmaster Bertram Soot** runs the Great Kiln; his ledger lists every delivery, each countersigned **"C.A."**. (This version shows where the cult's cinders are *made*; Harrowfield shows where they are *used*.)

### Khazrun: "Charges on the Dam" — [Stonewake Dale](../05-zones/t2-stonewake-dale.md)

ST-01 → ST-05 The Sinks → ST-07 Paid in Slag → ST-09 Powder in the Millrace → ST-14 Charges on the Dam → ST-16 The Blackmatch Ledger → ST-17 Sapper-Chief → ST-20 Ledger Signed "C.A."

Gravelkin are being paid in slag-iron to open sinkholes under the hill dwarves' fields while Slagborn sappers mine the great dam above the dale with cinder-charges. **Dolga Blackmatch** means to drown the valley and Hammerdeep's ground-level halls with it. The Dalefolk and the mountain engineers who built the dam must work together for the first time in a generation. Her ledger records payments from a human — **"C.A."**.

### Khazrun: "The Undercut" — [Stoneheart Deeps](../05-zones/t2-stoneheart-deeps.md)

SD-01 → SD-05 The Underwarrens → SD-07 Paid in Crystal → SD-09 Powder in the Dark → SD-14 The Undercut → SD-16 The Bore-Captain's Plans → SD-17 Stop the Great Bore → SD-20 Plans Signed "C.A."

Inside the mountain, Gravelkin are being paid in crystal to gnaw passages between cavern levels while a Slagborn drilling engine — the **Great Bore** — drives a tunnel toward the foundations of Hammerdeep's Great Forge. **Bore-Captain Vigga Deepdrill** means to drop the forge, and the Thane's Hall above it, into the dark. Her survey plans are drawn in a human hand and initialled **"C.A."**. (The dam and the Bore are two halves of one plan: flood the city from above and collapse it from below.)

## Act III — Flame (levels 10–15)

### Meridia: "Empty Graves" — [Thornwick Marches](../05-zones/t3-thornwick-marches.md)

TM-01 → TM-05 Empty Graves → TM-06 Shovels by Moonlight → TM-10 The Cinder in the Corpse → TM-11 Acolytes of the Brand → TM-14 The Gallowmere Road

Thornwick's dead are being dug up to order. Cult acolytes place a cinder in each corpse and it rises as a **Cinderwight** — an army that needs no pay. The bodies are carted to the old crypts under the Gallowmere.

### Khazrun: "Hexes and Hammers" — [Ironroot Highlands](../05-zones/t3-ironroot-highlands.md)

IR-01 → IR-05 Ogre Sign → IR-06 Brutes at the Gate → IR-10 Hexes and Hammers → IR-11 The Smugglers' Trail → IR-14 Warlord of the Stonemaw → IR-15 Manifest of the Dead

Stonemaw ogres are raiding with branded dwarf-forged hammers. The Slagborn sold them — to start a border war that keeps Hammerdeep's eyes east. Warlord Urgoth's manifest lists a second shipment: *"cinders, 400 count — Gallowmere."* The player takes the ship from Hammerdeep's Seagate to Solhaven and rides north to Thornwick.

## The Dungeon — The Brandfather (levels 13–16)

**TM-15**, inside [Gallowmere Crypts](../07-instances/dungeons.md#1-gallowmere-crypts-1316). Both versions converge at the crypt gate. **Brandfather Oswin Kray**, a defrocked priest, runs the corpse-works. He dies clutching a dossier of the cult's patrons. One name is underlined: **Magister Corvin Ashgrave**, lately of the Queen's court.

## Act IV — Inferno (levels 16–20)

### Meridia: "House of Ash"
TM-24 The Name in the Dossier (to Solhaven; the Queen signs a warrant) → TM-25 House of Ash → TM-26 The Magister's Study. Ashgrave's estate is a factory of brands; his notes speak of "the shard that sings" and of a patron he calls only *the Heir*.

### Khazrun: "Quench the Brandforge"
IR-23 The Name on the Anvil (to Hammerdeep; the High Thane seals a writ) → IR-24 Brandforge Pass → IR-25 Quench the Brandforge → IR-35 Runemaster's Reckoning → IR-36 To the Brandspire. The forge that made every brand is broken and its Runemaster slain; her ledger shows Ashgrave has withdrawn to his tower on the Thornwick coast.

## Finale — The Branded Lord (level 19–20)

| Quest | Beat |
|---|---|
| **TM-35 Brandspire** | Wardens of Thornwick and Rangers of Ironroot besiege the tower together — the first time the player sees humans and dwarves fight as one army. Cut through sentinels and pyromancers; assemble the Spire Key |
| **TM-36 The Branded Lord** | Atop the tower, Ashgrave is serene. *"You think I lit this fire? I am only where it burns."* At 40% his chest splits open: where his heart should be sits a sliver of black crystal. He is **hollow** — has been for years. When he falls the shard dissolves into the air, and something far to the east, under the Rift, *answers* |

**Epilogue (on turn-in):** the Warden-Captain and the Ranger-Captain agree the cult is broken — but neither can say what Ashgrave was *for*. Players who have started Main Chain 2 recognise the shard. Players who have not are pointed to the Athenaeum.

**Rewards:** blue item choice, the title **Brandbreaker**, Solhaven and Hammerdeep reputation, and the Brandspire becomes a phased friendly outpost with an eyrie.

## Design notes

- **Total steps:** 26 quests on either route. **XP:** ≈ 26,000 (≈ 16% of the XP needed for 1–20), so it is a spine, not the whole body.
- **Solo-friendly:** only TM-15 requires a dungeon group; act bosses are tuned for 2–3 but soloable two levels later.
- **Tells both races' stories:** a player who levels a second character on the other continent sees the other half of the plot.
- **Foreshadowing:** "a crown for every brow", "the Heir", hollowness → Main Chain 2. Wildpact envoy Ysolde Thornwake (TM-23) and ogre shaman Mugga (IR-22) set up the Wildpact as a third party with its own interests.
