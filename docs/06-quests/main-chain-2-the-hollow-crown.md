# Main Chain 2 — The Hollow Crown (levels 15–30)

[← Master Plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md) · [Leveling Path](../05-zones/00-leveling-path.md) · Prev: [Main Chain 1](main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md) · Next: [Dungeons](../07-instances/dungeons.md)

**Premise.** Three hundred years ago the Hollow Crown broke into five shards. Now they are being gathered. People who live near a shard are "hollowed" — alive, but emptied. The scholars of the Athenaeum race a hidden rival to the shards, lose, and must send heroes down into a buried city to face the Emperor the Crown was made for.

**Structure.** One short opening act in two versions (either T3 zone), then a single path that crosses all three T4 zones, two dungeons and the raid.

| Shard | Where it is | Who has it | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — the Sliver | Mistral Hollow / Deepseam | A hollowed elder / foreman | **Player recovers it** (Act I) |
| 2 | The Slagworks | Forgemaster Khardun Slagheart, smelting it into a weapon | **Player recovers it** (Act II) |
| 3 | Tor Vael, dug out by outlaw salvagers | Captain Morrigan → sold | Lost to House Vael (Act III) |
| 4 | Heart of Magister Ashgrave | Released at his death (Chain 1) | Flies to House Vael |
| 5 | House Vael heirloom | Lady Seraphine Vael | Always hers |

## Act I — The Hollowing (levels 15–18)

Starts from **Archivist Maelis Dorne** (Solhaven Athenaeum) or **Lorekeeper Brunhild Deepscroll** (Hammerdeep Scrollery) — two rival scholars who become the chain's recurring double-act.

| Meridia — [Thornwick](../05-zones/t3-thornwick-marches.md) | Khazrun — [Ironroot](../05-zones/t3-ironroot-highlands.md) | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| TM-28 The Hollowing | IR-27 The Hollowing | A village / a mine where people have stopped speaking |
| TM-29 Empty Eyes | IR-28 Empty Eyes in the Dark | Through the Lens, the hollowed have no inner light at all |
| TM-30 What the Well Remembers | IR-29 What the Seam Remembers | Aether is leaking from one point: something buried is *drinking* the people |
| TM-31 The Sliver | IR-30 The Sliver | The eldest hollowed guards a splinter of black crystal |
| TM-32 A Crown in Five Pieces | IR-31 A Crown in Five Pieces | The scholars tell the story of Vaelric the Last and the Sundering. The Sliver tugs, like a compass needle, toward the south |

## Act II — The Crucible (levels 20–23) — [Cinderfall Barrens](../05-zones/t4-cinderfall-barrens.md)

CF-01 Ash on the Wind → CF-05 Raiders of the Slagborn → CF-06 The Pyresmith's Orders → **CF-09 The Shard in the Crucible** ([The Slagworks](../07-instances/dungeons.md#2-the-slagworks-2023)) → CF-12 The Shard Sings

The Sliver leads to the Slagborn's great smelter. Forgemaster Khardun has a second, larger shard in his crucible and is trying to forge a *weapon* from it, on commission; his orders are sealed with a crest nobody at the fort recognises — a crowned spire. With Khardun dead, the two recovered shards are brought together. They ring like struck glass, and both lean hard to the west — over the sea, toward Meridia's eastern cape.

## Act III — The Dig (levels 24–27) — [The Riftlands](../05-zones/t4-riftlands.md)

RL-11 Follow the Song → RL-12 Cutthroat Business → RL-13 The Hexwright's Price → RL-14 Three Envoys → RL-15 Captain Morrigan Blackspade → RL-16 Too Late

In the neutral mesa-town of Kite's Rest, old Fenwick Dustwright explains what lies under the canyon floor: Tor Vael, where the Crown was made. The Blackspade Company has been hired to dig out the throne-plaza.

**Three Envoys** is the chain's hinge and the launch game's window onto the future factions. The Wildpact's Ysolde Thornwake wants the Crown destroyed because it wounds the living world. The Covenant's Legate Corvane wants it destroyed because Vaelric, crowned again, would enslave the dead who have been free for three centuries. Both offer help; neither trusts the Concord not to keep it. (The player's dialogue choices here are recorded as a flag for future faction content; they change nothing at launch.)

The player descends the Widow's Shaft and kills Captain Morrigan — and finds her ledger. The shard was brought up a month ago and already sold, for a fortune, to **House Vael of Veilmoor**. The crowned spire.

## Act IV — The Heir (levels 27–30) — [Veilmoor](../05-zones/t4-veilmoor.md)

VM-24 The Buyer → VM-25 Knights Without Faces → VM-26 The Court of Hollows → VM-27 Handmaidens → VM-28 The Keep Wards → **VM-29 The Last Heir** ([Hollowspire Keep](../07-instances/dungeons.md#3-hollowspire-keep-2730)) → VM-32 The Crown Goes Under

Lady Seraphine Vael is the last descendant of the imperial line. Her diary (VM-27) is the emotional core of the chain: orphaned, raised on stories of a stolen throne, she found the family's heirloom shard at fourteen and has been hearing her ancestor's voice ever since. She was "the Heir" who funded the Cinderbrand; Ashgrave was her first hollowed servant. Her whole household has been emptied to feed the shards.

In the keep's chapel the player's group defeats her — but she was never trying to win. As she falls, the three shards she holds **tear the two recovered shards from the player's possession**, the Crown assembles above her, and the chapel floor splits open: the Crown is dragged down into the earth and away to the south, toward the Rift. Her gown is left empty on the stones.

*"He is awake. I only had to bring it close enough to hear him."*

## Finale — The Hollow King (level 30)

| Quest | Beat |
|---|---|
| **RL-35 The Vault Key** | Dorne and Deepscroll, finally working together at Spire Camp, build a key to the sealed throne-vault from three resonant pieces: *Slagheart's Crucible-Shard*, *Seraphine's Signet* and the *Behemoth's Heart*. Requires Friendly with the Athenaeum. This is the **raid attunement** |
| **RL-36 The Hollow King** | Ten heroes descend into [The Hollow Throne](../07-instances/raid-the-hollow-throne.md). Vaelric sits where he has sat for 312 years, the Crown whole on his brow. Kill him and break it |

**Ending.** The Crown shatters for good. Vaelric's last line — *"Then who will hold it all together?"* — hangs over the epilogue: at Kite's Rest, the three envoys watch the dust-storms over the Rift die away, and each quietly sends a messenger home. The truce that existed *because* of the common threat is over. (Sets up the three-faction war and the Sundrel expansion.)

**Rewards:** epic item choice, title **Crownbreaker**, Athenaeum reputation, and a permanent change to the Tor Vael skybox (the storm clears) for characters who have finished.

## Design notes

- **Total steps:** 25 quests (5 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 2). **XP:** ≈ 62,000.
- **Group content on the spine:** two dungeon steps and the raid step. Everything else is soloable; RL-15 is tuned for 3.
- **Players lose.** The Crown is assembled despite them — this justifies the raid and avoids the "world saved at level 20, why is there a raid" problem.
- **Each T4 zone gets one act**, guaranteeing every player sees all three zones and their world bosses.
- **The two scholars** (human and dwarf) keep both races present in a single-path story.
- **Chain 1 link:** not a prerequisite. Chain 1 finishers get extra dialogue in VM-27 and RL-14.
