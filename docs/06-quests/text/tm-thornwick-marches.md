### TM-01 · [MC1] The Warden-Captain
*Level 10 · Requires HF-20 or RW-20, or none (see notes) · Warden-Captain Alric Dunmoor → Warden-Captain Dunmoor*

**Offer:** Thornwick is the last walled town before the fens. I hold it with sixty wardens and a great deal of stubbornness. Our dead are leaving their graves, and I can't tell the Mayor why. If you've come from the south with something that explains it, I'm listening.
**Tracker:** Deliver the "C.A." papers to Warden-Captain Dunmoor
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** The same hand, north and south. So the bandits, the fires and my empty graves are one piece of work. Good. One enemy I can fight. Speak to Gravekeeper Hollis at the cemetery. He saw it first and nobody believed him.
**Rewards:** 375 XP
**Notes:** If the player delivered HF-20 or RW-20 to Dunmoor, this is offered and completed in the same conversation. Offered directly at level 10 to anyone else; the tracker then reads "Speak to Warden-Captain Dunmoor".

### TM-04 · A Town Afraid
*Level 10 · Requires — · Mayor Lysa Hartwick → Mayor Hartwick*

**Offer:** People are frightened, and frightened people tell their Mayor nothing. They'll talk to a stranger. Would you go round the town for me? Speak to the innkeeper at the Hanged Lantern, the smith, and old Hollis at the cemetery. Tell me what they're really saying.
**Tracker:** Speak to the innkeeper · Speak to the smith · Speak to Gravekeeper Hollis
**Progress:** Have you seen all three?
**Complete:** So the inn is full of people who won't go home after dark, the smith is forging locks for coffins, and Hollis says the graves are being robbed from the outside. Not rising. Robbed. That is somehow worse. Thank you, <name>.
**Rewards:** 375 XP · 3s
**Notes:** Each NPC has one gossip line that grants credit. Innkeeper: "Nobody's gone home since the Tanner boy saw his grandmother at the window." Smith: "Coffin locks. Forty this week. I never thought I'd make one." Hollis: "They're not climbing out. Somebody's digging them out. Look at the spoil. It's thrown *outward*."
