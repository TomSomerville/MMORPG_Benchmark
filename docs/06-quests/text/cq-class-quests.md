# Quest Text — Class Quests (levels 10, 20 and 30)

[← Quest Text Index](00-quest-text-index.md) · Rules: [Class Framework §6](../../03-classes/class-framework.md#6-class-quests)

The ten class quests: three Fighter, four Cleric, three Druid. The Druid's level 20 quest teaches Reawaken only; Druids never shapeshift. Each is offered by any trainer of that class at the stated level (in practice the player's capital trainer sends word: the trainer NPC in any hub shows the quest marker). Each has a Meridia and a Khazrun version of its objective with identical text otherwise; the player may do either. Format and global rules are in the [index](00-quest-text-index.md). These quests are not in any zone table and are counted separately from the 296 zone quests.

Class-quest reward items are blue, Bind on Pickup, at item level = quest level + 8.

## Fighter

### CQ-FIG-10 · The Shield-Wall Lesson
*Level 10 · Requires level 10 · Any Fighter trainer → Master-at-Arms Godric Stane (Thornwick) or Axe-Thane Ragnar Holdfast (Ironroot Hold)*

**Offer:** You hit hard enough. Now learn to be hit. There's a line being held in the north that could use another shield, and a master there who teaches the stance the old legions used. Report to him. Bring a shield. If you don't own one, buy one. This isn't a lesson you can borrow for.
**Tracker:** Report to the stance-master · Hold the line: survive 3 waves with a shield equipped · Taunt 5 attackers off the recruit
**Progress:** Shield up. Higher. You're guarding your knees.
**Complete:** You stood, and the lad behind you is still breathing. That's the whole of it. Everything else I teach is detail. This is Guard Stance. You'll hit softer in it and you'll be very hard to kill, and anything you mock will come for you and not for your friends.
**Rewards:** 1,125 XP · teaches **Guard Stance**, **Taunt** and **Sunder** (rank 1) · *Stance-Master's Heater* (shield, ilvl 18, +5 Str +7 Sta)
**Notes:** Meridia: at Thornwick's north gate, 3 waves of Restless Bones (3, 4, 5) attack a Warden Recruit (800 health). Khazrun: at Ironroot Hold's outer gate, 3 waves of Stonemaw Brutes (2, 2, 3) attack a Ranger Recruit. The player is lent Taunt for the event. Fails if the recruit dies; repeat at once.

### CQ-FIG-20 · Trial of the Red Field
*Level 20 · Requires CQ-FIG-10 · Any Fighter trainer → Knight-Marshal Osmund Heron (Solhaven) or Axe-Master Thorgrim Twinbit (Hammerdeep)*

**Offer:** There is a stance I don't teach in a yard. You learn it with your back to nothing and your shield left at home. Go out to the proving ground and fight what I've arranged for you. Three opponents, one after another, no rest. Don't defend. I'll know if you defend.
**Tracker:** Defeat the First Blade · Defeat the Second Blade · Defeat the Champion of the Field
**Progress:** Still standing? Then you're not done.
**Complete:** There. You felt it, in the third bout. The moment you stopped caring whether you were hit. That's Reckless Stance, and it will get you killed if you live in it. Visit it. Choose your steel.
**Rewards:** 2,625 XP · 30s · teaches **Reckless Stance** · choose 1: *Red Field Greatsword* (2H sword, ilvl 28, speed 3.5, +18 Str +25 Sta) · *Red Field Twins* (two 1H swords given together, ilvl 28, speed 2.3, each +9 Str +12 Sta) · *Red Field Reaping-Axe* (2H axe, ilvl 28, speed 3.6, +18 Str +25 Sta)
**Notes:** Meridia: the old tilt-field east of Dawn's Stand. Khazrun: the duelling ring at Fort Ashwatch. Opponents are friendly-faction sparring NPCs (level 20, 21, 22; ×2, ×2.5, ×3.5 template health) who yield at 5%. Shields cannot be equipped inside the ring. The player keeps the health they end each bout with.

### CQ-FIG-30 · Bladebound
*Level 30 · Requires CQ-FIG-20 · Any Fighter trainer → Weapon Master Aldous Brandt (Solhaven) or Weapon Master Sigvald Ninehafts (Hammerdeep)*

**Offer:** A soldier carries a weapon. A master is carried by one. I can have a blade made for your hand and nobody else's, but the smith needs three things I can't buy: starmetal that has killed something, a hilt-wrap cut from a drake, and an edge quenched somewhere the dead have stood. Bring them and I'll bind them.
**Tracker:** Blooded Starmetal 0/1 · Cinderwing Hilt-Hide 0/1 · Quench the blank at a barrow-spring or the Emberwell
**Progress:** All three, or it's only a sword.
**Complete:** Hold it. No, don't swing, just hold it. Feel how it sits? That's yours. It'll never suit another hand as well. Wear it into that vault under the Rift and bring it back with a story.
**Rewards:** 4,575 XP · 1g · choose 1: *Oathbound Greatblade* (2H sword, ilvl 38, speed 3.5, +24 Str +35 Sta) · *Oathbound Warblade* (1H sword, ilvl 38, speed 2.4, +12 Str +17 Sta) and *Oathbound Parrying-Blade* (off-hand 1H sword, same stats) · *Oathbound Guardblade* (1H sword, ilvl 38, speed 2.2, +10 Str +15 Sta, +6 Defense)
**Notes:** Blooded Starmetal: drops from Clinker (Cinderfall) or any Tor Vael Sentinel (5%), or from the last boss of the Slagworks (100% for players on the quest). Cinderwing Hilt-Hide: Cinderwing Drake (15%) or Rimewing Whelp (8%). Quench: use the *Unquenched Blank* (given at accept) at the Emberwell spring-head or the barrow-spring on the Veilmoor Barrowfield.

## Cleric

### CQ-CLE-10a · Vigil of the First Flame
*Level 10 · Requires level 10 · Any Cleric trainer → Sister Avelyn (Thornwick) or Ancestor-speaker Gudrun Hallowstone (Stoneheart Deeps)*

**Offer:** You have learned to mend a wound. There is a harder thing, and it is not taught, it is kept. Go to the one who keeps the vigil over the unquiet dead. Take a candle lit from this flame and do not let it go out on the road. If it does, come back and begin again. I will not think less of you. Everyone comes back once.
**Tracker:** Carry the Vigil-Flame to the vigil-keeper without letting it go out
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Still burning. Then you can keep a small thing safe a long way, and that is the first half. Set it there, among the others. Now you will sit with me until dawn, and tomorrow you will do the second half.
**Rewards:** 1,125 XP · *Vigil-Keeper's Mace* (1H mace, ilvl 18, speed 2.5, +5 Str +5 Int +4 Sta)
**Notes:** *Vigil-Flame* is given at accept. It goes out if the player takes damage from a hostile creature, enters water, or mounts; the quest then fails and can be retaken. Walking the roads avoids all three.

### CQ-CLE-10b · The Second Half
*Level 12 · Requires CQ-CLE-10a · Sister Avelyn or Ancestor-speaker Hallowstone → the same*

**Offer:** There is a soldier in the next room who died this morning. Not long enough to be lost. Long enough to be frightened. The rite takes ten breaths, and for all ten you must want it for them and not for yourself. Go in. I will be at the door.
**Tracker:** Perform the rite of Raise the Fallen on the Fallen Warden
**Progress:** Ten breaths. Do not rush them.
**Complete:** They are awake, and asking for water, and they do not know your name. They never do. That is how you will know you did it for the right reason. You may call the dead back now, <name>, when the fighting is over. Never during. The Dawn does not hurry.
**Rewards:** 930 XP · teaches **Raise the Fallen**
**Notes:** The player is given a one-use version of the spell; the 10-second cast on the *Fallen Warden* (or *Fallen Lamp-Guard*) object completes the quest.

### CQ-CLE-20 · The Oath Unbroken
*Level 20 · Requires CQ-CLE-10b · Any Cleric trainer → High Dawnmother Seraphie Ward (Solhaven) or High Hearthkeeper Arnora Flamewarden (Hammerdeep)*

**Offer:** Ground can be made holy. It is done by standing on it and refusing to leave. There is a place the dead have claimed that was ours first. Go there, plant this standard, and hold the ground under it until the standard burns white. They will come at you from every side. That is the point.
**Tracker:** Plant the Dawn Standard · Hold the ground for 90 seconds
**Progress:** If you step off the ground, the standard gutters.
**Complete:** The standard burned white. I saw it from here, or I like to think I did. What you felt under your feet is Hallowed Ground. You can call it anywhere now. Stand in it, and let them come to you.
**Rewards:** 2,625 XP · 30s · teaches **Hallowed Ground** · choose 1: *Aegis of the Unbroken Oath* (shield, ilvl 28, +10 Str +14 Sta) · *Oathkeeper's Maul* (2H mace, ilvl 28, speed 3.6, +14 Str +10 Int +18 Sta) · *Dawn Standard-Bearer's Sceptre* (1H mace, ilvl 28, speed 2.6, +8 Int, +26 healing)
**Notes:** Meridia: the Barrowfield edge in Veilmoor (waves of Barrow Wights). Khazrun: the Buried Caravan in Cinderfall (waves of restless Slagborn dead, level 22–23). 6 waves of 2 at 15-second intervals; the player must stay within 8 yards of the standard and is lent Hallowed Ground rank 1 during the event.

### CQ-CLE-30 · The Dawnbearer's Charge
*Level 30 · Requires CQ-CLE-20 · Any Cleric trainer → Brother-Templar Edric Stone (Solhaven) or Oath-Warden Grimkell Stonevow (Hammerdeep)*

**Offer:** Every cleric who goes into the dark carries the words with them. I want you to carry them bound. A psalter, written in your own hand, in ink mixed with three things: ash from a consecrated fire, water from a spring that has been defended, and gall from a thing that should not live. Write it, bind it, and bring it to me to be sealed.
**Tracker:** Consecrated Ash 0/1 · Defended Spring-Water 0/1 · Hollow Gall 0/1 · Write the psalter at a shrine
**Progress:** In your own hand. I'll know.
**Complete:** Your letters wander. Good. It means you wrote it and didn't copy it. It's sealed. Keep it where you can reach it. You won't read from it. You'll just know it's there.
**Rewards:** 4,575 XP · 1g · choose 1: *Psalter of the Dawnbearer* (relic; Mending Light heals +30) · *Psalter of the Shield-Sworn* (relic; Hallowed Ground deals +18 total and its threat +10%) · *Psalter of Reckoning* (relic; Verdict deals +14)
**Notes:** Consecrated Ash: use Hallowed Ground on the remains of any elite undead. Defended Spring-Water: complete CF-35 or fill the flask at the Pilgrim's Cairn font after VM-21. Hollow Gall: any Hollowed elite (10%) or any boss of Hollowspire Keep (100%). Write at the shrine in either capital's Cleric district (10-second channel).

## Druid

### CQ-DRU-10 · The Circle's Welcome
*Level 10 · Requires level 10 · Any Druid trainer → Hedgewarden Sorrel (Russetwood) or Stonesinger Fjola (Stoneheart Deeps)*

**Offer:** You've been taught to mend and to sting. You haven't been taught to listen. There's a keeper who tends a place where the old growth is very close to the surface. Go there, and do as you're told, which will be very little. Mostly you'll be asked to wait.
**Tracker:** Speak to the keeper · Sit in the circle until the grove answers · Heal 5 wounded creatures of the grove with what you hear
**Progress:** You're fidgeting. It can tell.
**Complete:** There it is. You stopped pushing and let it come up through you. That's Overgrowth. It mends at once and then goes on mending, the way a wood closes over a path. You're of the circle now. Take a staff cut from this place.
**Rewards:** 1,125 XP · teaches **Overgrowth** (rank 1) · *Circle-Cut Staff* (staff, ilvl 18, speed 3.0, +9 Int +8 Spi +3 Sta)
**Notes:** Meridia: the Mossback Stand (wounded fawns and a young treant). Khazrun: the Glowcap Grotto (wounded cave-crickets and a young shambler). "Sit in the circle" is a 30-second wait in a marked ring. The player is lent Overgrowth for the 5 heals.

### CQ-DRU-20 · Seed of the Old Wood
*Level 20 · Requires CQ-DRU-10 · Any Druid trainer → Hedgewarden Corwin Ashby (Thornwick) or Stonesinger Eitri Deepmoss (Ironroot)*

**Offer:** There is one more thing to learn, and it's the heaviest I'll ever hand you. A seed from the first wood, the one that was here before the Empire, before the hedge-circles. Planted in someone who has just fallen, it'll grow them back. In the middle of a fight. With everything still trying to kill you both. Go and fetch one. The old wood doesn't give them up to the careless.
**Tracker:** Seed of the Old Wood 0/1 · Reawaken the fallen sentinel-spirit
**Progress:** It has to be given, not taken. You'll understand when you get there.
**Complete:** It worked. And it cost you a seed, and it'll cost you one every time, and there'll be days you have none and someone's on the ground. That's the weight of it. Buy seeds. Always carry seeds.
**Rewards:** 2,625 XP · 30s · teaches **Reawaken** (rank 1) · 3 *Seed of Renewal* · choose 1: *Old-Wood Seed-Pouch* (held off-hand, ilvl 28, +10 Int +9 Spi) · *Antler-Hafted Mace* (1H mace, ilvl 28, speed 2.5, +8 Int +8 Spi)
**Notes:** Meridia: the heart-tree in the Blackwater Fen, guarded by 3 Bog Shamblers that must be subdued (not killed) as in RW-12. Khazrun: the root-hollow under the Singing Stones, guarded by 3 Gravelkin Deepdelvers that must be driven off. The seed is then used on a *Fallen Sentinel-Spirit* while 2 hostile creatures attack.

### CQ-DRU-30 · Convergence
*Level 30 · Requires CQ-DRU-20 · Any Druid trainer → Arch-Hedgewarden Isabeau Thorn (Solhaven) or Elder Stonesinger Ragnhild Rootmother (Hammerdeep)*

**Offer:** The Choir sings from below and the Lattice shines from above, and you've spent thirty seasons learning to hear one or the other. Now hear both at once. There are three places where they meet: a root that has grown into starmetal, a pool that holds the sky at noon, a stone the lightning returns to. Take a charm-blank to each. Let them mark it.
**Tracker:** Charm marked at the starmetal root · Charm marked at the noon pool · Charm marked at the lightning-stone
**Progress:** Three marks. They'll look like nothing. They aren't.
**Complete:** Three marks, and they've run together into one. That's the convergence, there, in your palm. Most of us pick a side and call it wisdom. You'll carry both. Choose how it's strung.
**Rewards:** 4,575 XP · 1g · choose 1: *Charm of the Verdant Convergence* (relic; Renewal heals +24 in total) · *Charm of the Storm Convergence* (relic; Thornlash deals +12) · *Charm of the Star Convergence* (relic; Astral Lance deals +20)
**Notes:** Starmetal root: beside any Starmetal Deposit on the Molten Stair, in Tor Vael or on the Barrowfield (one marked object in each zone). Noon pool: the Emberwell lower pool or a Veilmoor moor pool, usable only between 11:00 and 13:00 game time. Lightning-stone: the top of Kite's Perch in the Riftlands or the Screecher Peaks in Ironroot, during a storm or dust-storm weather state (the object also triggers one 60 seconds after being touched).
