# Quest Text — Brightmead Vale (BM-01 to BM-18)

### BM-01 · A Warden's Welcome
*Level 1 · Requires — · (granted at character creation) → Warden Elyse Farrow*

**Offer:** You have come to Larkhill Steading to answer the Concord's call for able hands. The Warden of the vale, Elyse Farrow, keeps the lodge at the top of the lane. She is expecting you.
**Tracker:** Speak to Warden Farrow at the lodge
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** So you're the new one. Good. I asked Solhaven for ten and they sent me you, so you'll have to be worth ten. Larkhill is a quiet posting, <name>. I mean to keep it that way, and I'll need your help to do it.
**Rewards:** 40 XP
**Notes:** Auto-accepted on first login. Farrow stands 20 yards from the spawn point.

### BM-02 · Wolves at the Fold
*Level 1 · Requires BM-01 · Warden Farrow → Warden Farrow*

**Offer:** The wolves whelped early this year, and the pups have no fear of people. They've been at the lambing pens every night this week. I won't have the shepherds sitting up with pitchforks. Go out to the pastures and thin the pups before they grow into something worse.
**Tracker:** Vale Wolf Pups slain 0/8
**Progress:** The pastures are just past the lane, <name>. The pups won't thin themselves.
**Complete:** Eight fewer mouths at the pens. The shepherds will sleep tonight, and you've shown me you can follow a simple order. Take a pair of these. Your hands will thank you.
**Rewards:** 80 XP · 25c · choose 1: *Larkhill Cloth Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 3) · *Larkhill Hide Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 3) · *Larkhill Ringed Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 3)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### BM-03 · A Summons from Your Trainer
*Level 1 · Requires BM-01 · Warden Farrow → class trainer*

**Offer:** One more thing. Your trainer sent word that they want to see you before you get yourself hurt. Fighters report to Sergeant Kern at the practice yard, clerics to Brother Aldwin at the shrine, druids to Hedgewarden Mirelle by the old oak. Don't keep them waiting.
**Tracker:** Speak to your class trainer
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** There you are. The Warden says you can swing at a wolf pup without falling over. That's a start. Everything after that, you learn from me, and the first lesson costs you nothing.
**Rewards:** 40 XP · the first trainable ability is free (trainer cost waived once)
**Notes:** Turn-in NPC is chosen by class: Sergeant Tobias Kern (Fighter), Brother Aldwin (Cleric), Hedgewarden Mirelle (Druid). Completion text is shared.

### BM-04 · Thieves Below
*Level 2 · Requires BM-02 · Quartermaster Hobb → Quartermaster Hobb*

**Offer:** Burrowkin. Little candle-headed thieves, living in the old Hartwell Mine. They used to take a turnip here and there. Now they come up through my cellar floor and take whole crates. I count stock for a living, <name>, and my count is wrong every morning. Go down to the mine mouth and make it right.
**Tracker:** Burrowkin Diggers slain 0/8
**Progress:** Still hearing scratching under the floorboards.
**Complete:** Quiet under the floor for the first time in a month. Here, from the stock you saved. Eat it before something else does.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 50c · 5 *Larkhill Brown Bread* (food, restores 61 health over 18 s)

### BM-05 · Candle Count
*Level 2 · Requires BM-02 · Quartermaster Hobb → Quartermaster Hobb*

**Offer:** While you're down there: every Burrowkin wears a tallow candle on its head. My tallow. I had forty candles put by for winter and now I have none. Bring me back eight and I'll call the ledger square.
**Tracker:** Tallow Candles 0/8
**Progress:** How many candles have you got? I'll know if you're short.
**Complete:** Eight. A bit chewed, but eight. You'll want something to carry your winnings in if you mean to keep this up.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 50c · *Hobb's Spare Pouch* (6-slot bag)
**Notes:** Tallow Candle drops from Burrowkin Digger, Tunneler and Candlewarden (70%).

### BM-06 · Bristles and Bacon
*Level 2 · Requires BM-02 · Cook Marta → Cook Marta*

**Offer:** The Warden feeds twenty hands out of my kitchen and sends me nothing to cook. The meadow boars are fat on windfall apples just now. Bring me six good haunches and I'll show you how to roast one properly. A <class> who can't feed themselves doesn't last.
**Tracker:** Boar Haunches 0/6
**Progress:** No haunches, no supper.
**Complete:** Now those are haunches. Watch: salt, hot stones, turn it once. There. You can do it yourself next time.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 25c · *Recipe: Roasted Boar Haunch* (Cooking 1) · 5 *Roasted Boar Haunch*
**Notes:** Boar Haunch drops from Meadow Boar (75%). If the player does not know Cooking, the recipe stays in the bag until they learn it.

### BM-07 · [MC1] The Stolen Seed-Grain
*Level 3 · Requires BM-04 · Warden Farrow → Warden Farrow*

**Offer:** Hobb's candles are one thing. This is another. Last night the Burrowkin took the spring seed-grain, all of it, out of a locked barn. Without it there is no planting, and without planting the vale goes hungry by autumn. The sacks are somewhere in Hartwell Mine. Bring back what you can carry.
**Tracker:** Grain Sacks recovered 0/6
**Progress:** Every sack matters, <name>. That grain is next year's bread.
**Complete:** All six. Good. But you say they were stacked? Neatly, beside crates with an ash mark on them? Burrowkin don't stack. Burrowkin don't plan. Somebody paid them to do this, and I don't like that at all.
**Rewards:** 200 XP · 1s · choose 1: *Larkhill Arming Sword* (1H sword, ilvl 5, speed 2.2) · *Shrine-Keeper's Mace* (1H mace, ilvl 5, speed 2.4) · *Hedgewarden's Walking Staff* (staff, ilvl 5, speed 3.0)
**Notes:** Grain Sacks are world objects in the Tunneler gallery of Hartwell Mine (10 spawn points). Each stands beside an *Ash-Marked Crate* (scenery, examine text: "A hand pressed in ash. No maker's name.").

### BM-08 · Road to Millbrook
*Level 3 · Requires BM-07 · Warden Farrow → Reeve Halvard Stokes*

**Offer:** I've written down what you found. Take this report down the road to Reeve Stokes in Millbrook. He keeps the peace for the whole vale, and he should hear about paid thieves before he hears about them from a burned barn. Stay on the road and you'll be there before dark.
**Tracker:** Deliver the Warden's Report to Reeve Stokes in Millbrook
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** From Farrow? Let me see. Hm. Ash marks on crates. I had hoped she was going to tell me it was only Burrowkin. Rest at the Miller's Rest if you need it, <name>. I think I'll have work for you.
**Rewards:** 100 XP · 50c
**Notes:** *Warden's Report* is given at accept. Completing this unlocks the Millbrook eyrie point when the player speaks to Eyrie-keeper Wren Aldous.

### BM-09 · Webs in Greenhollow
*Level 4 · Requires BM-08 · Reeve Stokes → Reeve Stokes*

**Offer:** The woodcutters won't go into Greenhollow any more. Spiders, big as hounds, strung across every path. We need that timber for the new granary. Clear ten of them and the cutters might find their courage again.
**Tracker:** Greenhollow Spiders slain 0/10
**Progress:** The cutters are still sitting in the inn, so I take it the spiders are still in the wood.
**Complete:** I heard axes this morning. First time in a fortnight. You've earned a decent pair of boots out of the vale's purse.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 1s 50c · choose 1: *Greenhollow Slippers* (cloth feet, ilvl 6) · *Woodcutter's Boots* (leather feet, ilvl 6) · *Millbrook Chain Boots* (mail feet, ilvl 6)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### BM-10 · Silk for the Seamstress
*Level 4 · Requires BM-08 · Seamstress Odette Lane → Seamstress Lane*

**Offer:** You're going into Greenhollow? Then do a seamstress a kindness. Spider silk is the finest thread there is and I haven't had a spool since the wood went bad. Six lengths, clean ones, not the sticky rubbish. I'll make it worth your while.
**Tracker:** Spider Silk 0/6
**Progress:** Clean silk, mind. Wind it round a stick.
**Complete:** Oh, that's lovely. Look at the sheen on it. Here, I made this up from the last of my old stock. It'll keep the rain off better than whatever you came in with.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 75c · *Lane's Stitched Cloak* (back, ilvl 9, +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Spider Silk drops from Greenhollow Spider (65%).

### BM-11 · [MC1] Smoke on the Horizon
*Level 4 · Requires BM-08 · Reeve Stokes → Miller Joss Cray*

**Offer:** Look east. That smoke is coming from Cray's mill, and Joss Cray doesn't burn stubble in spring. I can't leave the town. Go and see what has happened, and if Cray is alive, hear what he saw.
**Tracker:** Investigate the Burned Mill · Speak to Miller Cray
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Thirty years. Thirty years that wheel turned, and they burned it in a night. I saw them, <name>. Men, not Burrowkin. Laughing. Every one of them had hands black with ash to the wrist, and they pressed them on my door before they lit it.
**Rewards:** 135 XP
**Notes:** "Investigate" completes on entering the mill yard (area trigger). Cray is hiding behind the cider press on the orchard side.

### BM-12 · [MC1] The Ashen Hand
*Level 4 · Requires BM-11 · Miller Cray → Miller Cray*

**Offer:** They're still here. Cutpurses going through what's left of my house, and the ones with torches working their way down the orchard rows. I'm an old man with a flour scoop. You're not. Drive them off my land.
**Tracker:** Ashhand Cutpurses slain 0/8 · Ashhand Torchbearers slain 0/4
**Progress:** I can still hear them laughing out there.
**Complete:** Gone, or dead, and I find I don't much care which. Take this. It was my son's, and he'd want it worn by somebody who stood up.
**Rewards:** 340 XP · 2s · choose 1: *Cray's Rope Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 6) · *Miller's Leather Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 6) · *Orchard-Guard Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 6)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### BM-13 · Cray's Last Barrels
*Level 4 · Requires BM-11 · Miller Cray → Miller Cray*

**Offer:** The mill's gone, but the cider isn't. I rolled five barrels out into the orchard before the roof came down. If those brutes haven't staved them in, that's all I have left to sell this year. Bring them back to the press, would you?
**Tracker:** Cider Barrels recovered 0/5
**Progress:** Five barrels, out among the trees. Mind the ones with torches.
**Complete:** All five, and not a stave cracked. Sit down. No, sit. Nobody saves a man's cider and leaves without a cup of it.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 1s · 5 *Cray's Sweet Cider* (drink, restores 151 mana over 18 s)
**Notes:** Cider Barrels are world objects among the orchard rows (8 spawn points).

### BM-14 · Something Fishy at Silverrun
*Level 5 · Requires BM-08 · Fisher Tam Rill → Fisher Rill*

**Offer:** Gillkin. Fish-folk, come up the creek from who knows where. They've cut my nets and built their little mud huts right on the best pool. A man can't fish with a spear coming at him out of the reeds. Clear them off the Silverrun and you can have my spare pole.
**Tracker:** Silverrun Gillkin slain 0/8
**Progress:** They're still croaking down there. I can hear it from my door.
**Complete:** That's the pool back. Here's the pole I promised. Drop a line by the big willow. That's where the fat ones sit.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Rill's Spare Fishing Pole* (fishing pole, ilvl 5)
**Notes:** Gillkin call allies within 10 yards; huts are placed so pulls of two are normal.

### BM-15 · [MC1] Branded Coin
*Level 5 · Requires BM-12 · Reeve Stokes → Reeve Stokes*

**Offer:** Ash on their hands, Cray says. I want to know who is paying them. Men like that carry their wages. There's a camp in the northern hills where they've been gathering. Go through their purses and bring me what you find.
**Tracker:** Branded Coins 0/6
**Progress:** Their purses, <name>. Whatever they've been paid in.
**Complete:** These aren't Concord marks. Look: a hand, burned into the silver after it was struck. Somebody is taking honest coin and branding it. That's an oath-token. These men haven't been hired. They've been recruited.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s 50c · choose 1: *Reeve's Cloth Cuffs* (cloth wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Int +1 Spi) · *Hillwatch Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Agi +1 Sta) · *Millbrook Chain Bracers* (mail wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Str +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Branded Coin drops from Ashhand Cutpurse, Torchbearer and Brander (70%).

### BM-16 · [MC1] The Brander
*Level 5 · Requires BM-15 · Reeve Stokes → Reeve Stokes*

**Offer:** One of the prisoners talked. Their recruiter is a man called Hale Morrow, and he's set up in Hartwell Deep, under the old mine, putting a hot iron to anyone desperate enough to kneel for it. I want him stopped before the whole vale has ash on its hands. He won't be alone. Take a friend if you have one.
**Tracker:** "Brander" Hale Morrow slain 0/1
**Progress:** Hartwell Deep, below the Burrowkin galleries. Don't go down alone if you can help it.
**Complete:** Dead. And those were his last words? "It burns clean. You'll beg for it." I've buried men who died cursing and men who died praying. I never heard one die selling. Choose something from the armoury, <name>. You've earned better than we can give.
**Rewards:** 525 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Morrow's Branding Iron* (1H mace, ilvl 10, speed 2.4, +3 Str +2 Sta) · *Hartwell Greatpick* (2H axe, ilvl 10, speed 3.4, +6 Str +5 Sta) · *Reeve's Ashwood Staff* (staff, ilvl 10, speed 3.0, +5 Int +4 Spi +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Suggested players: 2. Morrow casts Searing Brand and summons 2 Ashhand Cutpurses at 50% health. He yells the last-words line on death. He drops *Morrow's Brand* (quest item for BM-18) for every player on BM-16.

### BM-17 · Old Greymaw
*Level 5 · Requires BM-09 · Shepherd Dunn → Shepherd Dunn*

**Offer:** There's a wolf in Greenhollow that isn't like the others. Grey in the muzzle, big as a calf, and clever. He's had eleven of my ewes and he took my dog. The Warden says she can't spare anyone. I'm asking you. Bring him down, and bring a friend, because he won't be alone and he won't run.
**Tracker:** Old Greymaw slain 0/1
**Progress:** He hunts the deep end of the wood, past the fallen oak.
**Complete:** You got him. I'd have liked to see it. My father made these from that brute's sire, twenty years back. Seems right they go to whoever ended the line.
**Rewards:** 525 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Greymaw Pelt Mantle* (leather shoulders, ilvl 10, +4 Agi +5 Sta) · *Wolfshead Buckler* (shield, ilvl 10, +2 Str +3 Sta) · *Shepherd's Crook-Charm* (held off-hand, ilvl 10, +3 Int +2 Spi)
**Notes:** Elite, level 5; patrols with 2 Vale Wolves. Suggested players: 2. The off-hand option was added so druids have a choice.

### BM-18 · [MC1] Word to Harrowfield
*Level 5 · Requires BM-16 · Reeve Stokes → Reeve Maren Holt (Harrow's Cross) or Forest-Reeve Birch (Cooper's Hollow)*

**Offer:** This is bigger than one valley. Take Morrow's iron north. Reeve Maren Holt at Harrow's Cross has more farms to lose than I do. Or, if you'd sooner take the forest track, Forest-Reeve Birch at Cooper's Hollow in the Russetwood should see it. Either of them needs to know what a branded hand means before one knocks on their door.
**Tracker:** Carry Morrow's Brand to Reeve Holt in Harrowfield or Forest-Reeve Birch in Russetwood
**Progress:** —
**Complete (Holt):** A branding iron. And the ash hand on it. Sit down, <name>. We've had strangers at the crossroads asking the field hands whether they're happy with their lot. I think you've just told me who sent them.
**Complete (Birch):** Let me see that. Aye. I've found that mark cut into three of my oldest trees this month and took it for boys with knives. It isn't boys, is it?
**Rewards:** 175 XP · 1s
**Notes:** *Morrow's Brand* is the item looted in BM-16. Turning in to Holt unlocks HF-01; turning in to Birch unlocks RW-01. Either satisfies the Act I step of [Embers of the Brand](../main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md); the other zone's quests remain available as side content.
