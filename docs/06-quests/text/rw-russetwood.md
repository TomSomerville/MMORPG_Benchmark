### RW-01 · [MC1] The Forester's Burden
*Level 5 · Requires BM-18 or ST-18, or none (see notes) · Forest-Reeve Anselm Birch → Forest-Reeve Birch*

**Offer:** The Russetwood has stood in autumn since before the Concord had a name. I've walked it forty years and I know its sounds. Lately there are new ones. Axes at night. Kilns burning where no charcoal-burner of mine has a stack. If you've come with news, give it to me straight.
**Tracker:** Present the brand to Forest-Reeve Birch
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** An ash hand. Then the marks on my trees are a claim. Somebody is telling the wood it belongs to them. Well, it doesn't. You're a forester now, <name>, whether you wanted to be or not. Joan Thicket runs my patrols. Start with her.
**Rewards:** 175 XP
**Notes:** If the player turned in BM-18 to Birch, this quest is offered and completed in the same conversation. Offered directly at level 5 to anyone else; the tracker then reads "Speak to Forest-Reeve Birch".

### RW-02 · Foxes in the Henhouse
*Level 5 · Requires — · Innkeeper Marnie Bung → Innkeeper Bung*

**Offer:** If you want eggs with your breakfast at the Split Stave, you'll do something about the foxes. They've had nine of my hens this week. The woods are full of them this year, and they're thin and bold. Ten foxes, and breakfast is on the house.
**Tracker:** Russet Foxes slain 0/10
**Progress:** They dart off when they're hurt. Don't chase them into a second one.
**Complete:** The hens are laying again. Here's your breakfast, and here's something better. A traveller left it against his bill and never came back.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Split-Stave Travelling Cloak* (back, ilvl 10, +3 Sta +3 Spi)

### RW-03 · Beetles in the Timber
*Level 6 · Requires — · Master Cooper Hilde Stave → Master Cooper Stave*

**Offer:** A barrel is only as good as its staves, and leafmold beetles have got into my seasoned oak. They spit something that eats the wood to pulp. Go out along the Rides and kill eight before there's not a sound plank left in the Hollow.
**Tracker:** Leafmold Beetles slain 0/8
**Progress:** What they spit will eat your armor the same as my oak.
**Complete:** Good. I can save most of the stack. Coopers' gloves, these. They'll stand up to a mallet, so they'll stand up to most things.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Stave-Sorter's Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 11, +4 Int +4 Spi) · *Cooper's Hide Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 11, +4 Agi +6 Sta) · *Hoop-Setter's Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 11, +4 Str +6 Sta)

### RW-04 · Venison and Chanterelles
*Level 6 · Requires — · Cook Ham Gristle → Cook Gristle*

**Offer:** Foresters eat what the forest gives, and what it gives this time of year is venison and chanterelles. Six cuts off the stags, four good mushrooms. You'll find those in the leaf-litter where the beetles feed. Bring me the lot and I'll show you a stew that'll keep you walking all day.
**Tracker:** Venison 0/6 · Chanterelles 0/4
**Progress:** The stags will knock you flat if you stand in front of them. Stand anywhere else.
**Complete:** That's proper meat. Now, you brown it first. Anyone who tells you different is a poacher. Take a few bowls with you.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 1s 50c · *Recipe: Woodsman's Stew* (Cooking 25) · 5 *Woodsman's Stew*
**Notes:** Venison drops from Rutting Stag (75%). Chanterelles drop from Leafmold Beetle (60%). Cook Gristle is at Birch's Lodge.

### RW-05 · [MC1] Poachers' Trails
*Level 6 · Requires RW-01 · Forester Joan Thicket → Forester Thicket*

**Offer:** I've always had poachers. A hungry man takes a deer and I look the other way. These aren't hungry men. They have new bows and good boots, and they take forty pelts a week out of Snare Hollow. Someone is buying. Put ten of them down and we'll see who comes to complain.
**Tracker:** Woodland Poachers slain 0/10
**Progress:** They shoot first and they cut deep up close.
**Complete:** Ten. And nobody's come to complain, which tells me they're more afraid of their buyer than of us. Here. Forester's issue.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Lodge-Keeper's Wristwraps* (cloth wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Int +3 Spi) · *Trail-Cutter's Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Agi +4 Sta) · *Thicket's Chain Vambraces* (mail wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Str +4 Sta)

### RW-06 · Snares and Spoils
*Level 7 · Requires RW-05 · Forester Thicket → Forester Thicket*

**Offer:** The pelts are bundled up in the Hollow waiting for a cart. Those are the Concord's deer and the foresters' winter pay. Bring back eight bundles. The trappers who made the kill won't be far from them, and watch where you put your feet.
**Tracker:** Bundles of Stolen Pelts 0/8
**Progress:** Their snares will hold you fast for a few breaths. That's all a trapper needs.
**Complete:** Eight bundles. That's every forester paid through midwinter. This satchel's yours. You've been carrying our work in your arms long enough.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · *Forester's Game Bag* (8-slot bag)
**Notes:** Bundles are world objects in Snare Hollow (12 spawn points), each beside 1–2 Poacher Trappers.

### RW-07 · [MC1] Paid in Charcoal
*Level 7 · Requires RW-05 · Forester Thicket → Forester Thicket*

**Offer:** One of the trappers talked. Their fence is a man named Odo Ketch, in a cabin at the bottom of the Hollow. He buys every pelt and keeps a tally of who's owed what. Kill him and bring me that tally. I want to know what he pays with.
**Tracker:** Odo Ketch slain 0/1 · Poachers' Tally 0/1
**Progress:** The cabin with the drying racks. You'll smell it before you see it.
**Complete:** He doesn't pay in coin at all. Look. "Forty pelts, two sacks charcoal." Charcoal. And they were glad to get it. What kind of charcoal is worth more than silver to a poacher?
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Fence's Silk Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Pelt-Trader's Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Hollow-Breaker Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)
**Notes:** Odo Ketch: level 8 humanoid, non-elite, 2 Trapper guards, 5-minute respawn. *Poachers' Tally* is looted from the *Tally-Desk* in his cabin.

### RW-09 · [MC1] What the Kilns Burn
*Level 8 · Requires RW-07 · Old Peg Embery → Old Peg Embery*

**Offer:** Sixty years I've burned charcoal in this wood. You stack it, you turf it, you let it smoulder nine days. Those folk in the clearings turn out a stack in a night, and it comes out red at the heart and never goes grey. That isn't charcoal. Fetch me six lumps off them and I'll tell you what it is.
**Tracker:** Cinder-Charcoal 0/6
**Progress:** Don't hold it long. It doesn't cool.
**Complete:** Feel that. A day out of the kiln and it'd still light your pipe. I've heard of this from my gran. Hollow-fire. It burns without using itself up, and it gets into whoever warms their hands at it. That's what your poachers were paid in. They can't do without it now. Take my mitts. You'll be handling more of it.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · *Embery's Kiln-Mitts* (leather hands, ilvl 13, +4 Agi +3 Sta, +5 Fire resistance)
**Notes:** Cinder-Charcoal drops from Ashhand Charcoaler (70%). Old Peg is at the Charcoal-Burners' Camp.

### RW-10 · Rootgnawers

**Offer:** First beetles, now Burrowkin. They've dug in under the fallen giants on the north side and they're gnawing through the roots of the standing ones. A three-hundred-year oak came down yesterday with no wind at all. Ten of them. Those oaks are a hundred years of barrels.

**Complete:** That's saved the north stand. These were made for a customer who never paid. His loss.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · choose 1: *Cooper's Apprentice Breeches* (cloth legs, ilvl 13, +7 Int +6 Spi) · *Bark-Stripper's Leggings* (leather legs, ilvl 13, +7 Agi +9 Sta) · *Root-Guard Chain Leggings* (mail legs, ilvl 13, +7 Str +9 Sta)

### RW-11 · The Woodcutter's Axe
*Level 8 · Requires RW-08 · Woodcutter Hewer → Woodcutter Hewer*

**Offer:** It's a foolish thing to ask. My grandfather's axe. I set it down by a stump at the Burrows, and when I turned round one of those candle-headed rats was dragging it down a hole. Three generations of Hewers felled with that axe. I'd give a lot to have it back.
**Tracker:** Hewer's Heirloom Axe 0/1
**Progress:** One of the gnawers has it. They don't let go of a shiny thing.
**Complete:** That's her. Look, there's my grandfather's notch, and my father's. I haven't much, but take one of these. My mother made them, for luck in the wood.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 2s 50c · choose 1: *Hewer's Whetstone Pendant* (neck, ilvl 13, +2 Str +2 Sta) · *Hewer's Leaf-Charm* (neck, ilvl 13, +2 Int +2 Spi)
**Notes:** Hewer's Heirloom Axe drops from Burrowkin Rootgnawer (35%).

### RW-12 · The Mossbacks Wake
*Level 8 · Requires — · Hedgewarden Sorrel → Hedgewarden Sorrel*

**Offer:** The old ones in the Mossback Stand have slept since before the first cooper. The smoke has woken them, and they have woken in pain. They can't tell a forester from a fire-setter. I won't have them killed. Beat them down until the rage leaves them, and bring me the heartknots they shed. With those I can sing the rest back to sleep.
**Tracker:** Mossback Treants subdued 0/8 · Heartknots 0/5
**Progress:** My own craft slides off their bark. Yours may do better. Fire certainly will, though I'd rather you didn't.
**Complete:** Five heartknots. That's enough for the song. They'll sleep by nightfall, and they'll wake in a better temper in a hundred years. Take my spare sickle. This wood gives freely to anyone who cuts with care.
**Rewards:** 725 XP · 6s · *Sorrel's Herb Sickle* (tool) and 10 *Dewleaf*
**Notes:** At 10% health a Mossback Treant stops fighting, roots itself and becomes non-hostile for 2 minutes; this gives "subdued" credit and it can then be looted. Heartknot drops at 65%. Treants resist Nature 50% and are weak to Fire.

### RW-13 · Wisps of the Kiln
*Level 9 · Requires RW-09 · Old Peg Embery → Old Peg Embery*

**Offer:** There's sparks come loose from those kilns. They've got a sort of life in them, and they drift through the dry leaves like they're looking for something. One good gust and the whole Russetwood goes up. Put out eight of them. They flicker about, so don't waste your swing on where they were.
**Tracker:** Kilnfire Wisps slain 0/8
**Progress:** You're still seeing lights in the trees? Then you're not done.
**Complete:** The wood's dark again, the way it should be at night. I had a nephew in the militia once. He left this lot with me.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 6s · choose 1: *Ember-Watcher's Mantle* (cloth shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Int +5 Spi) · *Charcoal-Burner's Spaulders* (leather shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Agi +7 Sta) · *Ash-Dusted Pauldrons* (mail shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Str +7 Sta)

### RW-14 · [MC1] Fire in the Russetwood
*Level 9 · Requires RW-07 and RW-09 · Forest-Reeve Birch → Forest-Reeve Birch*

**Offer:** They've fired the wood. The whole length of the north ride is burning, and there are kiln tenders walking along it feeding the flames. I've got every forester on the fire-line. Take these skins of water. Six fires out and six tenders dead, or there's no Russetwood by morning.
**Tracker:** Fires doused 0/6 · Kiln Tenders slain 0/6
**Progress:** It's still spreading. Go!
**Complete:** It's held at the stream. We lost the ride, but we kept the wood. Forty years I've been Reeve here and I never thought I'd see someone burn it on purpose. You'll take something from the lodge stores, and I won't hear otherwise.
**Rewards:** 825 XP · 7s · choose 1: *Leaf-Fall Robe* (cloth chest, ilvl 14, +8 Int +6 Spi) · *Fire-Ride Jerkin* (leather chest, ilvl 14, +8 Agi +9 Sta) · *Forest-Reeve's Hauberk* (mail chest, ilvl 14, +8 Str +9 Sta)
**Notes:** *Forester's Waterskin* (unlimited use, 10-yard range) is given at accept. 12 *Ride Fire* objects, 45-second respawn. Kiln Tenders cast Stoke to enrage a nearby ally.

### RW-15 · Owl Pellets

**Offer:** I'm aware of how this sounds. The horned owls of the Burning Ride eat things that eat the kiln-ash, and what they cough up afterward is the purest binding agent I have ever worked with. Six pellets. They hunt silently, so the first you'll know of one is its claws.

### RW-16 · [MC1] The Kilnmaster's Ledger
*Level 9 · Requires RW-14 · Forest-Reeve Birch → Forest-Reeve Birch*

**Offer:** All of it comes from one place. The Great Kiln, in the old quarry. A brickworks that hasn't made a brick in ten years and now smokes day and night. A works that size keeps accounts. There's a counting-shed on the quarry rim. Get in quietly, take the ledger, and get out. I want to know who they're selling to.
**Tracker:** Slip into the Great Kiln · Kilnmaster's Ledger taken from the counting-shed 0/1
**Progress:** The shed stands apart, up on the rim. Go round by the spoil heaps.
**Complete:** Cartloads. Hundreds of cartloads of the stuff, all sent north, all to one buyer, signed for as "C.A." And the kilnmaster is Bertram Soot. He sold me charcoal for my own hearth for fifteen years.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 7s
**Notes:** The spoil-heap path passes 4 Great Kiln Guards instead of the 10 on the cart road. *Kilnmaster's Ledger* is a desk object. Kilnmaster Soot is not present until RW-17 is accepted.

### RW-17 · [MC1] Ashes to Ashes
*Level 10 · Requires RW-16 · Forest-Reeve Birch → Forest-Reeve Birch*

**Offer:** While that kiln burns, the fire comes back. Bertram Soot is on the kiln floor. Kill him and rake out the firebeds. Mind the dampers. When he opens them the flame runs across the floor in lines, and it does not care who it catches. Take two good friends.
**Tracker:** Kilnmaster Bertram Soot slain 0/1
**Progress:** The kiln floor, down in the quarry. You'll know him by the rake.
**Complete:** The smoke's stopped. First clear sky over the quarry in a year. What did he say at the end? ..."You can't put it out. It was never lit." I don't know what that means, and I don't like not knowing. Choose something. The wood owes you.
**Rewards:** 1,125 XP · 15s · choose 1: *Soot's Coal-Rake* (2H mace, ilvl 15, speed 3.5, +8 Str +10 Sta) · *Kiln-Door Shield* (shield, ilvl 15, +4 Str +6 Sta) · *Copperleaf Staff* (staff, ilvl 15, speed 3.0, +8 Int +7 Spi +2 Sta) · *Forester's Hand-Axe* (1H axe, ilvl 15, speed 2.3, +4 Str +5 Sta)
**Notes:** Elite, level 10, group 2–3. Open the Dampers (patterned floor flame), Rake Coals (frontal cone), calls 2 Kiln Tenders at 50%. Soot yells the "never lit" line on death.
