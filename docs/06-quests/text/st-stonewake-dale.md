# Quest Text — Stonewake Dale (ST-01 to ST-18)

Format and global rules are in the [index](00-quest-text-index.md). This zone mirrors Brightmead quest for quest, with the same XP and coin.

### ST-01 · A Dale Welcome
*Level 1 · Requires — · (granted at character creation) → Steadwife Marit Sunbank*

**Offer:** You have come down into Stonewake Dale for the spring work, when the lambs come, the barley goes in and every steading is short of hands. Sunbank Steading has a place at its hearth for you. Steadwife Marit Sunbank keeps the longhouse at the foot of the terraces, and she has had the kettle on since she saw you on the road.
**Tracker:** Speak to Steadwife Sunbank at the longhouse
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** There you are, and in time for breakfast. Sit. Eat first, we'll talk after. I'm Marit, and this is Sunbank: forty terraces, two hundred ewes and not nearly enough of us. Nobody goes hungry at this hearth and nobody stands idle at it. You'll do well here.
**Rewards:** 40 XP
**Notes:** Auto-accepted on first login.

### ST-02 · Buzzards over the Paddock
*Level 1 · Requires ST-01 · Steadwife Sunbank → Steadwife Sunbank*

**Offer:** The lambs came early this year, and the buzzards came with them. They sit on the terrace walls above the lambing paddock and wait for a ewe to look the other way. I've lost four lambs this week. Go up along the lower terraces and thin them out before I lose a fifth.
**Tracker:** Highland Buzzards slain 0/8
**Progress:** The lower terraces, straight up from the paddock. You'll see them on the walls.
**Complete:** That's a quieter sky. The ewes have noticed, even if the lambs haven't. Here, these were my son's before his hands outgrew them. Mornings are still cold for bare fingers.
**Rewards:** 80 XP · 25c · choose 1: *Sunbank Wool Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 3) · *Sunbank Hide Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 3) · *Sunbank Ringed Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 3)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### ST-03 · A Summons from Your Trainer
*Level 2 · Requires ST-01 and level 2 · Steadwife Sunbank → class trainer*

**Offer:** There's someone asking after you. Fighters go to Dale-Sergeant Shieldwall in the practice yard, clerics to Hearth-mother Emberkin at the hearth-nook, druids to Stonesinger Furrow up on the terrace wall. Go and be taught. The Dale is gentle, but it isn't soft.
**Tracker:** Speak to your class trainer
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** So Marit has let you out of the kitchen. Good. Stand there and show me what you know. The first thing I teach is free. After that it's coin, same as seed-barley.
**Rewards:** 40 XP · the first trainable ability is free (trainer cost waived once)
**Notes:** Offered only from level 2, when the first trainer rank can be bought (Battle Cry, Litany of Resolve or Gift of the Grove, rank 1); that rank is the one the reward makes free. Turn-in NPC by class: Dale-Sergeant Hakon Shieldwall (Fighter), Hearth-mother Sigrid Emberkin (Cleric), Stonesinger Arne Furrow (Druid).

### ST-04 · Rock-Eaters at the Wall
*Level 2 · Requires ST-02 · Quarrier Odd Capstone → Quarrier Capstone*

**Offer:** I cut stone up at Greywall and I build it into terrace walls down here, and this spring something is eating both. Gravelkin. Rock-eaters. They've come up out of those new sinkholes in the low field and they take a dressed stone off my sledge like it was a bun off a tray. Go down to the Sinks and show them the Dale's walls are not for supper.
**Tracker:** Gravelkin Sinkdwellers slain 0/8
**Progress:** You can hear them at it. A wall shouldn't crunch.
**Complete:** Quiet down there? Good. I can lay a course without counting my stones after. Here, the Steadwife baked this morning and I begged a few loaves for you.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 50c · 5 *Sunbank Barley Bread* (food, restores 61 health over 18 s)

### ST-05 · Dale Pasties
*Level 2 · Requires ST-02 · Cook Hroar Panhandle → Cook Panhandle*

**Offer:** A Dale pasty is goat, onion and a buzzard egg to bind it, and it'll sit in a shepherd's pocket for three days and be the better for it. Somebody's goats got loose three winters back and now there's a hundred of the wild brutes on the terraces, eating the barley down to the wall. Fetch me six cuts of goat. I've eggs and onions enough, and I'll teach you the folding.
**Tracker:** Goat Meat 0/6
**Progress:** Don't stand with your back to a drop. They butt.
**Complete:** Good, clean cuts. Now watch. You crimp it left over right. Right over left is how they do it in Ridgefold, and we don't speak of Ridgefold. Take these with you.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 25c · *Recipe: Dale Pasty* (Cooking 1) · 5 *Dale Pasty*
**Notes:** Goat Meat drops from Feral Billy (75%).

### ST-06 · Blunt Axes
*Level 2 · Requires ST-02 · Sawyer Ketil Larchwood → Sawyer Larchwood*

**Offer:** We fell larch up in the Hangers and saw it here in the pit, and a saw wants a whetstone every hour. I had eight. I have none. The rock-eaters came in the night and took every one, and I'd swear they ate the grindstone where it stood. My lads are felling with axes as blunt as spoons. Get my whetstones back from the Sinks, whatever's left of them.
**Tracker:** Stolen Whetstones 0/8
**Progress:** Eight whetstones. They'll have tooth-marks, I expect.
**Complete:** Gnawed at the corners, every one, but they'll put an edge on. Take this sack. It carried wood-chips, and you'll soon be carrying more than that.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 50c · *Larchwood's Chip-Sack* (6-slot bag)
**Notes:** Stolen Whetstone drops from Gravelkin Sinkdweller and Bonechewer (70%).

### ST-07 · [MC1] Stolen Stores
*Level 3 · Requires ST-04 · Steadwife Sunbank → Steadwife Sunbank*

**Offer:** Walls and whetstones are one thing. This is another. The spring stores are gone from the tithe barn: the seed-barley, the salt, the lamp-oil for lambing nights, taken a crate at a time through a hole in the floor. Without them there's no sowing, and without sowing the Dale eats thin by autumn. The crates are somewhere down in the Sinks. Bring back what you can carry.
**Tracker:** Crates of Stolen Stores recovered 0/6
**Progress:** Every crate matters, <name>. That barley is next year's bread and next year's ale.
**Complete:** All six, and the seed still dry. Bless you. But you say they were stacked? Neatly? Gravelkin eat rock. They don't keep a pantry. And what's this in the bottom of the crate... forge-slag, with a hand pressed into it while it was soft. That never came out of a Dale hearth. Somebody is paying them to do this.
**Rewards:** 200 XP · 1s · choose 1: *Sunbank Hatchet* (1H axe, ilvl 5, speed 2.3) · *Hearth-Nook Mace* (1H mace, ilvl 5, speed 2.4) · *Terrace-Walker's Staff* (staff, ilvl 5, speed 3.0)
**Notes:** Crates of Stolen Stores are world objects in the second gallery of the Sinks (10 spawn points). Each stands beside a *Hand-Marked Slag Lump* (scenery, examine text: "A lump of forge-slag. A hand was pressed into it while it was soft.").

### ST-08 · Down to Hopstead
*Level 3 · Requires ST-07 · Steadwife Sunbank → Innkeeper Solveig Dryboot*

**Offer:** The Dale-Reeve should hear about that slag, and you should have a bed nearer the work. Take my tally of the stores down to Solveig Dryboot at the Dry Boot in Hopstead. She keeps the Moot's accounts as well as its ale, and she'll see you housed. Tell her the tithe barn is six crates to the good and no thanks to its floor.
**Tracker:** Deliver the Steadwife's Tally to Innkeeper Dryboot in Hopstead
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Marit's tally, and in her own hand. Six crates back out of a hole in the ground? Well, well. Wipe your feet and come in. There's a bed over the kitchen where it's warm, and the Reeve drinks here most evenings, so you'll not have far to carry your news.
**Rewards:** 100 XP · 50c
**Notes:** *Steadwife's Tally* is given at accept. Sets up the Hopstead inn as a bind point.

### ST-09 · Baskets on the Bank
*Level 4 · Requires ST-08 · Apothecary Liv Hedgerow → Apothecary Hedgerow*

**Offer:** Best herb country on Khazrun, and my gatherers won't go near it. They were cutting on the banks above the hop-yards, under the larches, when a bear came down for a look at them. A young one, and more curious than cross, but they dropped six full baskets and ran. That's a week of picking. Fetch the baskets down, would you? I'll have something warm made for you by the time you're back.
**Tracker:** Herb Baskets recovered 0/6
**Progress:** Along the top of the hop-yards, where the bank meets the trees. Wicker baskets, with my mark on the lid.
**Complete:** All six, and hardly a leaf bruised. You've a lighter foot than my gatherers. Here, my mother wove this for cutting in the wet. It's warm, and it sheds rain.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 75c · *Hedgerow's Gathering Cloak* (back, ilvl 9, +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Herb Baskets are world objects on the herb banks along the upper edge of the Hop-Yards (8 spawn points).

### ST-10 · Beetles in the Bines
*Level 4 · Requires ST-08 · Hop-grower Thora Bine → Hop-grower Bine*

**Offer:** No hops, no ale. No ale, no Dale. The bines are just coming up the poles, and the blight beetles are coming up after them. One beetle will strip a pole in a night, and what it doesn't eat it sprays. Ten of them, please, before there's nothing left to string. And don't let them spray you. It takes the strength out of your knees.
**Tracker:** Hop-Blight Beetles slain 0/10
**Progress:** If you feel weak at the knees, that's the spray. Walk it off.
**Complete:** Ten fewer, and the bines will outgrow the rest. These boots have walked every row in this yard. You'll want them in the mud.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 1s 50c · choose 1: *Hop-Picker's Slippers* (cloth feet, ilvl 6) · *Bine-Walker Boots* (leather feet, ilvl 6) · *Hopstead Chain Boots* (mail feet, ilvl 6)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### ST-11 · [MC1] Empty Folds
*Level 4 · Requires ST-08 · Dale-Reeve Garrik Stonewake → Shepherdess Bruna Ramsbell*

**Offer:** I'm the one the Dale-Moot voted to do the worrying, and Marit Sunbank has just sent me something new to worry about. Paid rock-eaters. And this morning Bruna Ramsbell sends down from Ridgefold that the High Fold is standing empty, sixty head gone in a night. I can't leave the Moot. Go up the Ridge Track, look at that fold with your own eyes, and hear what Bruna saw.
**Tracker:** Investigate the High Fold · Speak to Shepherdess Ramsbell
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** You've seen it, then. Gate off its hinges and not a ewe inside. I saw them go, <name>. Men with ropes and cudgels and big rough dogs, driving my flock up toward the bothies as bold as daylight. They're not Dalefolk. I know every thief in this valley by name, and I've fed most of them.
**Rewards:** 135 XP
**Notes:** "Investigate" completes on entering the High Fold (area trigger). Ramsbell is at the Ridgefold sheep-fold.

### ST-12 · [MC1] Rustlers on the Ridge
*Level 4 · Requires ST-11 · Shepherdess Ramsbell → Shepherdess Ramsbell*

**Offer:** They've made themselves at home in my bothies on the High Pastures, rustlers below and the hound-masters above. I can't get my flock back past them and neither can the rangers. Thin them out. And mind the ones with the whistles. One note and every dog and cudgel on the hill comes running.
**Tracker:** Dale Rustlers slain 0/8 · Rustler Hound-Masters slain 0/4
**Progress:** Still too many ropes on that hill.
**Complete:** That'll make the rest think. I wove this at lambing with one hand, so it isn't my best, but it's yours.
**Rewards:** 340 XP · 2s · choose 1: *Ramsbell's Wool Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 6) · *Shepherd's Leather Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 6) · *Fold-Guard Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 6)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### ST-13 · The Old Brewery
*Level 4 · Requires ST-08 · Brewmaster Eski Maltby → Brewmaster Maltby*

**Offer:** My grandfather's brewhouse has stood shut for twenty years, and this spring I mean to open it. Only the old vats were never emptied, and what's in them has had two decades to think about things. It has got out. And it has swallowed the copper fittings off every pipe in the place. Five of them, and I can pipe the new coppers. I'll stand you a share of the first cask.
**Tracker:** Copper Fittings 0/5
**Progress:** What they splash on you is sour and it sticks. Keep moving and it can't slow you much.
**Complete:** Fittings, and still sound! We'll be brewing by midsummer. Give me till... no, here, take some of the Dry Boot's best. A promise is poor drinking.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 1s · 5 *Hopstead Ale* (drink, restores 151 mana over 18 s)
**Notes:** Copper Fitting drops from Fermented Ooze (70%).

### ST-14 · Bears in the Adit
*Level 5 · Requires ST-09 · Smith Torgil Ploughshare → Smith Ploughshare*

**Offer:** Every ploughshare in this valley started as copper and tin out of the Daleside Adit, and I've had neither for a month. Shale bears wintered in the upper drift and they've not moved on. Liv's gatherers met the cubs. My miners met the mothers. Clear eight of the grown ones out of the drift and off the spoil banks, and I'll give you a knife and a pick besides. Anyone working these hills should have both.
**Tracker:** Shale Bears slain 0/8
**Progress:** A bear in a doorway is still a bear. Draw it out into the light.
**Complete:** Eight bears, and you can still walk. The crew goes back in tomorrow. Here, as promised. The knife's for hides and the pick's for copper. You'll find plenty of both up there.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Ploughshare's Skinning Knife* (tool) and *Hopstead Mining Pick* (tool)

### ST-15 · [MC1] The Peddler's Coin
*Level 5 · Requires ST-12 · Dale-Reeve Garrik Stonewake → Dale-Reeve Stonewake*

**Offer:** Bruna says those rustlers were paid before they ever touched a sheep. We had a peddler through at shearing last year, giving the herd-lads coins with a hand burned into them. I took it for a trinket. I want to see what the rustlers carry in their purses. Six coins, from the ropers or the whistlers, I don't care which.
**Tracker:** Peddler's Coins 0/6
**Progress:** Bring me the coin. I'll know that mark if I see it again.
**Complete:** There it is. A hand, burned in. The same hand that's pressed into the slag Marit found in her crates. One purse is paying for my sheep to walk and my fields to fall in, <name>, and I'd dearly like to know what else it's buying.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s 50c · choose 1: *Dale-Reeve's Cloth Cuffs* (cloth wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Int +1 Spi) · *Ridge-Watch Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Agi +1 Sta) · *Hopstead Chain Bracers* (mail wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Str +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Peddler's Coin drops from Dale Rustler and Rustler Hound-Master (70%).

### ST-16 · [MC1] Paid in Slag
*Level 5 · Requires ST-15 · Dale-Reeve Stonewake → Dale-Reeve Stonewake*

**Offer:** One of the rustlers talked, for a hot meal and a promise. The peddler pays the rock-eaters too, through their chief. Gnarr, the big one, at the bottom of the deepest sink, with a slate hung round his neck. My ranger swears he saw a stranger hand it to him. Kill Gnarr and bring me that slate. He keeps two of his biggest by him and he goes mad when he's nearly done. Take a friend if you have one.
**Tracker:** Chief Gnarr slain 0/1
**Progress:** The Deep Sink, down the ramp from the gallery where the crates were. He's the one that doesn't run.
**Complete:** He can't read it. He doesn't need to. It's tally marks: so many fields undermined, so many folds emptied, so many lumps of slag paid. And one line at the bottom with no payment against it yet. A wall, drawn across a valley. That's the dam, <name>. Somebody means to pay for the dam. Choose something from the Moot's store. You've earned better than we can give.
**Rewards:** 525 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Gnarr's Jawbone Club* (1H mace, ilvl 10, speed 2.4, +3 Str +2 Sta) · *Dale-Moot Felling Axe* (2H axe, ilvl 10, speed 3.4, +6 Str +5 Sta) · *Dalefolk Crook-Staff* (staff, ilvl 10, speed 3.0, +5 Int +4 Spi +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Suggested players: 2. Gnarr has 2 Bonechewer guards and frenzies at 30% health. He drops *Payroll-Slate* (quest item for ST-18) for every player on ST-16.

### ST-17 · Old Bitterhorn
*Level 5 · Requires ST-12 · Shepherdess Ramsbell → Shepherdess Ramsbell*

**Offer:** There's a ram on the ridge top that was never anyone's. Old Bitterhorn. Twice the size of mine, horns like a cart axle, and he's killed two dogs and broken a shepherd's leg. With the folds standing open he's leading off what ewes the rustlers left me. I'd admire him if he wasn't ruining me. Bring him down, and bring a friend, because he won't run.
**Tracker:** Old Bitterhorn slain 0/1
**Progress:** Top of the ridge, above the last bothy. Listen for the ewes.
**Complete:** Well. I'll miss him, the old devil. We'll have the hide tanned. These were made from his sire, years back. Seems right they go to whoever ended the line.
**Rewards:** 525 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Bitterhorn Mantle* (leather shoulders, ilvl 10, +4 Agi +5 Sta) · *Ram's-Brow Buckler* (shield, ilvl 10, +2 Str +3 Sta) · *Horn-Toggle Charm* (held off-hand, ilvl 10, +3 Int +2 Spi)
**Notes:** Elite, level 5. Suggested players: 2. 4 ewes follow him and are not hostile. The off-hand option was added so druids have a choice.

### ST-18 · [MC1] Word to the Mountain
*Level 5 · Requires ST-16 · Dale-Reeve Stonewake → Forgemaster Durgan Emberhand (Anvilrest) or Hallwarden Lampwright (Lanternhold)*

**Offer:** That slate has the dam on it, and the dam is mountain work. I'll not carry this to Dammaster Sluicewright myself. She and I have not exchanged a civil word in eleven years, and she'd say a Dale farmer had drawn it to frighten her. Take it to the mountain folk who will listen. Up through Bouldergate to Forgemaster Emberhand at Anvilrest, where that slag was poured. Or through the Daleside Adit to Hallwarden Lampwright at Lanternhold in the Deeps. Either way, put that hand in front of someone with the sense to fear it.
**Tracker:** Carry the Payroll-Slate to Forgemaster Emberhand in Embercrag Hollow or Hallwarden Lampwright in the Stoneheart Deeps
**Progress:** —
**Complete (Emberhand):** Give it here. ...That mark again. We have found it stamped into iron up here, and I had hoped it went no further than this hollow. Rock-eaters paid in slag, and the dam on the tally. Come in out of the wind, <name>. You have walked into the cold half of the same trouble.
**Complete (Lampwright):** An ash hand. I have seen that scratched on the lamp-posts of the lower road, always where a lamp had been put out. And the Dale has it on a slate, with the dam drawn beneath. Thank you for bringing this. I wish you had brought better news.
**Rewards:** 175 XP · 1s
**Notes:** *Payroll-Slate* is the item looted in ST-16. Turning in to Emberhand unlocks EC-01; to Lampwright unlocks SD-01. Either satisfies the Act I step of [Embers of the Brand](../main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md); the other zone's quests remain available as side content.
