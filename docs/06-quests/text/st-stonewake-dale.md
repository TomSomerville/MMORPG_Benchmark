### ST-01 · [MC1] The Dale-Reeve's Burden
*Level 5 · Requires EC-18 or BM-18, or none (see notes) · Dale-Reeve Garrik Stonewake → Dale-Reeve Stonewake*

**Offer:** You're in Hopstead, and I'm the one the Dale-Moot voted to do the worrying. We grow the barley, we brew the ale, we mind the sheep. The mountain folk built their dam over our heads and told us it was for everyone's good. I've sinkholes in my fields and rustlers on my ridge. If you've more trouble for me, set it on the table.
**Tracker:** Present the Branded Ingot to Dale-Reeve Stonewake
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Slagborn, armed by an outsider. And that same hand on the coins the peddler gave our lads. Well. The Dale looks after its own, and it seems you're one of us for the season. Ranger Fellhammer keeps the ridge. Go and make yourself useful to him.
**Rewards:** 175 XP
**Notes:** If the player turned in EC-18 to Stonewake, this quest is offered and completed in the same conversation. Offered directly at level 5 to anyone else; the tracker then reads "Speak to Dale-Reeve Stonewake".

### ST-02 · Billies in the Barley
*Level 5 · Requires — · Hop-grower Thora Bine → Hop-grower Bine*

**Offer:** Somebody's goats got loose three winters back and nobody owned up to it. Now there's a hundred of the wild brutes on the terraces, eating the barley down to the wall. A terrace takes a lifetime to build and a billy a week to ruin. Ten of them, please, and my thanks.
**Tracker:** Feral Billies slain 0/10
**Progress:** Don't stand with your back to a drop. They butt.
**Complete:** That's the lower terraces safe till harvest. My mother wove this. It's warm, and it sheds rain.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Bine's Terrace Cloak* (back, ilvl 10, +3 Sta +3 Spi)

### ST-03 · Bears in the Bothy
*Level 6 · Requires ST-01 · Ranger Odrin Fellhammer → Ranger Fellhammer*

**Offer:** The shepherds keep stone bothies up on the High Pastures to sleep in at lambing. Shale bears have moved into four of them. A shepherd opened a door last week and I'm told he came down the hill faster than a dwarf has ever moved. Clear eight bears out.
**Tracker:** Shale Bears slain 0/8
**Progress:** A bear in a doorway is still a bear. Draw it outside.
**Complete:** Four bothies swept and aired. Here, ranger's issue. You'll want them on the scree.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Bothy-Keeper's Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 11, +4 Int +4 Spi) · *Ridge-Ranger's Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 11, +4 Agi +6 Sta) · *Fellhammer's Chain Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 11, +4 Str +6 Sta)

### ST-04 · Dale Pasties
*Level 6 · Requires — · Cook Hroar Panhandle → Cook Panhandle*

**Offer:** A Dale pasty is goat, onion and a buzzard egg to bind it, and it'll sit in a shepherd's pocket for three days and be the better for it. Mountain folk won't eat them. More for us. Fetch six cuts of goat and four eggs and I'll teach you the folding.
**Tracker:** Goat Meat 0/6 · Buzzard Eggs 0/4
**Progress:** Goat and egg. I've the onions.
**Complete:** Good. Now you crimp it left over right. Right over left is how they do it in Ridgefold, and we don't speak of Ridgefold. Take these with you.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 1s 50c · *Recipe: Dale Pasty* (Cooking 25) · 5 *Dale Pasty*
**Notes:** Goat Meat drops from Feral Billy (75%). Buzzard Egg drops from Highland Buzzard (60%).

### ST-05 · [MC1] The Sinks
*Level 6 · Requires ST-01 · Ranger Fellhammer → Ranger Fellhammer*

**Offer:** The fields below Ridgefold are falling in. Sinkholes, a new one every week, and when you look down them there are Gravelkin looking back. Rock-eaters never came this far down the Dale. Something has driven them, or sent them. Go into the Sinks and kill ten.
**Tracker:** Gravelkin Sinkdwellers slain 0/10
**Progress:** The ground's rotten down there. Stay on stone.
**Complete:** Ten, and you say they had stores down there? Sacks and barrels? Gravelkin eat rock. They don't keep a pantry. Somebody's provisioning them.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Dalefolk Wristwraps* (cloth wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Int +3 Spi) · *Sink-Climber's Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Agi +4 Sta) · *Ridgefold Chain Vambraces* (mail wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Str +4 Sta)

### ST-06 · Stolen Stores
*Level 7 · Requires ST-05 · Innkeeper Solveig Dryboot → Innkeeper Dryboot*

**Offer:** Those sacks and barrels in the Sinks are mine. Well, the Moot's. Winter stores for the whole of Hopstead, taken out of the tithe barn a crate at a time. The bonechewers have them. Bring back eight crates and I'll see you never pay for a bed here again.
**Tracker:** Crates of Stolen Stores 0/8
**Progress:** They go mad when they're nearly dead. Finish them fast.
**Complete:** Eight crates. Some gnawed, but the Dale will eat. Here. A drover left it behind, and you've more to carry than he ever did.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · *Drover's Pack* (8-slot bag)
**Notes:** Crates are world objects in the lower Sinks (12 spawn points), each beside 1–2 Gravelkin Bonechewers.

### ST-07 · [MC1] Paid in Slag
*Level 7 · Requires ST-05 · Ranger Fellhammer → Ranger Fellhammer*

**Offer:** They have a chief down there. Gnarr, the big one, with a slate hung round his neck. My scout swears he saw a dwarf handing it to him. Kill Gnarr and bring me that slate. I'd like to know what a Gravelkin needs to read.
**Tracker:** Chief Gnarr slain 0/1 · Payroll-Slate 0/1
**Progress:** Bottom of the deepest sink. He's the one that doesn't run.
**Complete:** He can't read it. He doesn't need to. It's tally marks: so many fields undermined, so many lumps of slag paid. Slag, Reeve help us. They're being fed forge-slag as wages, and it's marked with that hand.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Slate-Reader's Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Sinkdweller-Hide Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Gnarr's Tooth Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)
**Notes:** Chief Gnarr: level 8 humanoid, non-elite, 2 Bonechewer guards, 5-minute respawn. He drops the *Payroll-Slate* (100%).

### ST-09 · [MC1] Powder in the Millrace
*Level 8 · Requires ST-07 · Tinker Dagny Sparkwright → Tinker Sparkwright*

**Offer:** I maintain the millrace, the stone channel that brings dam-water down to the wheels. I found this wedged in a culvert this morning. It's a charge. Cinder-packed, with a slow fuse that's meant to be lit from somewhere else. There'll be more. Six culverts, six charges, I'd bet my bench on it. Pull them out. Gently.
**Tracker:** Cinder-Charges removed from culverts 0/6
**Progress:** Don't drop one. Don't warm one. Don't look at one too hard.
**Complete:** Six. Set them down there. Slowly. That's Slagborn work, and whoever placed them knew the race better than I do. If these had gone, every mill in the Dale would have stood dry. Here, I wear these for exactly this. I've a second pair.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · *Sparkwright's Insulated Mitts* (leather hands, ilvl 13, +4 Agi +3 Sta, +5 Fire resistance)
**Notes:** 6 *Millrace Culvert* objects along the race, per-player state; each takes a 4-second channel. A Slagborn Sapper spawns and attacks on 2 of the 6 (chosen at random per player).

### ST-10 · Hound-Masters

**Offer:** Bruna's rustlers have men running hounds for them, big rough brutes trained to cut sheep out of a flock. They cut rangers out of a patrol just as well. Kill ten of the hound-masters. Kill the man first and the dogs lose heart. Let him whistle and they lose their minds.
**Tracker:** Rustler Hound-Masters slain 0/10

### ST-11 · The Shepherdess's Bell
*Level 8 · Requires ST-08 · Shepherdess Ramsbell → Shepherdess Ramsbell*

**Offer:** They took my lead-ram, and he wore the Ramsbell. My family is named for that bell. It's led our flock down this ridge for nine generations. The ram's mutton by now, I know that. But one of those hound-masters has the bell on his belt. I heard it. I'd know its voice anywhere.
**Tracker:** The Ramsbell 0/1
**Progress:** Listen for it. Low, with a crack in the note.
**Complete:** There's the crack in the note. My great-grandmother dropped it on the dam road. Thank you. It'll go on the new lead-ram tomorrow. I had this made from a chip of the old clapper. Wear it for luck.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 2s 50c · *Ramsbell Charm* (neck, ilvl 13, +2 Spi +2 Sta)
**Notes:** The Ramsbell drops from Rustler Hound-Master (35%).

### ST-12 · The Old Brewery
*Level 8 · Requires — · Brewmaster Eski Maltby → Brewmaster Maltby*

**Offer:** My grandfather's brewhouse has stood shut for twenty years, and I mean to open it. Only the old vats were never emptied, and what's in them has had two decades to think about things. It has got out. Kill eight of the oozes and bring me five copper fittings they've swallowed. I need those to pipe the new coppers.
**Tracker:** Fermented Oozes slain 0/8 · Copper Fittings 0/5
**Progress:** They split in two the first time you cut them. Don't be alarmed. Be thorough.
**Complete:** Fittings, and still sound! We'll be brewing by midwinter. Have the first of the new season's, and take these. The old brewer kept his tools in good order.
**Rewards:** 725 XP · 6s · *Maltby's Skinning Knife* (tool), 10 *Tin Ore* and 5 *Hopstead Ale* (drink, restores 294 mana over 21 s)
**Notes:** Copper Fitting drops from Fermented Ooze (70%). Split halves give kill credit only for the original.

### ST-13 · Slag Sprites
*Level 9 · Requires ST-09 · Dammaster Helga Sluicewright → Dammaster Sluicewright*

**Offer:** You're the one Sparkwright sent. I'm Sluicewright. The dam is mine. Below the forge-vents there's a gully where the slag runs off, and it's started getting up and walking. Sprites, spitting hot slag at my maintenance crews. I've a dam to inspect for charges and nobody to do it while those are loose. Eight of them.
**Tracker:** Loose Slag Sprites slain 0/8
**Progress:** What they spit keeps burning. Put it out or wait it out.
**Complete:** Efficient. I approve of efficient. The Dalefolk will tell you my people think only of stone and water. That's wrong. We think of whoever lives under the stone and the water. Take a pair.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 6s · choose 1: *Sluice-Tender's Mantle* (cloth shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Int +5 Spi) · *Gully-Walker's Spaulders* (leather shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Agi +7 Sta) · *Dam-Crew Pauldrons* (mail shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Str +7 Sta)

### ST-14 · [MC1] Charges on the Dam
*Level 9 · Requires ST-07 and ST-09 · Dammaster Sluicewright → Dammaster Sluicewright*

**Offer:** The millrace was the test. The dam is the target. My crews have found six charges set into the sluice-houses and there are Slagborn sappers up there guarding them. If the dam goes, Hopstead is under forty feet of water before anyone can ring a bell. I have engineers. The Reeve has rangers. Neither is enough. You're both. Go.
**Tracker:** Cinder-Charges defused 0/6 · Slagborn Sappers slain 0/6
**Progress:** Cut the grey cord, never the red. Go!
**Complete:** All six. The dam stands. I have just sent a runner to the Dale-Reeve to say so, and to say who did it. He and I have not exchanged a civil word in eleven years. I think that may now change. Choose what you like from the dam stores.
**Rewards:** 825 XP · 7s · choose 1: *Dam-Warden's Robe* (cloth chest, ilvl 14, +8 Int +6 Spi) · *Sluice-House Jerkin* (leather chest, ilvl 14, +8 Agi +9 Sta) · *Dammaster's Hauberk* (mail chest, ilvl 14, +8 Str +9 Sta)
**Notes:** 6 *Set Cinder-Charge* objects in the sluice-houses, per-player state, 5-second channel that is not interrupted by damage. On turn-in, Dale rangers and dam engineers stand guard together on the dam top for that player (world-state change).

**Offer:** Helga Sluicewright sent me a letter. A polite one. I had to sit down. The sappers came out of a side canyon the rangers call Blackmatch Camp. Somebody there is counting powder and paying for it. Get in, find their ledger, and bring it out. Leave their chief for now.

**Rewards:** 660 XP · 7s
