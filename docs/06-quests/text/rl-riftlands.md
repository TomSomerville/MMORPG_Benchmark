### RL-01 · The Eastern Cape
*Level 20 · Requires — · Notice board (Solhaven, Hammerdeep) or Sergeant Orrin (Warden's Rest) → Fort-Captain Elra Voss (Dawn's Stand)*

**Offer:** BY ORDER OF THE CONCORD. Able hands are wanted at Dawn's Stand, the fort at the mouth of the Riftlands on Meridia's eastern cape. Salvage gangs dig the old imperial ruins without licence, and the hyena-folk raid the caravan road. Pay is fair and the country is dry. Report to Fort-Captain Elra Voss.
**Tracker:** Report to Fort-Captain Voss at Dawn's Stand
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Another one who can read a notice. Good. I've a hundred soldiers to hold a canyon that goes down half a mile and across three leagues, and everything worth stealing is at the bottom. Stow your gear. There's work on every side of the wall.
**Rewards:** 875 XP
**Notes:** Orrin's spoken version: "They're hiring out east, at Dawn's Stand. Dry country. After this fen, I'd go myself." Reached by the east road from Thornwick.

### RL-14 · [MC2] Three Envoys
*Level 25 · Requires RL-13 · Speaker Quill → Speaker Quill*

**Offer:** Two envoys keep tents in my town, and both have asked to see the one carrying the shards. Ysolde Thornwake speaks for the Wildpact. Legate Corvane speaks for the Ashen Covenant, and yes, he is what he looks like. They won't sit in a room together, so you'll have to go to each. Hear them. **Tracker:** Hear out Ysolde Thornwake · Hear out Legate Corvane
**Progress:** Both of them, <name>. I want both sides.
**Complete:** So the forest wants it broken because it wounds the world, and the dead want it broken because its master would chain them again. And neither trusts the Concord not to keep it. For once, three powers want the same thing. I've been Speaker eleven years and I've never seen that. I don't expect it to last past the day it's done.
**Rewards:** 1,175 XP · 17s · +250 Freeholders reputation
**Notes:** **Ysolde:** "We meet again, or for the first time; it matters little. The Crown is a wound in the living world. Where a shard lies, nothing grows true. The Wildpact would see it unmade. We will lend what we know. We will not lend our trust. Your Concord has kept dangerous things before." **Corvane:** "Forgive the hour; I keep no others. Three centuries ago your Emperor's death freed my people from his service. Should Vaelric be crowned again, every one of the free dead becomes his once more. The Covenant will pay, generously, to see that crown broken. We will also be watching what you do with the pieces." Each ends with a two-option reply ("We want the same thing." / "The Concord will decide what's done with it."); the choice is stored as flags `rl14_ysolde` and `rl14_corvane` and changes nothing at launch. Players who completed TM-23 get Ysolde's alternative opening: "Corwin's friend. I wondered when the fire would bring you here." Players who completed TM-36 get an extra line from Corvane: "You are the one who opened Ashgrave. Then you have seen what a shard does to its bearer."
