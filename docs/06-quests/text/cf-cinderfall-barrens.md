### CF-01 · [MC2] Ash on the Wind
*Level 20 · Requires TM-32 or IR-31 · Archivist Dorne (Solhaven Athenaeum) or Lorekeeper Deepscroll (Hammerdeep Scrollery) → Commander Ragna Ashwatch (Fort Ashwatch)*

**Offer:** The Sliver pulls. Set the lead box on a table and it creeps toward the south wall. I have plotted the line, and it runs to the Cinderfall Barrens, south of the Ironroot Highlands. The Concord holds a fort there, under a Commander Ashwatch. Take the Sliver to her. My adjunct, Fenn Inkfinger, is already at the fort with instruments. Do not open the box.
**Tracker:** Carry the Crown Sliver to Commander Ashwatch at Fort Ashwatch
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** So this is what the scholars have been writing to me about. A splinter in a box. I've four hundred soldiers holding a wall against Slagborn raiders, and now I'm to help you follow a compass. Fine. Inkfinger says it points at the Slagworks. So do I, most days. Welcome to the Barrens.
**Rewards:** 875 XP
**Notes:** *Crown Sliver (boxed)* is given at accept and kept until VM-29. Athenaeum reputation.
