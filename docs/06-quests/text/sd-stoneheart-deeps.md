# Quest Text — Stoneheart Deeps (SD-01 to SD-20)

Format and global rules are in the [index](00-quest-text-index.md). Levels, XP and coin match the other three level 5–10 zones quest for quest.

### SD-01 · [MC1] The Hallwarden's Burden
*Level 5 · Requires EC-18 or BM-18, or none (see notes) · Hallwarden Ragnhild Lampwright → Hallwarden Lampwright*

**Offer:** Welcome to Lanternhold. Stand in the light, please. It is a custom here. The Deeps are the roots of Mount Khaz, and everything Hammerdeep burns or forges comes up through my halls. For a month my lamps have been going out, one by one, on the lower roads. If you know why, tell me.
**Tracker:** Present the Branded Ingot to Hallwarden Lampwright
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** An outsider arming the Slagborn. Then the lamps are being put out so that somebody can work in the dark. Below us are the roots of the Great Forge itself. I will not think about that yet. Sapper-Sergeant Fuse holds the lower roads. Report to her.
**Rewards:** 175 XP
**Notes:** If the player turned in EC-18 to Lampwright, this quest is offered and completed in the same conversation. Offered directly at level 5 to anyone else; the tracker then reads "Speak to Hallwarden Lampwright".

### SD-02 · Bats in the Lamps
*Level 5 · Requires — · Lamp-keeper Inga Wickfast → Lamp-keeper Wickfast*

**Offer:** Every lamp on the Long Stair is mine to keep. Lanternwing bats roost in the niches and drink the oil. Drink it! And then the lamp gutters and some poor carter walks off the edge. Ten bats, if you please. And mind your ears.
**Tracker:** Lanternwing Bats slain 0/10
**Progress:** That chirp of theirs will knock a spell clean out of your head.
**Complete:** The Stair's bright from top to bottom. This belonged to the keeper before me. Lamp-keepers are a draughty trade.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Wickfast's Lamp-Keeper Cloak* (back, ilvl 10, +3 Sta +3 Spi)
**Notes:** Wickfast stands at the top landing of the Long Stair, so this is the first quest a player arriving from Hammerdeep meets.

### SD-03 · Shamblers in the Glowcaps
*Level 6 · Requires — · Stonesinger Fjola → Stonesinger Fjola*

**Offer:** The Glowcap Grotto is the only green thing under this mountain, though it is not green. I tend it. Lately the old caps have been pulling up their roots and walking, and they trample the young ones. I do not know what has disturbed them. Cut down eight, and the grotto can breathe.
**Tracker:** Glowcap Shamblers slain 0/8
**Progress:** Their spores will spoil your aim. And my own craft hardly touches them.
**Complete:** The young caps will come on now. I wove these from grotto fibre. They glow a little. You get used to it.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Grotto-Tender's Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 11, +4 Int +4 Spi) · *Spore-Picker's Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 11, +4 Agi +6 Sta) · *Lanternhold Chain Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 11, +4 Str +6 Sta)

### SD-04 · Bear and Glowcap Hotpot
*Level 6 · Requires — · Cook Bodvar Ladle → Cook Ladle*

**Offer:** Surface folk think we live on rock and regret down here. We live on hotpot. Pale bear and glowcap, simmered a day in a lidded pot. Six cuts of bear, four good caps from the shamblers, and I'll teach you. Don't pick caps off the floor, they're someone's garden.
**Tracker:** Bear Meat 0/6 · Glowcaps 0/4
**Progress:** Bear and caps. The pot's waiting.
**Complete:** Now watch. Lid on, and you don't lift it. Not once. If you lift it, start again. Here's some I made the proper way.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 1s 50c · *Recipe: Glowcap Hotpot* (Cooking 25) · 5 *Glowcap Hotpot*
**Notes:** Bear Meat drops from Pale Cave Bear (75%). Glowcap drops from Glowcap Shambler (60%).

### SD-05 · [MC1] The Underwarrens
*Level 6 · Requires SD-01 · Sapper-Sergeant Halla Fuse → Sapper-Sergeant Fuse*

**Offer:** Lampwright sent you? Good, I'm short. The Gravelkin have gnawed tunnels between the cavern levels. That's how something is moving about down here without using my roads. Go into the Underwarrens and kill ten. I want those tunnels to be somewhere nobody wants to walk.
**Tracker:** Gravelkin Underdwellers slain 0/10
**Progress:** Their tunnels cross each other. Mark your way or you'll come out a level down.
**Complete:** Ten. Did you see anything odd? ...Crystals? They were hoarding crystals? Gravelkin eat crystal like you'd eat an apple. They don't save it for later.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Lamp-Trimmer's Wristwraps* (cloth wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Int +3 Spi) · *Tunnel-Runner's Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Agi +4 Sta) · *Sapper's Chain Vambraces* (mail wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Str +4 Sta)

### SD-06 · Derailed
*Level 7 · Requires — · Rail-master Torvald Cartwright → Rail-master Cartwright*

**Offer:** Three ore trains have gone off the Trestles this month. Three! In forty years I've lost two. Somebody's been at the rails. The carts are hung up on the lower spans with the crates still in them. I need eight crates back, and I need you not to look down while you fetch them.
**Tracker:** Ore Crates recovered 0/8
**Progress:** The rats have got into the wrecks. They come in pairs.
**Complete:** Eight crates, and the assay seals whole. You've a head for heights. Take this. It hung on a cart that won't be needing it.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · *Carter's Ore-Bag* (8-slot bag)
**Notes:** Crates are world objects in wrecked carts on the lower Trestle spans (12 spawn points), each beside 2 Rail Rats or 1 Gravelkin Shardchewer.

### SD-07 · [MC1] Paid in Crystal
*Level 7 · Requires SD-05 · Sapper-Sergeant Fuse → Sapper-Sergeant Fuse*

**Offer:** Hoarded crystal means they're being paid in it. They have a chief, Krakk, and my scouts say he carries a geode the size of your head, cut open and marked. Kill him and bring it to me.
**Tracker:** Shard-Chief Krakk slain 0/1 · Payroll-Geode 0/1
**Progress:** Lowest of the Underwarrens, where the tunnels glitter.
**Complete:** Look inside. Somebody's scratched tallies on the crystal faces. Tunnels dug, crystals paid. And there in the middle, a hand, burned black. The rock-eaters are being hired to dig a road through my mountain.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Geode-Reader's Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Underdweller-Hide Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Krakk's Shard Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)
**Notes:** Shard-Chief Krakk: level 8 humanoid, non-elite, 2 Shardchewer guards, 5-minute respawn. He drops the *Payroll-Geode* (100%).

### SD-08 · Skitterers

**Offer:** The Crystal Galleries hold the best tin and silver under Khaz, and I can't get an assay team into them. Skitterers. They look like part of the wall until the wall bites you. Eight of them, and I'll count it a favour to the whole mint.

**Complete:** My team went in this morning and all came out again. Take a pair of these. Assayers walk more than you'd think.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Assayer's Soft Shoes* (cloth feet, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Gallery-Walker's Boots* (leather feet, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Brightvein Chain Boots* (mail feet, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)

### SD-09 · [MC1] Powder in the Dark
*Level 8 · Requires SD-07 · Sapper-Sergeant Fuse → Sapper-Sergeant Fuse*

**Offer:** I'm a sapper. I know a set charge when I see one, and I've just seen one, wedged into a gallery pillar where no charge of mine has ever been. Cinder-packed. If the pillars go, the Great Cavern roof comes down on Lanternhold. There'll be six. There are always six. Pull them, and bring them to me. We may want them.
**Tracker:** Cinder-Charges removed from pillars 0/6
**Progress:** Steady hands. Grey cord out first.
**Complete:** Six, all Slagborn make. They mean to drop the roof on us to cover whatever they're doing below. Well, now I have six charges and they have none. These are my spare mitts. Wear them next time.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · *Fuse's Blast-Mitts* (mail hands, ilvl 13, +4 Str +3 Sta, +5 Fire resistance)
**Notes:** 6 *Gallery Pillar* objects in the Crystal Galleries, per-player state, 4-second channel. A Slagborn Tunneler spawns and attacks on 2 of the 6. The player keeps *Recovered Cinder-Charges* (quest item) for SD-14.

### SD-10 · Ancestors Unquiet
*Level 8 · Requires — · Ancestor-speaker Gudrun Hallowstone → Ancestor-speaker Hallowstone*

**Offer:** The Shattered Hall fell four hundred years ago, with a Thane and his whole household inside. We sealed it and sang over it and they slept. They are not sleeping now. Something is shaking the stone beneath them, and they walk and do not know us. Lay ten of them to rest. Strike cleanly. They are our grandfathers.
**Tracker:** Restless Ancestors laid to rest 0/10
**Progress:** If one rebukes you, you will find you cannot speak for a moment. Bear it. They are entitled.
**Complete:** I felt each one settle. Thank you. Something below is drilling, <name>. The dead feel it before the living do. Take these, with the Hall's blessing.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · choose 1: *Speaker's Mourning Trousers* (cloth legs, ilvl 13, +7 Int +6 Spi) · *Hall-Sweeper's Leggings* (leather legs, ilvl 13, +7 Agi +9 Sta) · *Elder Hall Chain Leggings* (mail legs, ilvl 13, +7 Str +9 Sta)

### SD-11 · The Thane's Lost Seal
*Level 8 · Requires SD-10 · Ancestor-speaker Hallowstone → Ancestor-speaker Hallowstone*

**Offer:** The Thane of the Shattered Hall was buried with his seal. While it lies loose in the rubble his household will keep rising to look for it. One of them will be carrying it. They always pick it up and never remember why. Find it and bring it to me, and I will set it in the wall where it belongs.
**Tracker:** Seal of the Elder Hall 0/1
**Progress:** One of the ancestors has it. Look for the one that walks as though searching.
**Complete:** Yes. That is his mark. I will set it in the wall tonight. The Hall keeps a few tokens for those who do it a service. Choose one.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 2s 50c · choose 1: *Seal of the Elder Hall* (neck, ilvl 13, +2 Str +2 Sta) · *Hallowstone Bead* (neck, ilvl 13, +2 Int +2 Spi)
**Notes:** The quest item is *Thane's Lost Seal*, dropped by Restless Ancestor (35%). The reward neck piece is a replica and is a different item.

### SD-12 · The Runaway Line
*Level 8 · Requires SD-06 · Rune-wright Sten Golemsbane → Rune-wright Golemsbane*

**Offer:** Before you ask: the name is a family name and I find it less funny every year. The rail-golems on the lower line have slipped their runes. They charge up and down the track flattening anything on it. Break eight and bring me five control-runes. Somebody re-cut those runes, and I want to see the hand.
**Tracker:** Rogue Rail-Golems destroyed 0/8 · Control-Runes 0/5
**Progress:** They charge in a straight line. Step off the line.
**Complete:** There. See that stroke? No dwarf-trained rune-wright cuts a stroke like that. These were re-cut by someone taught elsewhere. Have a pick, and the run of the tin seam.
**Rewards:** 725 XP · 6s · *Golemsbane's Mining Pick* (tool) and 10 *Tin Ore*
**Notes:** Control-Rune drops from Rogue Rail-Golem (70%). Golems are immune to bleed and poison.

### SD-13 · Geode Sprites
*Level 9 · Requires SD-08 · Assayer Brightvein → Assayer Brightvein*

**Offer:** Now the geodes are hatching. I can think of no better word. Sprites of living crystal, drifting through the lower galleries, throwing shards at my surveyors. They are very beautiful and I want eight of them dead by supper.
**Tracker:** Geode Sprites slain 0/8
**Progress:** Arcane craft slides off them like water off glass.
**Complete:** Splendid. If you found any stones in them, keep them, with the mint's compliments. And take a pair of these.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 6s · choose 1: *Gem-Sorter's Mantle* (cloth shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Int +5 Spi) · *Shard-Catcher Spaulders* (leather shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Agi +7 Sta) · *Mint-Guard Pauldrons* (mail shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Str +7 Sta)

### SD-14 · [MC1] The Undercut
*Level 9 · Requires SD-07 and SD-09 · Hallwarden Lampwright → Hallwarden Lampwright*

**Offer:** We have found them. The Slagborn are boring tunnels from the Underworks toward the roots of the Great Forge. Six bores, driven day and night. Sergeant Fuse says you still have the charges you pulled from our pillars. Use their own powder. Collapse all six bores, and kill the tunnelers working them.
**Tracker:** Bore-tunnels collapsed 0/6 · Slagborn Tunnelers slain 0/6
**Progress:** Set the charge at the bore mouth and get back. Ten paces at least.
**Complete:** I felt it through the floor. Six bores closed. But the scouts say there is a seventh. Wider than the rest, with an engine in it. For today, we have bought time, and you have earned the Hold's thanks.
**Rewards:** 825 XP · 7s · choose 1: *Lantern-Lit Robe* (cloth chest, ilvl 14, +8 Int +6 Spi) · *Undercut Jerkin* (leather chest, ilvl 14, +8 Agi +9 Sta) · *Hallwarden's Hauberk* (mail chest, ilvl 14, +8 Str +9 Sta)
**Notes:** Use *Recovered Cinder-Charges* at 6 *Bore Mouth* objects (per-player state). 4-second fuse, then the tunnel model swaps to its collapsed state; standing within 8 yards deals heavy damage. If the player lost the charges, Sergeant Fuse re-issues them.

### SD-15 · Borer Teeth
*Level 9 · Requires — · Alchemist Solva Deepwell → Alchemist Deepwell*

**Offer:** A rock borer's tooth will cut granite and never dull. Ground fine, it does remarkable things in a hardening draught. Six teeth. They come up through the floor, so if the ground shivers under you, you've found one.
**Tracker:** Borer Teeth 0/6
**Progress:** When the gravel moves by itself, step back and get ready.
**Complete:** Six, and sharp enough to shave with. I've made these up for you. Red mends. The grey ones toughen the skin.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 3s · 5 *Lesser Healing Potion* and 3 *Elixir of Minor Defense*
**Notes:** Borer Tooth drops from Rock Borer (65%). Deepwell is in Lanternhold.

### SD-16 · [MC1] The Bore-Captain's Plans
*Level 9 · Requires SD-14 · Hallwarden Lampwright → Hallwarden Lampwright*

**Offer:** A bore that size is surveyed before it is cut. There will be plans, and plans say where a tunnel is going and who paid for it. The Slagborn have a drafting-tent in the Undercut, away from the engine. Take the plans. Do not start a war in there. Not yet.
**Tracker:** Enter the Undercut · Survey Plans taken from the drafting-tent 0/1
**Progress:** The tent with the lamps. Slagborn hate lamps, so it will be the only one.
**Complete:** It runs straight to the forge-root. They mean to open the heart of the Great Forge and put their own fire in it. And here, in the margin: "Approved. C.A." The engine's master is Vigga Deepdrill. She built half the lifts in this hold before she was cast out.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 7s
**Notes:** The ventilation crawl passes 4 Slagborn Blastwrights instead of the 10 mobs in the main bore. *Survey Plans* is a table object. Deepdrill is not present until SD-17 is accepted.

### SD-17 · [MC1] Stop the Great Bore
*Level 10 · Requires SD-16 · Hallwarden Lampwright → Hallwarden Lampwright*

**Offer:** If the Great Bore reaches the forge-root, there is no Hammerdeep. Kill Vigga Deepdrill and stop her engine. The Bore itself will fight for her. Its arm sweeps half the chamber. There are three valves on its flank. Close them and the engine weakens, and so does she. Take two with you who can be trusted to turn a wheel.
**Tracker:** Bore-Captain Vigga Deepdrill slain 0/1
**Progress:** The seventh tunnel. Follow the noise.
**Complete:** It has stopped. I can hear my own lamps again. Did she speak? ..."The Forge was always hollow. We were only going to show you." No. I will not believe that. Choose from the Hold's armoury, <name>. Lanternhold stands because of you.
**Rewards:** 1,125 XP · 15s · choose 1: *Deepdrill's Bit* (1H mace, ilvl 15, speed 2.4, +4 Str +5 Sta) · *Bore-Plate Bulwark* (shield, ilvl 15, +4 Str +6 Sta) · *Glowcap Staff* (staff, ilvl 15, speed 3.0, +8 Int +7 Spi +2 Sta) · *Trestle-Beam Maul* (2H mace, ilvl 15, speed 3.6, +8 Str +10 Sta)
**Notes:** Elite, level 10, group 2–3. Drill Sweep, Steam Vent, calls 2 Tunnelers at 50%. Each of 3 *Bore Valve* objects closed (3-second channel) removes one stack of her +15% damage buff and slows Drill Sweep. She yells the "always hollow" line on death.

**Offer:** There is a bear in the deep grotto that has never seen light. Old Blindmaw. White as a root, the size of an ore cart. He was content for years. Now the drilling has maddened him and he kills whatever he can smell. I can't reach him. Take friends, and end it kindly.

**Complete:** Poor old thing. He was here before I was. The grotto-folk made these from his winter sheddings, long ago.

**Notes:** Elite, level 9, suggested players: 3. Blind Rage: ignores taunts for 6 seconds every 30 seconds and attacks a random player.

### SD-20 · [MC1] Plans Signed "C.A."
*Level 10 · Requires SD-17 · Hallwarden Lampwright → Ranger-Captain Farstride (Ironroot Hold)*

**Offer:** The Bore's iron, its powder and its orders came in by the Deep Gate rail-tunnel from the Ironroot Highlands. Ride the line south and put these plans in the hands of Ranger-Captain Farstride at Ironroot Hold. Tell the captain that Lanternhold's lamps are lit again, and that I mean to keep them so.
**Tracker:** Ride the Deep Gate line · Deliver the Survey Plans to Ranger-Captain Farstride in Ironroot Hold
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** You came up the Deep Gate line? Then it's open again. That's the first good news this week. Let me see. ..."C.A." We took a map in that hand off a dead cultist at the old barrows. Sit, <name>. You and I have the same enemy.
**Rewards:** 375 XP · 5s
**Notes:** Accepting the quest unlocks the Deep Gate mine-cart ride for the player. Unlocks IR-01. Satisfies the Act II step of [Embers of the Brand](../main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md).
