# Quest Text — Embercrag Hollow (EC-01 to EC-18)

### EC-01 · A Cold Welcome
*Level 1 · Requires — · (granted at character creation) → Warden Hilda Stonebraid*

**Offer:** You have come up to Coldhearth Camp, under the shoulder of Mount Khaz, to serve a season with the wardens. Warden Hilda Stonebraid keeps the hall at the head of the camp. She does not like to be kept waiting, and the wind does not like you standing still.
**Tracker:** Speak to Warden Stonebraid at the hall
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** You're late, or the snow's early. Either way, get by the fire. I'm Stonebraid. I run this camp, and this camp keeps the road to the Old Forge open. You work, you eat. That's the whole of the law up here.
**Rewards:** 40 XP
**Notes:** Auto-accepted on first login.

### EC-02 · Culling the Cubs
*Level 1 · Requires EC-01 · Warden Stonebraid → Warden Stonebraid*

**Offer:** The rime wolves denned on Frostpine Rise this winter and the cubs have found our meat racks. A grown wolf has the sense to fear a dwarf-hall. Cubs don't. Go up the rise and cull them before the pack decides the camp is its larder.
**Tracker:** Rime Wolf Cubs slain 0/8
**Progress:** Frostpine Rise, straight up from the hall. You can't miss the howling.
**Complete:** That'll do. The racks are safe and you've blood on your boots, which is how a first day ought to end. Here. Fingers are the first thing the cold takes.
**Rewards:** 80 XP · 25c · choose 1: *Coldhearth Wool Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 3) · *Coldhearth Hide Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 3) · *Coldhearth Ringed Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 3)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### EC-03 · A Summons from Your Trainer
*Level 1 · Requires EC-01 · Warden Stonebraid → class trainer*

**Offer:** Your trainer has been asking after you. Fighters go to Battlemaster Ironvow at the ring, clerics to Hearthpriest Coalbright at the hearth-shrine, druids to Stonesinger Mosshelm by the standing stone. Go and be taught something before the mountain teaches it to you harder.
**Tracker:** Speak to your class trainer
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** So the Warden let you out of her sight. Good. Stand there. No, there. The first thing I teach is free. Everything after that you pay for, in coin or in bruises.
**Rewards:** 40 XP · the first trainable ability is free (trainer cost waived once)
**Notes:** Turn-in NPC by class: Battlemaster Kol Ironvow (Fighter), Hearthpriest Magna Coalbright (Cleric), Stonesinger Yrsa Mosshelm (Druid).

### EC-04 · Gnashers in the Larder
*Level 2 · Requires EC-02 · Provisioner Dagmar Saltbeard → Provisioner Saltbeard*

**Offer:** Gravelkin. Rock-eaters. They've tunnelled up under my larder and they're not even after the food, they're eating the hearthstones out of the floor. Half my shelves fell in last night. Get down into the warrens under the camp and remind them whose stone this is.
**Tracker:** Gravelkin Scrabblers slain 0/8
**Progress:** I can hear them chewing. Stone shouldn't crunch like that.
**Complete:** Quiet below. I'll get the floor relaid. Here's something from the shelves that didn't fall in.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 50c · 5 *Coldhearth Black Bread* (food, restores 61 health over 18 s)

### EC-05 · Ram Chops for the Camp
*Level 2 · Requires EC-02 · Provisioner Saltbeard → Provisioner Saltbeard*

**Offer:** With the larder half in a hole, I need fresh meat. The crag rams on the rise are in good flesh. Six flanks will feed the camp for two days, and I'll show you the way my grandmother cooked them, which is the only right way.
**Tracker:** Ram Flanks 0/6
**Progress:** Mind the horns. They only hit you once, but you'll remember it.
**Complete:** Fine flanks. Now watch. Pepper, a hot flat stone, and you leave it alone until it's done. Leaving it alone is the hard part.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 25c · *Recipe: Seared Ram Chop* (Cooking 1) · 5 *Seared Ram Chop*
**Notes:** Ram Flank drops from Crag Ram (75%).

### EC-06 · Pilfered Picks
*Level 2 · Requires EC-02 · Miner Ulf → Miner Ulf*

**Offer:** It's not just hearthstones. The scrabblers have taken every pick my crew owns. I don't know what a thing with teeth like that wants with a pick. Maybe they like the taste of the iron. Get me eight back and my lads can stop digging with spoons.
**Tracker:** Stolen Picks 0/8
**Progress:** Eight picks. They'll have my mark on the haft.
**Complete:** Chewed on, every one, but they'll dig. Take this pouch. You'll be carrying more than picks before long.
**Rewards:** 130 XP · 50c · *Ulf's Ore Pouch* (6-slot bag)
**Notes:** Stolen Pick drops from Gravelkin Scrabbler, Gnasher and Rockchucker (70%).

### EC-07 · [MC1] The Cold Forge
*Level 3 · Requires EC-04 · Warden Stonebraid → Forgemaster Durgan Emberhand*

**Offer:** The Old Forge has burned for a thousand years. We didn't light it. Our grandfathers' grandfathers found it burning. This morning the glow on the snow was gone. Go up to the forge, look into the firebed with your own eyes, and then take what you saw down to Forgemaster Emberhand in Anvilrest. I'll not send that news by rumour.
**Tracker:** Inspect the Old Forge · Report to Forgemaster Emberhand in Anvilrest
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Cold. You put your hand in the bed and it was cold. Then the Ever-Ember is gone, <name>. It doesn't go out. It can't. Someone has carried it off, and whoever did knew exactly what they were taking.
**Rewards:** 200 XP · 1s · choose 1: *Anvilrest Hand Axe* (1H axe, ilvl 5, speed 2.3) · *Hearth-Shrine Mace* (1H mace, ilvl 5, speed 2.4) · *Stonesinger's Walking Staff* (staff, ilvl 5, speed 3.0)
**Notes:** "Inspect" is a click on the *Cold Firebed* world object. Examine text: "Grey ash, and a clean hollow where something the size of a fist once sat."

### EC-08 · Down to Anvilrest
*Level 3 · Requires EC-07 · Warden Stonebraid → Innkeeper Torva Alekettle*

**Offer:** If you're to work out of Anvilrest, you'll need a bed there. Take my tally of the camp's stores to Torva Alekettle at the Banked Coal. She victuals us, and she'll see you housed. Tell her the larder count is short and it's not my doing.
**Tracker:** Deliver the Warden's Tally to Innkeeper Alekettle in Anvilrest
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Stonebraid's tally, and short again. Gravelkin, is it? It's always something up at that camp. Well, you look frozen through. There's a fire, there's stew, and the bed by the chimney is yours.
**Rewards:** 100 XP · 50c
**Notes:** *Warden's Tally* is given at accept. Sets up the Anvilrest inn as a bind point.

### EC-09 · Lynx Pelts for Winter
*Level 4 · Requires EC-08 · Trapper Sif Whitepelt → Trapper Whitepelt*

**Offer:** The snowshoe lynxes on Tuskfall Ridge have their winter coats. Warmest fur on the mountain. I'd take them myself, but I turned my knee in a drift. Six pelts, and take them clean. I'll line you a cloak out of the offcuts.
**Tracker:** Lynx Pelts 0/6
**Progress:** They'll see you before you see them. Watch the rocks above you.
**Complete:** Clean cuts. You've done this before, or you learn quick. Here's the cloak. It won't look like much, but you'll bless it when the wind comes round.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 75c · *Whitepelt's Lined Cloak* (back, ilvl 9, +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Lynx Pelt drops from Snowshoe Lynx (65%).

### EC-10 · Rockchucker Roundup
*Level 4 · Requires EC-08 · Forgemaster Emberhand → Forgemaster Emberhand*

**Offer:** The Gravelkin have grown bold since the forge went cold. They sit on the ridge above the road and pelt my ore carts. Two drivers are abed with cracked heads. Go up there and clear ten of the throwers off the high ground.
**Tracker:** Gravelkin Rockchuckers slain 0/10
**Progress:** A rock to the helm is a rock to the helm, however small the hand that threw it.
**Complete:** The carts came through this morning without a dent. Good work. These boots have iron in the sole. You'll want that on the ridge.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 1s 50c · choose 1: *Anvilrest Felt Boots* (cloth feet, ilvl 6) · *Ridge-Walker Boots* (leather feet, ilvl 6) · *Carter's Chain Boots* (mail feet, ilvl 6)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### EC-11 · [MC1] Tracks in the Snow
*Level 4 · Requires EC-08 · Forgemaster Emberhand → Scout Brynja*

**Offer:** I walked up to the forge myself after you told me. There are bootprints leading away from it, and every one is black. Soot, pressed into the snow. They run toward Kiln Cave. I've sent Scout Brynja ahead. Follow the prints and find her.
**Tracker:** Follow the sooty footprints to Kiln Cave · Speak to Scout Brynja
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Keep your voice down. They're inside. Dwarves, <name>, our own kin by the look of them, but their beards are singed short and their skin is grey with slag. I know what they are. Slagborn. We cast them out for working hollow-fire. I didn't think any were left alive.
**Rewards:** 135 XP
**Notes:** *Sooty Footprint* decals lead from the Old Forge to the cave approach; three area triggers along them must be touched in order. Brynja crouches behind a boulder 30 yards from the cave mouth.

### EC-12 · [MC1] Soot and Shadow
*Level 4 · Requires EC-11 · Scout Brynja → Scout Brynja*

**Offer:** They've lookouts on the ledges and firebrands by the cave mouth. I can't get past them and neither can a message. Thin them out. And watch the crossbows. Slagborn never could forge a decent blade, but they could always shoot.
**Tracker:** Slagborn Lookouts slain 0/8 · Slagborn Firebrands slain 0/4
**Progress:** Still too many eyes on that ledge.
**Complete:** That's opened the approach. I took this off one of theirs last winter and never knew who he was. I'd rather you wore it than I did.
**Rewards:** 340 XP · 2s · choose 1: *Brynja's Rope Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 6) · *Scout's Leather Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 6) · *Ledge-Guard Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 6)
**Notes:** White items, armor only.

### EC-13 · The Brewer's Ice
*Level 4 · Requires EC-08 · Brewer Olaf Barleymash → Brewer Barleymash*

**Offer:** Rimewater Ale is brewed cold, and I mean cold. The only ice that'll do it comes off the sprites on Rimewater Pond. They don't give it up willingly. Five shards, and I'll stand you a share of the first cask.
**Tracker:** Rimewater Shards 0/5
**Progress:** The sprites nip. Keep moving and they can't slow you much.
**Complete:** Feel that. Colder than the pond it came from. Give me till tomorrow, and... no, here, take some of the last batch. A promise is poor drinking.
**Rewards:** 270 XP · 1s · 5 *Rimewater Ale* (drink, restores 151 mana over 18 s)
**Notes:** Rimewater Shard drops from Rimewater Sprite (70%).

### EC-14 · Tusks of Tuskfall
*Level 5 · Requires EC-09 · Trapper Whitepelt → Trapper Whitepelt*

**Offer:** The boars have come down off the high ridge and they're rooting up my trap lines faster than I can lay them. Put down eight of the brutes. I'll give you my second-best knife and a pick besides. Anyone working this mountain should have both.
**Tracker:** Tuskfall Boars slain 0/8
**Progress:** Step aside when they charge. Only the first hit has the weight in it.
**Complete:** Eight boars and you can still walk. Here, as promised. The knife's for skins and the pick's for copper. You'll find plenty of both up there.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Whitepelt's Skinning Knife* (tool) and *Anvilrest Mining Pick* (tool)

### EC-15 · [MC1] Branded Ingots
*Level 5 · Requires EC-12 · Forgemaster Emberhand → Forgemaster Emberhand*

**Offer:** Brynja says the Slagborn are carrying ingots. Exiles with no forge and no ore have no business with ingots. I want to see that metal. Take six from them, lookouts or firebrands, I don't care which.
**Tracker:** Branded Ingots 0/6
**Progress:** Bring me the metal. I'll know its maker when I see it.
**Complete:** This isn't our iron, and it isn't theirs. See the stamp? A hand, pressed in while the metal was soft and filled with ash. Somebody is supplying them, <name>. Somebody outside the mountain wanted our forge cold.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s 50c · choose 1: *Forgemaster's Cloth Cuffs* (cloth wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Int +1 Spi) · *Kilnside Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Agi +1 Sta) · *Anvilrest Chain Bracers* (mail wrist, ilvl 10, +1 Str +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Branded Ingot drops from Slagborn Lookout and Firebrand (70%).

### EC-16 · [MC1] Sootbeard's Reckoning
*Level 5 · Requires EC-15 · Forgemaster Emberhand → Forgemaster Emberhand*

**Offer:** Their chief is Grolm Sootbeard. I knew him. I was there when his name was struck from the hall-stone. He has the Ever-Ember in Kiln Cave, and he'll be trying to feed hollow-fire with it. Kill him and bring the Ember home. He keeps guards, and he fights hardest when he's nearly done. Don't go alone.
**Tracker:** Grolm Sootbeard slain 0/1 · Ever-Ember recovered 0/1
**Progress:** Is it done? Do you have it?
**Complete:** Give it here. Gently. ...Still warm. Still alive. A thousand years and it nearly ended in a cave. Grolm was a fine smith once. Take your pick of these, and know that Anvilrest will not forget who carried the fire home.
**Rewards:** 525 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Sootbeard's Tongs* (1H mace, ilvl 10, speed 2.4, +3 Str +2 Sta) · *Ember-Quenched Greataxe* (2H axe, ilvl 10, speed 3.4, +6 Str +5 Sta) · *Stonesinger's Rootstaff* (staff, ilvl 10, speed 3.0, +5 Int +4 Spi +1 Sta)
**Notes:** Suggested players: 2. Sootbeard has 2 Lookout guards and enrages (Kindle) at 30%. The *Ever-Ember* is looted from the *Slag Crucible* world object behind him, usable only after his death. On turn-in the Old Forge world state changes from cold to lit for that player.

### EC-17 · Grawl the Boulder-Eater
*Level 5 · Requires EC-06 · Miner Ulf → Miner Ulf*

**Offer:** There's a big one in the deep warrens. Grawl, the lads call him. He doesn't eat hearthstones, he eats boulders, and he's brought down two of my galleries doing it. I can't dig with that thing loose. Take help. His hide's as hard as what he eats.
**Tracker:** Grawl the Boulder-Eater slain 0/1
**Progress:** Deep warrens, past the second fall. Listen for the grinding.
**Complete:** Dead? You're sure? I'll believe it when I've dug for a week with nothing falling on me. Here, we cut these from what was left of him.
**Rewards:** 525 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Boulder-Eater's Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 10, +4 Str +5 Sta) · *Grawl's Molar* (held off-hand, ilvl 10, +3 Int +2 Spi) · *Warren-Delver's Mantle* (leather shoulders, ilvl 10, +4 Agi +5 Sta)
**Notes:** Elite, level 5. Suggested players: 2. The leather option was added so every class has a choice.

### EC-18 · [MC1] Word to Stonewake
*Level 5 · Requires EC-16 · Forgemaster Emberhand → Dale-Reeve Garrik Stonewake (Hopstead) or Hallwarden Lampwright (Lanternhold)*

**Offer:** Somebody armed the Slagborn, and they'll have armed others. Take one of these ingots out through Bouldergate to Dale-Reeve Garrik Stonewake at Hopstead. The hill-folk should know. Or carry it down the inner road to Hallwarden Lampwright at Lanternhold in the Deeps. Either way, put that stamp in front of someone with the sense to fear it.
**Tracker:** Carry the Branded Ingot to Dale-Reeve Stonewake in Stonewake Dale or Hallwarden Lampwright in the Stoneheart Deeps
**Progress:** —
**Complete (Stonewake):** Mountain iron with a stranger's mark. Hm. We had a peddler through at shearing, giving the herd-lads coins with a hand burned in them. I thought it was a trinket. Come inside, <name>.
**Complete (Lampwright):** An ash hand. I have seen that scratched on the lamp-posts of the lower road, always where a lamp had been put out. Thank you for bringing this. I wish you had brought better news.
**Rewards:** 175 XP · 1s
**Notes:** *Branded Ingot (sample)* is given at accept. Turning in to Stonewake unlocks ST-01; to Lampwright unlocks SD-01. Either satisfies the Act I step of [Embers of the Brand](../main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md).
