### EC-01 · [MC1] The Forgemaster's Burden
*Level 5 · Requires ST-18 or BM-18, or none (see notes) · Forgemaster Durgan Emberhand → Forgemaster Emberhand*

**Offer:** You're in Anvilrest, and you've come in a bad week. The Old Forge has burned for a thousand years. We didn't light it. Our grandfathers' grandfathers found it burning. Nine mornings ago the glow on the snow was gone, and I put my own hand in the firebed and it was cold. I've gravelkin in my ore road and a dark forge on my conscience. If you've more trouble for me, set it on the anvil.
**Tracker:** Present the Payroll-Slate to Forgemaster Emberhand
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** Rock-eaters paid in slag, and that hand on every lump. We've the same hand up here, stamped into iron that isn't ours. And the dam on the tally. So the Dale's trouble and mine have one paymaster. Well. Warden Stonebraid keeps the camp under the Rise, and the gravelkin are thickest under her floor. Go and make yourself useful to her.
**Rewards:** 175 XP
**Notes:** If the player turned in ST-18 to Emberhand, this quest is offered and completed in the same conversation. Offered directly at level 5 to anyone else; the tracker then reads "Speak to Forgemaster Emberhand".

### EC-02 · Fleece for Winter
*Level 5 · Requires — · Trapper Sif Whitepelt → Trapper Whitepelt*

**Offer:** It's always winter up here, and winter wants fleece. The crag rams have come down off Frostpine Rise onto the snowfields, in good coat and bad temper, and they've butted two of my trap lines flat. I'd take them myself, but I turned my knee in a drift. Ten rams. I'll line you a cloak from the first fleece.
**Tracker:** Crag Rams slain 0/10
**Progress:** Mind the horns. They only stun you once, but you'll remember it.
**Complete:** Ten, and clean. You've done this before, or you learn quick. Here's the cloak. It won't look like much, but you'll bless it when the wind comes round.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Whitepelt's Lined Cloak* (back, ilvl 10, +3 Sta +3 Spi)

### EC-03 · Tusks of Tuskfall
*Level 6 · Requires EC-01 · Trapper Whitepelt → Trapper Whitepelt*

**Offer:** The boars have come down off the high ridge and they're rooting up what the rams left of my lines. They never came this low while the forge was lit. The warm ground kept them fat up top. Put down eight of the brutes at the foot of Tuskfall.
**Tracker:** Tuskfall Boars slain 0/8
**Progress:** Step aside when they charge. Only the first hit has the weight in it.
**Complete:** Eight boars and you can still walk. Here, trapper's issue. You'll want them on the ledges.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Anvilrest Felt Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 11, +4 Int +4 Spi) · *Trap-Line Gloves* (leather hands, ilvl 11, +4 Agi +6 Sta) · *Tuskfall Chain Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 11, +4 Str +6 Sta)

### EC-04 · Ram Chops for the Camp
*Level 6 · Requires — · Provisioner Dagmar Saltbeard → Provisioner Saltbeard*

**Offer:** Mind the hole in the floor. Half my larder went down it, so the camp eats what's fresh or it doesn't eat. A ram chop the way my grandmother cooked it is larded with boar fat, or it dries out on the stone. Fetch six ram flanks and four boar haunches, and I'll show you the only right way.
**Tracker:** Ram Flanks 0/6 · Boar Haunches 0/4
**Progress:** Ram and boar. I've the pepper.
**Complete:** Fine flanks. Now watch. Pepper, a strip of boar fat, a hot flat stone, and you leave it alone until it's done. Leaving it alone is the hard part. Take these with you.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 1s 50c · *Recipe: Seared Ram Chop* (Cooking 25) · 5 *Seared Ram Chop*
**Notes:** Ram Flank drops from Crag Ram (75%). Boar Haunch drops from Tuskfall Boar (60%).

### EC-05 · [MC1] Gnashers in the Larder
*Level 6 · Requires EC-01 · Warden Hilda Stonebraid → Warden Stonebraid*

**Offer:** I'm Stonebraid. I run this camp, and this camp keeps the road to the Old Forge open. You work, you eat. That's the whole of the law up here. Gravelkin have tunnelled up under my larder, and they're not after the food, they're eating the hearthstones out of the floor. Rock-eaters never came this near a dwarf-hall. Something has driven them, or sent them. Go down into the warrens and kill ten.
**Tracker:** Gravelkin Scrabblers slain 0/10
**Progress:** I can hear them chewing. Stone shouldn't crunch like that.
**Complete:** Ten, and you say they had ingots down there? Stacked? Gravelkin eat rock. They don't keep a strongroom. Somebody's paying them, and in iron with that hand on it.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Coldhearth Wool Wristwraps* (cloth wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Int +3 Spi) · *Warren-Runner's Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Agi +4 Sta) · *Warden's Chain Vambraces* (mail wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Str +4 Sta)

### EC-06 · Pilfered Picks
*Level 7 · Requires EC-05 · Miner Ulf → Miner Ulf*

**Offer:** It's not just hearthstones. The gnashers have carried off every tool crate my crew owns, picks and all. I don't know what a thing with teeth like that wants with a pick. Maybe they like the taste of the iron. They're stacked in the second chamber. Bring back eight crates and my lads can stop digging with spoons.
**Tracker:** Tool Crates recovered 0/8
**Progress:** They go mad when they're nearly dead. Finish them fast.
**Complete:** Eight crates. Chewed at the corners, every one, but the picks will dig. Take this sack. You've more to carry than my lads ever did.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · *Ulf's Ore Sack* (8-slot bag)
**Notes:** Tool Crates are world objects in the second chamber of the Warrens (12 spawn points), each beside 1–2 Gravelkin Gnashers.

### EC-07 · [MC1] Sootbeard's Reckoning
*Level 7 · Requires EC-05 · Warden Stonebraid → Warden Stonebraid*

**Offer:** There are bootprints leading away from the Old Forge, and every one is black. Soot, pressed into the snow. They run to Kiln Cave, and Scout Brynja has put a name to who is in it. Grolm Sootbeard. Slagborn. I walked him to the pass myself the day his name was struck from the hall-stone, for working hollow-fire. He has the Ever-Ember in that cave. Kill him and bring the Ember home. He keeps two guards, and he fights hardest when he's nearly done.
**Tracker:** Grolm Sootbeard slain 0/1 · Ever-Ember recovered 0/1
**Progress:** Is it done? Do you have it?
**Complete:** Give it here. Gently. ...Still warm. Still alive. A thousand years, and it nearly ended in a cave. Grolm was a fine smith once. Look up the valley tonight, <name>. There'll be a glow on the snow again, and Anvilrest will not forget who carried the fire home.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Forge-Tender's Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Kilnside Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Sootbeard's Chain Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)
**Notes:** Grolm Sootbeard: level 8 humanoid, non-elite, 2 Lookout guards, 5-minute respawn. He casts Slag Bolt and enrages (Kindle) at 30% health. The *Ever-Ember* is looted from the *Slag Crucible* world object behind him, usable only after his death. On turn-in the Old Forge world state changes from cold to lit for that player. *Sooty Footprint* decals lead from the Old Forge to the cave approach (scenery).

### EC-08 · Soot and Shadow
*Level 7 · Requires — · Scout Brynja → Scout Brynja*

**Offer:** Keep your voice down. They're inside. Dwarves, our own kin by the look of them, but their beards are singed short and their skin is grey with slag. Slagborn. I didn't think any were left alive. They've lookouts on the ledges, and nobody gets to that cave mouth while they stand there. Thin them out. And watch the crossbows. **Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Anvilrest Felt Boots* (cloth feet, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Ridge-Walker Boots* (leather feet, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Ledge-Guard Chain Boots* (mail feet, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)

### EC-09 · [MC1] Powder on the Cart Road
*Level 8 · Requires EC-07 · Tinker Gudrid Springlock → Tinker Springlock*

**Offer:** If it ticks, it's mine. If it bangs, that's also mine, and this is not mine. I keep the ore carts, and I found this under the ore in one of them this morning. It's a charge. Cinder-packed, with a slow fuse meant to be lit from somewhere else. Those carts stand loaded on the Tuskfall siding, waiting to go down Bouldergate to the Dale. There'll be more. Six carts, six charges, I'd bet my bench on it. Pull them out. Gently.
**Tracker:** Cinder-Charges removed from ore carts 0/6
**Progress:** Don't drop one. Don't warm one. Don't look at one too hard.
**Complete:** Six. Set them down there. Slowly. That's Slagborn work, and whoever hid them knew my carts better than I do. They weren't meant to go off here. They were meant to ride down to the Dale under honest ore. Here, I wear these for exactly this. I've a second pair.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · *Springlock's Insulated Mitts* (leather hands, ilvl 13, +4 Agi +3 Sta, +5 Fire resistance)
**Notes:** 6 *Loaded Ore Cart* objects on the Tuskfall siding, per-player state; each takes a 4-second channel. A Slagborn Sapper spawns and attacks on 2 of the 6 (chosen at random per player).

### EC-12 · The Brewer's Ice

### EC-14 · [MC1] The Powder Train
*Level 9 · Requires EC-07 and EC-09 · Warden Stonebraid → Warden Stonebraid*

**Offer:** The carts were the test. This is the train. My wardens have found six sledges at the head of Slagfall Gully, loaded with cinder-charges, with Slagborn sappers standing over them. That's why they wanted the forge dark: no glow on the snow, nobody sees a sledge go by night. They're bound down Bouldergate for the Stonewake Dam. If the dam goes, Hopstead is under forty feet of water before anyone can ring a bell. Go.
**Tracker:** Cinder-Charges defused 0/6 · Slagborn Sappers slain 0/6
**Progress:** Cut the grey cord, never the red. Go!
**Complete:** All six. The dam stands, and the Dale never heard a thing. I have just sent a runner down to the Dale-Reeve and another to the Dammaster, to say so and to say who did it. No warden of this camp has written to a Dale-Moot in my lifetime. I think that may now change. Choose what you like from the camp stores.
**Rewards:** 825 XP · 7s · choose 1: *Hearthpriest's Robe* (cloth chest, ilvl 14, +8 Int +6 Spi) · *Slagfall Jerkin* (leather chest, ilvl 14, +8 Agi +9 Sta) · *Warden's Hauberk* (mail chest, ilvl 14, +8 Str +9 Sta)
**Notes:** 6 *Powder Sledge* objects at the head of Slagfall Gully, per-player state, 5-second channel that is not interrupted by damage. On turn-in, Coldhearth wardens stand guard over the emptied sledges for that player (world-state change).
