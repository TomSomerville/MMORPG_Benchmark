### HF-01 · [MC1] The Reeve's Burden
*Level 5 · Requires BM-18 or ST-18, or none (see notes) · Reeve Maren Holt → Reeve Holt*

**Offer:** You have the look of someone carrying bad news. Harrow's Cross feeds Solhaven, <name>. Every loaf in the capital starts in these fields, and this year I have bandits in the ditches, straw men walking the hill, and strangers whispering to my field hands. If you know anything about any of it, I'll hear it now.
**Tracker:** Present the brand to Reeve Holt
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** So it has a shape. A hand in ash. I've been fighting three troubles and you're telling me they may be one. I'm deputising you. Captain Wyck commands the militia, such as it is. Farmer Fenner speaks for the growers. Help them, and keep your eyes open for that mark.
**Rewards:** 175 XP
**Notes:** If the player turned in BM-18 to Holt, this quest is offered and completed in the same conversation. A player arriving without the chain (from Stonewake Dale, or skipping) is offered it directly at level 5; the tracker then reads "Speak to Reeve Holt" and Holt's completion text is unchanged.

### HF-02 · Jackals in the Chaff
*Level 5 · Requires — · Farmer Odell Fenner → Farmer Fenner*

**Offer:** Jackals follow the reapers. Always have. But this year they're not waiting for the mice. They went for my youngest in the stubble yesterday, bold as you like. Ten of them dead might teach the rest some manners.
**Tracker:** Harrow Jackals slain 0/10
**Progress:** They hunt in packs. When one howls, the others come quicker.
**Complete:** That'll hold them till the frost. Prue ran this up from the good wool. Take it, with a farmer's thanks.
**Rewards:** 350 XP · 2s · *Fenner's Harvest Cloak* (back, ilvl 10, +3 Sta +3 Spi)

### HF-03 · Rooters in the Turnips
*Level 6 · Requires HF-02 · Farmer Fenner → Farmer Fenner*

**Offer:** Now it's the turnips. Tusked rooters, eight or nine of them, turning the whole lower field over like a plough gone mad. I planted those turnips. I'd like to be the one who digs them up.
**Tracker:** Tusked Rooters slain 0/8
**Progress:** Mind the tusks. They open you up and you keep bleeding.
**Complete:** Half a field saved is better than none. You'll want gloves for this kind of work.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Turnip-Puller's Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 11, +4 Int +4 Spi) · *Fieldhand's Grips* (leather hands, ilvl 11, +4 Agi +6 Sta) · *Fenner's Chain Gauntlets* (mail hands, ilvl 11, +4 Str +6 Sta)

### HF-04 · Harvest Pie
*Level 6 · Requires HF-02 · Goodwife Prue Fenner → Goodwife Fenner*

**Offer:** Odell has you killing rooters? Then don't leave the meat for the crows. Bring me six flanks, and four harrier eggs while you're in the fields. The birds nest in the stooks. I'll teach you my harvest pie. There are captains in Solhaven who'd pay gold for that recipe, and I'm giving it to you for an afternoon's work.
**Tracker:** Rooter Flank 0/6 · Harrier Eggs 0/4
**Progress:** Flanks and eggs, dear. Both.
**Complete:** Lovely. Now, the secret is you cook the flank first and you don't tell anyone about the egg. There. Take these for the road.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 1s 50c · *Recipe: Harvest Pie* (Cooking 10) · 5 *Harvest Pie*
**Notes:** Rooter Flank drops from Tusked Rooter (75%). Harrier Egg drops from Field Harrier (60%).

### HF-05 · [MC1] Reaver's Row
*Level 6 · Requires HF-01 · Captain Jorah Wyck → Captain Wyck*

**Offer:** The Reeve says you're useful. I have forty militia and thirty of them are holding scythes. The Reavers have a camp strung along the dry ditch east of here. They used to rob the odd cart. Now they take whole wagon trains, and they don't run when we come. Hit the Row. Ten thugs fewer and my people might hold the road.
**Tracker:** Harrow Reaver Thugs slain 0/10
**Progress:** They'll try to stun you with the first blow. Expect it.
**Complete:** Ten. That's more than my whole company managed in a month. Find something that fits from the stores.
**Rewards:** 420 XP · 3s · choose 1: *Militia Cloth Wristwraps* (cloth wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Int +3 Spi) · *Ditch-Runner Bracers* (leather wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Agi +4 Sta) · *Wyck's Chain Vambraces* (mail wrist, ilvl 11, +3 Str +4 Sta)

### HF-06 · Poached Goods
*Level 7 · Requires HF-05 · Quartermaster Pell → Quartermaster Pell*

**Offer:** Everything the Reavers took is sitting in crates along the Row with the Concord seal still on it. Flour, nails, lamp oil. I can't replace it and winter is coming. Bring back eight crates. The poachers guard them, so you'll have to go through them first.
**Tracker:** Crates of Poached Goods 0/8
**Progress:** Eight crates, <name>. I've counted what's missing twice.
**Complete:** Seals unbroken. You're a wonder. Here, this came in a crate nobody claimed. It'll hold more than that pouch of yours.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · *Quartermaster's Satchel* (8-slot bag)
**Notes:** Crates are world objects in Reaver's Row (12 spawn points), each beside 1–2 Reaver Poachers.

### HF-07 · [MC1] Paid in Ash
*Level 7 · Requires HF-05 · Captain Wyck → Captain Wyck*

**Offer:** Bandits who stand and fight are being paid to. One of the thugs you put down had a pay-chit on him, signed by a Paymaster Grice. He keeps a tent at the far end of the Row. Kill him and bring me the payroll. I want to see whose money this is.
**Tracker:** Paymaster Grice slain 0/1 · Reaver Payroll 0/1
**Progress:** The far end of the Row. He'll have a strongbox.
**Complete:** Look at these coins. Every one branded with a hand. And look at the sums. This is more than the Reavers ever stole. Somebody is paying them wages to ruin the harvest.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Paymaster's Silk Sash* (cloth waist, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Reaver-Hide Belt* (leather waist, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Row-Breaker Girdle* (mail waist, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)
**Notes:** Paymaster Grice: level 8 humanoid, non-elite, 2 Thug guards, 5-minute respawn. *Reaver Payroll* is looted from the *Paymaster's Strongbox* beside him.

### HF-08 · The Straw Men Walk
*Level 7 · Requires HF-03 · Farmer Fenner → Farmer Fenner*

**Offer:** You'll think I've been at the cider. The scarecrows on the hill have got down off their poles. They walk the rows at night and they flail at anything that moves. I put those up myself, out of straw and old coats. There's nothing in them to walk with. Knock eight of them down, would you? And tell me what you find inside.
**Tracker:** Cinderstuffed Scarecrows slain 0/8
**Progress:** Fire does for them quickest, if you have any.
**Complete:** Hot coals? In the chest? I never put coals in a scarecrow, a child would know better. Somebody's been up my hill in the dark. Go and tell Ines Wort, the alchemist. She'll know what it means.
**Rewards:** 500 XP · 4s · choose 1: *Scarecrow's Rag Shoes* (cloth feet, ilvl 12, +5 Int +4 Spi) · *Hill-Field Boots* (leather feet, ilvl 12, +5 Agi +6 Sta) · *Stubble-Stompers* (mail feet, ilvl 12, +5 Str +6 Sta)

### HF-09 · [MC1] Cinders in the Stuffing
*Level 8 · Requires HF-08 · Alchemist Ines Wort → Alchemist Wort*

**Offer:** A coal that makes straw get up and walk. I should like very much to see one. Bring me six of their hearts, still smouldering. Do not carry them next to your skin. I mean that kindly.
**Tracker:** Smoldering Cinder-Hearts 0/6
**Progress:** Still smouldering, please. A dead one tells me nothing.
**Complete:** Fascinating, and horrible. This is not coal. It burns without eating itself. It's the same fire as the brand on those coins, I'd stake my shop on it. Hollow-fire. Someone who knows the craft made these. Take these mitts. I made them for handling exactly this sort of thing.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · *Wort's Fireproof Mitts* (cloth hands, ilvl 13, +4 Int +3 Sta, +5 Fire resistance)
**Notes:** Smoldering Cinder-Heart drops from Cinderstuffed Scarecrow (70%).

### HF-10 · Netters of Longstrand
*Level 8 · Requires — · Fisher Corran → Fisher Corran*

**Offer:** The Gillkin have three villages on the Longstrand now. Last year it was one. They net our boats the way we net herring, and they've drowned two men doing it. Ten of their netters, and the boats can go out again.
**Tracker:** Longstrand Netters slain 0/10
**Progress:** If they net you, the others close in. Don't fight them in the shallows.
**Complete:** The boats went out on the morning tide and all came back. These were my brother's. He's one of the two. Wear them and think well of him.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 5s · choose 1: *Strand-Comber's Trousers* (cloth legs, ilvl 13, +7 Int +6 Spi) · *Fisher's Oilskin Leggings* (leather legs, ilvl 13, +7 Agi +9 Sta) · *Longstrand Chain Leggings* (mail legs, ilvl 13, +7 Str +9 Sta)
**Notes:** Fisher Corran stands at the Longstrand jetty.

### HF-11 · The Widow's Locket
*Level 8 · Requires — · Widow Tansy Brell → Widow Brell*

**Offer:** My Harl went out in the boats and the fish-folk took him. I've made my peace with that. But he wore a locket with my likeness in it, and one of their tidecallers wears it now. I've seen it shining on the creature's neck from the cliff. I can't bear that. Please.
**Tracker:** Widow's Locket 0/1
**Progress:** The ones that call the water. One of them has it.
**Complete:** Oh. Oh, that's it. It still opens. Thank you, <name>. I've nothing fit to give you but this. Harl gave it to me the day we were wed. I'd like it to go on keeping somebody safe.
**Rewards:** 580 XP · 2s 50c · *Brell's Keepsake* (neck, ilvl 13, +2 Spi +2 Sta)
**Notes:** Widow's Locket drops from Longstrand Tidecaller (35%). Tidecallers cast Healing Tide, which can be interrupted.

### HF-12 · Quarry Quandary
*Level 8 · Requires — · Quarry-boss Garrow → Quarry-boss Garrow*

**Offer:** Burrowkin in my quarry. I could live with that. Burrowkin with my blasting powder, I can't. They've no notion what it does. They light it to see the pretty sparks. Kill eight of the sappers and bring back five kegs before they bring the whole face down on us.
**Tracker:** Quarry Burrowkin Sappers slain 0/8 · Blasting Powder 0/5
**Progress:** When one throws a stick with a fizzing end, you move. Two seconds, no more.
**Complete:** All fingers still attached? Good. You can have a pick and the run of the tin seam for an afternoon. Here's a start on it.
**Rewards:** 725 XP · 6s · *Garrow's Mining Pick* (tool) and 10 *Tin Ore*
**Notes:** Blasting Powder drops from Quarry Burrowkin Sapper (70%).

### HF-13 · Thresher Trouble
*Level 9 · Requires HF-09 · Tinker Abel Marsh → Tinker Marsh*

**Offer:** They're mine. The threshers. I built them to bring the harvest in with half the hands, and they worked, I swear they worked. Then the fires started in the Burning Rows and something got into their boilers. Now they thresh anything that stands still. Break five of them. I'll build better.
**Tracker:** Thresher Constructs destroyed 0/5
**Progress:** When the blades spin up, get out from under. You'll hear it wind.
**Complete:** Five. That's a year's work in scrap. And the fireboxes were all the same? Not wood. Coals that won't go out. I didn't put those in. I want that understood.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 6s · choose 1: *Tinker's Padded Mantle* (cloth shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Int +5 Spi) · *Thresher-Belt Spaulders* (leather shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Agi +7 Sta) · *Cog-Plate Pauldrons* (mail shoulders, ilvl 14, +6 Str +7 Sta)
**Notes:** Tinker Marsh stands at the edge of the Burning Rows.

**Offer:** They've stopped stealing the harvest and started burning it. The Reavers have firestarters in the north rows, and the wind is toward the town. Take these buckets. Put out what you can and put down the ones holding the torches. Six fires, six firestarters. Go.
**Tracker:** Field fires doused 0/6 · Reaver Firestarters slain 0/6
**Progress:** The wind's still rising. Hurry.
**Complete:** The wind dropped an hour ago and the rows that are left will stand. You saved a third of the harvest, <name>. People will eat this winter because of today. Take whatever fits.
**Rewards:** 825 XP · 7s · choose 1: *Smoke-Stained Robe* (cloth chest, ilvl 14, +8 Int +6 Spi) · *Fire-Line Jerkin* (leather chest, ilvl 14, +8 Agi +9 Sta) · *Militia Captain's Hauberk* (mail chest, ilvl 14, +8 Str +9 Sta)

### HF-15 · Skink Eyes
*Level 9 · Requires HF-09 · Alchemist Wort → Alchemist Wort*

**Offer:** The skinks in Dustwater Quarry can stop a man in his tracks with a look. The humour of the eye does it. I can make something useful from that, but I am not going down there myself. Six eyes. Look away when they stare at you, or better, hit them while they try.
**Tracker:** Skink Eyes 0/6
**Progress:** The gaze takes them two seconds to gather. That's your chance.
**Complete:** Six, and not one burst. Give me a moment. There. Red ones mend you, the yellow ones will make you quick. Don't mix them up.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 3s · 5 *Lesser Healing Potion* and 3 *Swiftness Potion*
**Notes:** Skink Eye drops from Quarry Skink (65%).

### HF-16 · [MC1] The Marshal's Seal
*Level 9 · Requires HF-14 · Reeve Holt → Reeve Holt*

**Offer:** Close the door. The payroll you took from Grice was drawn on a strongroom in Vane Manor. Marshal Edrik Vane commands every soldier between here and the Marches. He is the man I would ask for help. I can't accuse him on a ledger. Get into his study and bring me his letters. Don't fight the whole household if you can avoid it.
**Tracker:** Enter Vane Manor · Sealed Letters taken from the study 0/1
**Progress:** The study is on the upper floor, above the hall.
**Complete:** His own seal. "The harvest will fail as agreed. See that the Reeve has no soldiers to spare." And every one is answered by someone who signs only "C.A." I have known Edrik Vane for twenty years. I stood at his wedding.
**Rewards:** 660 XP · 7s
**Notes:** The study is reached by the servants' stair, which passes 4 Vane Houseguards instead of the 10 in the main hall. *Sealed Letters* is a desk object. Marshal Vane is not present until HF-17 is accepted.

### HF-17 · [MC1] Traitor's Due
*Level 10 · Requires HF-16 · Reeve Holt → Reeve Holt*

**Offer:** There is no court that will sit on a Marshal in time, and he'll know by now that the letters are gone. I am the Reeve of Harrowfield and I name Edrik Vane traitor. Go to the manor hall and end it. He is the best sword in the province. Do not go alone.
**Tracker:** Marshal Edrik Vane slain 0/1
**Progress:** He'll be in the great hall, with his guard about him.
**Complete:** It's done, then. Did he say anything? ..."The fields were always going to burn. I only chose who held the torch." Twenty years. Take your choice of the manor's armoury. It belongs to the Concord now.
**Rewards:** 1,125 XP · 15s · choose 1: *Vane's Disgrace* (1H sword, ilvl 15, speed 2.3, +4 Str +5 Sta) · *Marshal's Tower Shield* (shield, ilvl 15, +4 Str +6 Sta) · *Harrowfield Reaping-Staff* (staff, ilvl 15, speed 3.0, +8 Int +7 Spi +2 Sta) · *Gleaner's Greatscythe* (2H axe, ilvl 15, speed 3.5, +8 Str +10 Sta)
**Notes:** Elite, level 10, group 2–3. Shield Wall at 50%, Rallying Cry summons 2 Houseguards, Disarm. Vane yells the "fields were always going to burn" line on death.

**Tracker:** Deliver the Sealed Letters to Warden-Captain Dunmoor in Thornwick
**Progress:** —

**Rewards:** 375 XP · 5s
