### VM-01 · The Lantern Road
*Level 20 · Requires — · Sergeant Orrin (Warden's Rest) or notice board (Solhaven) → Watch-Captain Ione Marsh (Lanternwatch)*

**Offer:** North-east of the fen the fog never lifts. That's Veilmoor. There's a town up there, Lanternwatch, that does nothing but keep the road-lamps lit, because on that moor a lit lamp is the only way to know you're still on the road. Their Watch-Captain has sent for help three times. Follow the lanterns. If you come to a dark one, turn round.
**Tracker:** Report to Watch-Captain Marsh at Lanternwatch
**Progress:** —
**Complete:** You kept to the lights. Good. The last two they sent didn't. I'm Marsh. I have forty lamplighters, twelve watchmen and nine miles of road, and every night a few more lamps go dark. Welcome to Veilmoor. Don't whistle after sunset.
**Rewards:** 875 XP

### VM-04 · Will-o'-Wisps
*Level 22 · Requires VM-01 · Lamplighter Tobin Wick → Lamplighter Wick*

**Offer:** The bog wisps look like our lamps. That's the whole trouble. A traveller sees a light off to the left, thinks it's the road, and they find him in the peat a month later. I want eight of their essences. Burned in a lamp, wisp-essence shows blue beside a true flame. Then nobody need be fooled.
**Tracker:** Wisp Essences 0/8
**Progress:** They'll pull you toward them. Go with it and strike when you arrive.
**Complete:** Look. Blue beside the gold. Now a child could tell them apart. I've been forty years on this road and that's the best night's work I've seen. Take one of these.
