# Dungeons

[← Master Plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md) · Prev: [Main Chain 2](../06-quests/main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md) · Next: [Raid](raid-the-hollow-throne.md) · [World Bosses](world-bosses.md)

Three 5-player instanced dungeons. Common rules: all creatures are elite; no respawn until reset; 5 instance entries per hour; meeting stone at each entrance; boss loot is Bind on Pickup and Rare (blue) unless stated; each boss drops **2 items** from its table (final boss 3). Intended group: 1 tank, 1 healer, 3 damage — any mix of the three classes works.

Design constraints inherited from the class kit: no encounter *requires* an ally magic-dispel or more than one interrupt (Fighter: Shield Bash/Pummel; Cleric Reckoning: Rebuke at tier 6 only). Crowd control available: Rootgrasp, Lull Beast (beasts), Hammer of Rebuke, Intimidating Shout, Rebuke Undead.

| Dungeon | Level | Zone | Bosses | Length | Enemy types |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gallowmere Crypts | 13–16 | Thornwick Marches | 4 (1 optional) | 35–45 min | Undead, cultists |
| The Slagworks | 20–23 | Cinderfall Barrens | 4 | 45–55 min | Slagborn dwarves, elementals |
| Hollowspire Keep | 27–30 | Veilmoor | 5 | 60–75 min | Hollowed nobles, spectres, constructs |

## 1. Gallowmere Crypts (13–16)

**Story:** the corpse-works of the Cinderbrand. Bodies stolen from Thornwick are carried down, fitted with cinders and raised. See [Main Chain 1](../06-quests/main-chain-1-embers-of-the-brand.md). **Quests:** TM-15, TM-16, TM-17.

**Layout:** Gibbet stair (entrance) → the Charnel Hall → Embalmers' Gallery (side wing, optional chapel) → the Cinder Kiln → the Brandfather's Sanctum. One loop, with a shortcut door back to the entrance opening after boss 2.

| Trash | Lvl | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Crypt Shambler | 14 | Slow, hard-hitting; Rotting Grasp (disease, −10% Str) |
| Cinderbrand Corpse-Stitcher | 14–15 | Caster: Stitch Flesh (heals undead — priority kill), Cinder Bolt |
| Cinderwight Thrall | 14–15 | Burning Grasp; explodes on death (move away) |
| Bone Sentinel | 15 | Shield; Bone Wall (blocks line of sight) |
| Kiln Stoker | 15–16 | Throws coals (ground fire) |
| Gravebound Hound | 14 | Fast pack of 3. Undead, so it cannot be Lulled but can be Rebuked |

### Bosses

**1 — Gravedigger Holt** (15). A huge, simple man with a shovel who thinks he is doing honest work.
- *Shovel Sweep* — frontal cone; tank faces him away.
- *Dig Up* — every 20 s, 2 Crypt Shamblers claw out of marked grave-plots; killing Holt near an open grave is faster because *Six Feet Under* (at 50%) knocks a random player into an open grave for 4 s (others click to pull them out).
- Loot: *Holt's Honest Shovel* (2H mace, 17.8 DPS, +7 Str +6 Sta) · *Gravedigger's Mud-Caked Boots* (mail, +5 Sta +4 Str) · *Burial Shroud Wraps* (cloth wrist, +4 Int +3 Spi) · *Sexton's Lantern* (off-hand, +5 Spi, +7 healing).

**2 — The Stitched Choir** (15). Six corpses sewn into one body; six mouths singing a hymn out of tune.
- *Dirge* — stacking raid-wide Shadow damage that grows while it sings; it stops for 6 s each time a *Mouth* is "silenced" by 400 damage to the boss in 4 s (burst windows) or by Shield Bash.
- *Loose Stitch* — at 66% and 33% an arm falls off and attacks as an add.
- *Discord* — random player: next spell cast costs double.
- Loot: *Choirmaster's Baton* (1H mace, +5 Int, +9 SP) · *Six-Throated Gorget* (neck, +5 Sta +5 Str) · *Stitched-Hide Jerkin* (leather chest, +7 Int +6 Sta) · *Catgut Cord* (leather belt, +5 Agi +5 Sta) · *Choir's Tuning Fork* (quest).

**3 — Sister Maudlin the Unshriven** (15, optional, side chapel). A murdered Dawn-priestess, mad with grief.
- *Wail* — fears the group 3 s every 30 s unless players stand in the *Circle of Candles* (which she extinguishes one candle at a time — the fight has a soft enrage).
- *Sorrow* — a HoT on herself; cannot be dispelled, but each *lit candle* the group stands in halves it.
- *Unshriven Touch* — curse on the tank: healing received −25% (Druid can Lift Curse at 22; at this level it must be healed through — tuned accordingly).
- Loot: *Maudlin's Rosary* (trinket: Use — next heal +120, 2 min CD) · *Veil of the Unshriven* (cloth head, +8 Int +7 Spi) · *Chapel-Stone Signet* (ring, +5 Sta, +4 Defense) · *Candle-Snuffer* (dagger, +4 Int, +6 SP).

**4 — Brandfather Oswin Kray** (16). Defrocked priest; final boss.
- Phase 1: *Searing Brand* (DoT on a random player; the branded player deals +20% damage but takes it too) · *Kindle the Faithful* (raises 2 Cinderwight Thralls from the wall-niches every 25 s) · *Sermon of Ash* (channel: cone of fire, move out).
- Phase 2 (40%): he brands **himself** — +50% attack speed, *Ashen Nova* every 12 s (heavy AoE; hide behind the Bone Walls left by dead Sentinels or use cooldowns).
- Loot (3): *Brandfather's Crozier* (staff, 21 DPS, +9 Int +8 Spi, +11 SP) · *Kray's Searing Iron* (1H mace, 13.6 DPS, chance on hit: 30 Fire) · *Ashen Vestments* (cloth chest) · *Hauberk of the Kindled* (mail chest, +9 Str +8 Sta) · *Cinder-Proof Legguards* (mail legs, +8 Sta +6 Defense) · *Pelt of the Penitent* (leather shoulders, +6 Agi +6 Sta) · *Kray's Dossier* (quest).

**Set — Gallowmere Garb (3 pieces, any class):** *Ashen Vestments* / *Stitched-Hide Jerkin* / *Hauberk of the Kindled* share a 1-piece-per-armor-type "set" tag for cosmetics only.

## 2. The Slagworks (20–23)

**Story:** the Slagborn's great smelter, where Forgemaster Khardun is trying to forge a crown shard into a weapon. See [Main Chain 2](../06-quests/main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md). **Quests:** CF-09, CF-10, CF-11.

**Layout:** Ore Lift (entrance) → Sorting Floor → the Slag Sluice (a river of molten slag with moving crust-platforms) → Bellows Hall (3 Great Bellows to destroy) → the Grand Crucible. Environmental hazard: slag (Fire damage per second, slows).

| Trash | Lvl | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Slagworks Laborer | 21 | Packs of 4 non-elite-strength; flee to pull levers that release slag |
| Slagborn Taskmaster | 22 | Whip Crack (buffs Laborers +30% damage — kill first) |
| Slagborn Slagcaster | 22–23 | Slag Wave (line AoE), Molten Armor |
| Crucible Golem | 23 | Slow; Slam (knockback toward slag) |
| Ember Sprite Swarm | 21 | 6 weak sprites; AoE check |
| Slagborn Shieldbearer | 22–23 | Protects casters behind it (frontal immunity) — flank |

### Bosses

**1 — Overseer Grint** (22). Whip in one hand, ledger in the other.
- *Double Shift* — enrages all Laborers in the room; they must be killed or kited before he's burned.
- *Lash* — bleed on tank. *Dock Pay* — throws his ledger: 3 s stun on the highest-threat non-tank (a tank-swap-lite / threat check).
- Loot: *Grint's Lash* (1H sword styled as a bladed whip, 16.9 DPS, +6 Agi) · *Overseer's Ledger* (off-hand, +7 Int, +8 SP) · *Taskmaster's Bracers* (mail wrist, +5 Str +5 Sta) · *Soot-Stained Trousers* (cloth legs, +8 Int +8 Spi).

**2 — Molten Maw** (22). A slag elemental living in the Sluice; fought from crust-platforms.
- *Engulf* — swallows the current platform after 8 s (players hop to the next).
- *Slag Spit* — pool under a random player. *Cool and Crack* — when lured onto a cooling grate (pull lever), its crust hardens: 10 s of +100% damage taken. The fight is about timing damage cooldowns for grates.
- Immune to Fire; no bleed.
- Loot: *Maw-Core Pendant* (neck, +6 Int +6 Sta, +10 Fire resist) · *Crustbreaker* (2H axe, 25.5 DPS, +11 Str +7 Sta) · *Slag-Walker Sabatons* (mail feet, +7 Sta, +10 Fire resist) · *Cooled-Slag Buckler* (shield) · *Molten Maw's Core* (quest).

**3 — Bellows-Master Thurga & Cinderpaw** (23). A smith and her Pyrehound.
- Thurga (caster): *Stoke* (heals/buffs Cinderpaw — interrupt or outdamage), *Bellows Blast* (knockback line).
- Cinderpaw (Beast — **can be Lulled** by a Druid, the intended "smart" strategy): *Fire Breath*, *Fetch* (drags a healer to Thurga).
- If one dies, the other enrages after 15 s — kill within a window or control Cinderpaw and burn Thurga.
- Loot: *Thurga's Tongs* (1H mace, 17.4 DPS, +5 Str +5 Sta) · *Cinderpaw's Collar* (leather belt, +7 Agi +6 Sta) · *Bellows-Leather Apron* (leather chest, +9 Int +8 Spi, +13 healing) · *Stoker's Mitts* (cloth hands, +6 Int, +9 SP).

**4 — Forgemaster Khardun Slagheart** (23). Final boss; fights at the Grand Crucible where the shard hangs.
- Phase 1: *Hammer of the Forge* (heavy tank hit; a block or Shield Block/Shield of Dawn mitigates the follow-up *Ringing* stun) · *Quench* (throws a player into the quench-trough: 4 s out of the fight, cleanses their debuffs) · *Sparks* (raid AoE pulses).
- Phase 2 (50%): he dips his hammer in the Crucible — *Hollow-Forged* strikes drain mana/rage; *Shard Resonance*: rings of Shadow expand from the Crucible (jump/step through gaps).
- Phase 3 (20%): Crucible cracks; slag floods the floor in sections — a 60 s burn race.
- Loot (3): *Slagheart's Forge-Hammer* (2H mace, 27 DPS, +12 Str +9 Sta) · *Hollow-Quenched Blade* (1H sword, 20.3 DPS, +6 Str, chance on hit: steals 30 health) · *Forgemaster's Greathelm* (mail head, +10 Sta +8 Str +5 Defense) · *Crucible-Tender's Cowl* (leather head, +10 Int +8 Sta, +12 Nature/Arcane SP) · *Mantle of Banked Coals* (cloth shoulders, +8 Int +7 Spi, +14 healing) · *Khardun's Signet-Seal* (ring, +7 Sta +6 Str) · *Slagheart's Crucible-Shard* (raid attunement part, always drops for those on RL-35) · plan: Starforged Longblade / schematic: Starmetal-Barrel Rifle (10% each).

## 3. Hollowspire Keep (27–30)

**Story:** the seat of House Vael, where the last imperial heir has hollowed her whole household. See [Main Chain 2](../06-quests/main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md). **Quests:** VM-29, VM-30, VM-31. Requires the outer wards down (VM-28) for at least one group member.

**Layout:** Gatehouse → Great Hall (a banquet frozen mid-toast) → two wings — the **Library** (west) and the **Barracks Chapel** (east), in either order → the Spire stair → the Crown Chapel. Mood: beautiful, silent, wrong. The hollowed do not shout; they applaud.

| Trash | Lvl | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Hollowed Footman | 28 | Formations of 3; *Hold the Line* |
| Hollowed Chamberlain | 28–29 | Caster: *Announce* (calls the next pack — kill or stun) |
| Spectral Handmaiden | 29 | Phases; heals nobles |
| Animated Armor | 29 | Construct; *Reassemble* unless its helm is destroyed (loot-object click) |
| Ink Wraith (Library) | 29 | Silence 3 s; splits into 2 Blots |
| Hollowed Chaplain (Chapel) | 29–30 | *Inverted Prayer* (damages by player's missing mana) |
| Spire Gargoyle | 30 | Stone Form; pairs |

### Bosses

**1 — Castellan Vorn** (29). The keep's steward, still dutiful.
- *Lock the Gates* — portcullises split the group in two for 15 s; each half faces 2 Footmen.
- *Ring of Keys* — throws keys: the player who catches the glowing key (stand in marker) opens the gate early.
- *Dutiful Strike* — stacking armor reduction on the tank; resets when gates drop.
- Loot: *Castellan's Ring of Keys* (trinket: +28 AP 15 s, 2 min CD) · *Vorn's Gate-Halberd* (polearm, 35 DPS, +13 Str +10 Sta) · *Steward's Chain of Office* (neck, +7 Int +7 Sta) · *Portcullis Greaves* (mail legs, +11 Sta +8 Str +6 Defense).

**2 — The Banquet of Hollows** (event, 29). Twelve seated nobles and Lord-Chamberlain Aubrec.
- The group must **take seats** (clickable) when the toast is called or take heavy Shadow damage; each course animates 3 nobles (themed: *Soup* — slows; *Roast* — knife-throwers; *Wine* — a drunk, hard-hitting baron; *Dessert* — Aubrec himself with all previous mechanics).
- A positional, comedic-horror encounter that tests add control more than throughput.
- Loot: *Aubrec's Carving Knife* (dagger, 19.6 DPS, +6 Agi) · *Baron's Wine-Stained Doublet* (leather chest, +11 Agi +9 Sta) · *Banquet-Hall Tablecloth* (cloak, +6 Int +6 Spi) · *Silver Service Tray* (shield, +8 Sta, +9 block) · *Signet of the Empty Chair* (ring, +7 Int, +8 SP).

**3 — Librarian Quill, the Ink-Drowned** (30, west wing).
- *Overdue* — marks a player; an Ink Wraith chases them (kite through light-shafts to dissolve it).
- *Redact* — silences casters standing in ink pools (pools spread from kills — add placement matters).
- *Index* — at 50% and 25% he opens a great book: the shelf that glows names the school he becomes immune to (Nature / Arcane / Holy / Physical) for 20 s — hybrids and the Druid's two DPS schools shine here.
- Loot: *Quill's Quill* (staff, 31 DPS, +11 Int, +16 Arcane SP) · *Ink-Drowned Robes* (cloth chest, +12 Int +9 Sta, +14 SP) · *Bookbinder's Gloves* (leather hands, +8 Int +7 Spi, +15 healing) · *Marginalia* (off-hand, +8 Int, +1% spell crit) · *Librarian's Index* (quest).

**4 — Ser Aldous the Faithless** (30, east wing). A Cleric of the Dawn who swore to protect Seraphine and broke his faith to keep his oath — a dark mirror of the player Cleric, using inverted class abilities.
- *Oath of Hollow* + *Verdict* — big Shadow strike on a random player, telegraphed by the Oath buff (stun him with Hammer of Rebuke / Concussive Blow during the buff to cancel it).
- *Unhallowed Ground* — a growing desecrated zone under him; tank must keep him moving.
- *Hollow Ward* at 30% — immune 8 s while healing; a Cleric's **Verdict (Radiance)** or 10 Holy-damage hits breaks it early.
- Loot: *Aldous's Broken Vow* (2H mace, 35.4 DPS, +14 Str +9 Int) · *Faithless Knight's Breastplate* (mail chest, +13 Sta +10 Str +7 Defense) · *Pauldrons of the Kept Oath* (mail shoulders, +9 Str +8 Sta) · *Psalter of Doubt* (Cleric relic: Mending Light +22) · *Aldous' Oathblade* (quest).

**5 — Lady Seraphine Vael** (30, Crown Chapel). Final boss.
- Phase 1 — *The Lady*: she does not attack. Three **Shards** orbit her and cast: *Shard of Ambition* (Fire beams sweeping the room), *Shard of Memory* (creates a hollow copy of a random player using 2 of their abilities), *Shard of Hunger* (drains health from the furthest player — stack or spread choice). Kill the shards' *Reflections* to make her vulnerable.
- Phase 2 (60%) — *The Heir*: she takes up the family blade. *Imperial Decree* (a random player must stop moving / stop casting / stop attacking for 4 s or take heavy damage), *Courtly Step* (teleports behind the healer).
- Phase 3 (25%) — *The Voice*: Vaelric speaks through her. *Crown's Shadow* pulses raid-wide and increases; a pure burn. At 0% the scripted ending of [VM-29](../06-quests/main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md#act-iv--the-heir-levels-2730--veilmoor) plays.
- Loot (3): *Heir's Regret* (1H sword, 24.6 DPS, +8 Str +7 Sta) · *Seraphine's Sceptre* (1H mace, +9 Int, +20 healing) · *Gown of the Last Heir* (cloth chest, +13 Int +11 Spi, +16 SP) · *Vael Heirloom Signet* (ring, +8 Sta +8 Int) · *Hollowspire Pauldrons* (leather shoulders, +10 Int +8 Sta, +12 SP) · *Girdle of Imperial Blood* (mail belt, +10 Str +9 Sta) · *Charm of the Empty Gown* (Druid relic: Renewal heals +18 in total) · *Seraphine's Signet* (raid attunement part) · plan: Starmetal Bulwark / pattern: Grovekeeper's Mantle (10% each).

## 4. Dungeon data needs

Each dungeon is a map + spawn set + encounter scripts. Encounter scripts use the shared timeline system (see [NPC AI](../02-systems/npc-ai-and-world.md#2-ai-state-machine)): timed abilities, health-phase triggers, add spawns, interactive objects (graves, levers, seats, candles), and room-wide state (slag level, gate state).

Tuning targets (appropriate-level group in quest greens and a few blues): boss fights 2–4 min; healer mana 20–40% remaining; a single avoidable mechanic missed is survivable, two is a likely death.
