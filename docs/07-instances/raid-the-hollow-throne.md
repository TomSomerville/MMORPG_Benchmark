## Setting

The throne-vault of Tor Vael, swallowed by the earth 312 years ago and held open ever since by failing wards. Walls of packed rubble hang suspended beside the walkways; entombed courtiers can be seen in the stone like insects in amber. Sections: **the Vault Gate** → **the Hall of Petitioners** → **the Buried Parade Ground** / **the Choir Vault** (either order) → **the Aether Conservatory** → **the Throne of Tor Vael**.

Raid-wide mechanic — **Ward Strain:** each boss room has ward-pylons holding the ceiling up. Avoidable damage taken by the raid cracks them; if all break the room collapses (wipe). This gives a visible, shared "mistake meter".

## Bosses

### 1 — The Bulwark of Tor Vael
A colossal ward-golem set into the Vault Gate. *The gear check.*
- **Seismic Slam** — heavy tank hit every 12 s, leaves *Fractured* (armor −20%, stacks). **Tank swap** at 3 stacks.
- **Rockfall** — one of four floor-lanes is marked; the ceiling drops along it (move to a clear lane).
- **Ward Pulse** — raid AoE every 30 s; healers' throughput check.
- **Cave-In** (soft enrage at 6 min) — Rockfalls double.
- Loot: *Gauntlets* tier token (**Vaultforged Grips**) · *Bulwark's Keystone* (shield, +12 Sta +9 Defense +18 block) · *Gate-Chain Girdle* (mail belt) · *Wardstone Loop* (ring, +9 Sta +9 Int) · *Gate-Breaker* (1H mace, 27 DPS, +7 Str +7 Sta).

### 2 — The Echo Sisters (council of three)
Three imperial choristers, entombed mid-hymn in the Choir Vault: **Lirael** (song), **Morwen** (storm), **Ysa** (stone). They must die within 15 s of each other or revive.
- Lirael: *Beckon* — charms the nearest 2 players (break with damage); stand far.
- Morwen: *Chain Squall* — a dust-storm that arcs as Nature lightning; spread 8 yd.
- Ysa: *Downdraft* — pulls everyone to her, then *Crush*; run out after the pull.
- *Harmony* — when two sisters are within 15 yd they heal each other: **tanks keep them apart** (needs 2 tanks + a kiter; a Druid with Rootgrasp or a Fighter kiting Ysa works).
- Loot: *Chorister's Whisper* (dagger, +8 Int, +17 SP) · *Sash of Three Voices* (cloth belt) · *Bracers of the Downdraft* (leather wrist) · *Morwen's Storm-Stone* (trinket: +32 Nature SP 20 s, 2 min) · *Lirael's Lullaby* (neck, +9 Int +8 Spi, +20 healing).

### 3 — Marshal Korvash the Entombed
Commander of the Tor Vael garrison, still drilling his legion on the Buried Parade Ground. (Lore from RL-32.)
- **Ballista Volley** — spectral siege engines fire down marked lanes.
- **Fall In!** — 4 Buried Legionaries march from the barracks every 40 s; off-tank gathers them; AoE.
- **Entomb** — the floor swallows a random player for 6 s (heavy damage; healers pre-HoT — *Druid Verdance showcase*).
- **Hold the Line** (30%) — the ceiling starts to come down in slabs; fight under the three intact arches, which fall one by one.
- Loot: *Shoulders* tier token (**Vaultforged Mantle**) · *Korvash's War-Axe* (1H axe, 27.4 DPS, +8 Str +6 Agi) · *Marshal's Dust-Grey Greatcoat* (leather chest) · *Field-Glass of the Last Watch* (trinket, +2% hit) · *Parade-Chain Bracers* (mail wrist).

### 4 — Ghul'thar, Spawn of the Rift Maw
A burrowing wyrm the size of a street, coiled through the Conservatory's broken dome. Fought on three ledges.
- **Undermine** — one ledge is burrowed out from beneath (8 s warning); everyone moves to the next.
- **Grit Breath** — frontal cone of scouring sand; tank faces it outward.
- **Spawn** — eggs on the walls hatch if not killed (ranged job).
- **Maw phase** at 50% — it swallows the main tank; the tank fights *Stomach Polyps* inside (dealing damage to the boss from within) while the off-tank holds; swap when spat out.
- Loot: *Legs* tier token (**Vaultforged Legguards**) · *Ghul'thar's Fang* (2H sword, 46 DPS, +18 Str +12 Sta) · *Wyrmskin Leggings* (leather) · *Polyp-Lined Cloak* (cloak, +10 Sta, +10 Nature resist) · *Rift-Spawn Scale* (shield spike/crafting reserve).

### 5 — Archmagister Ilyra, the Hollow Voice
Vaelric's court mage, who performed the binding 312 years ago and has been reciting it ever since.
- **Aether Polarity** — players are marked *Flow* or *Ebb*; same marks stack for +damage, opposite marks near each other explode.
- **Recitation** — a 10 s cast that wipes the raid unless **interrupted three times** in sequence (Shield Bash, Pummel, and stuns from Concussive Blow or Hammer of Rebuke all count). *The coordination check; designed for what the launch classes actually have.*
- **Hollow Lance** — tank-buster Shadow; *Potion of Hollow Warding* matters.
- **Mirror of Years** (40%) — each player fights a hollow copy of themselves for 20 s (personal responsibility).
- Loot: *Head* tier token (**Vaultforged Crown**) · *Ilyra's Binding Rod* (staff, 44 DPS, +14 Int, +24 SP) · *Voice of the Hollow* (off-hand, +10 Int, +14 SP, +1% crit) · *Slippers of the Recitation* (cloth feet) · *Band of Flow and Ebb* (ring).

### 6 — Vaelric, the Hollow King
The last Emperor, on his throne, the Hollow Crown whole on his brow. Three phases, ~9 minutes.
- **Phase 1 — The Emperor (100–65%).** *Imperial Edict* (as Seraphine's Decree, but two simultaneous orders to different groups) · *Sceptre Strike* (tank) · *Summon Court* (4 Hollow Courtiers that must be killed before they finish "kneeling", or he gains +10% damage each).
- **Phase 2 — The Crown (65–30%).** The five shards lift from the Crown and anchor to five players (*Crown-bearers*) who each get a unique burden: **Ambition** (deal +50% damage, take +50%), **Memory** (your abilities are echoed against the raid), **Hunger** (drain allies within 8 yd), **Grief** (cannot be healed above 50%), **Pride** (must not move). Bearers rotate every 30 s. The raid kills *Hollow Echoes* to weaken each shard.
- **Phase 3 — The Collapse (30–0%).** The wards fail. The vault caves in in rings from the edge inward (shrinking arena); *Earthen Wrath* pulses; *Bury* targets a healer (must be dug free). All cooldowns; a burn. Clerics' *Litany of Sanctity*, Fighters' *Recklessness*, Druids' *Stillwater* are the intended closers.
- **Death:** the Crown shatters; see the [chain ending](../06-quests/main-chain-2-the-hollow-crown.md#finale--the-hollow-king-level-30).
- Loot (3): *Chest* tier token (**Vaultforged Raiment**) · *Sceptre of the Last Emperor* (1H mace, 29 DPS, +10 Int, +30 healing) · *Sundering* (2H axe, 48 DPS, +20 Str +12 Sta, chance: 90 Shadow) · *Crownfall* (1H sword, 29.5 DPS, +9 Str +8 Sta, +1% crit) · *Vaelric's Sorrow* (staff, +16 Int +12 Spi, +28 SP) · *Throne-Guard's Rampart* (shield) · *Mantle of the Buried Court* (cloak) · *Hollow Shard Fragment* (legendary-quest reserve, 5%) · mount drop *Reins of the Vault Charger* (1%).

## Tier 1 sets — "Vaultforged" (5 pieces per class, Epic, level 30)

Tokens are class-agnostic (*Vaultforged Grips/Mantle/Legguards/Crown/Raiment*) and are exchanged at Spire Camp for the wearer's class piece; each class set has stats that suit all three specs, with the spec flavour in the bonuses.

| Class | Set name | 2-piece bonus | 4-piece bonus | 5-piece bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fighter | **Riftbreaker Battlegear** (mail) | +20 attack power, +8 Defense | Bloodletting no longer costs health; Shield Block +1 s | Your crits and blocks have a chance to grant 5 rage |
| Cleric | **Dawnwarden Regalia** (mail) | +14 SP / +26 healing, +10 Str | Verdict cooldown −1 s | Mending Light and Dawnstrike have a 10% chance to cost no mana |
| Druid | **Stonebloom Raiment** (leather) | +16 SP / +30 healing | Renewal, Starbrand and Stinging Swarm last 3 s longer | Each tick of your periodic effects has a 5% chance to restore 30 mana |

Typical piece (Fighter chest, ilvl 42): 480 armor, +16 Str, +15 Sta, +7 Defense, +1% crit.

## Rewards outside loot

Athenaeum reputation per boss (to Exalted over ~10 clears: resistance enchants, the Robe of the Verdant Choir pattern at Revered) · quest [RL-36](../05-zones/t4-riftlands.md#quests) · title *Crownbreaker*.

## Tuning philosophy

- First kill of boss 1 expected in pre-raid blues; Vaelric expected after ~4–6 weeks of tier and world-boss epics.
- Every fight has **one** primary idea and at most two secondary mechanics.
- Each of the nine specs has at least one encounter where its unique tool is the cleanest solution (noted in italics above).
- No fight requires an ally magic-dispel; *Purification Potions* are a soft advantage on bosses 2 and 5.
