# World Bosses

[← Master Plan](../../MASTER_PLAN.md) · Prev: [Raid](raid-the-hollow-throne.md) · Next: [Architecture](../08-tech/architecture.md)

Three outdoor raid bosses, one per T4 zone. They are un-instanced: whoever is there fights.

## Common rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Level | 33 (skull) |
| Intended force | One 20-player raid group at level 30 (two 10-player groups can also compete for the tag) |
| Respawn | Random 3–5 days after death; spawn announced zone-wide with a weather change (not a timer) |
| Tagging | First raid group to deal 5% of health holds the tag; loot goes to that group. If the tagged group wipes, the boss resets and the tag is released |
| Loot | 4 Epic items per kill from the boss table + 1 quest item for everyone on the zone's world-boss quest (CF-34 / RL-34 / VM-35) |
| Leash | Large (150 yd); evades and heals if dragged out |
| Lockout | Loot-eligible once per character per spawn |
| Future PvP | When allegiances arrive, world bosses in contested zones become three-way fights over the tag |

## 1. Grimroot, the Hangman's Oak

**Where:** Hangman's Hill, [Veilmoor](../05-zones/t4-veilmoor.md). **What:** an ancient oak the Wildpact once revered, used as a gallows for a century until it woke hateful. Wildpact Blightwatchers camp nearby and help (NPC archers).

- **Root Lash** — tank cleave; two tanks alternate when *Splintered* stacks reach 4.
- **Gallows Vines** — hoists 3 random players into the branches, choking (DoT). Others must kill the *Noose* vines to drop them.
- **Rotfall** — rotten fruit rains in marked circles (Nature damage, leaves poison — Druids/Clerics cleanse).
- **Saplings** — 6 Grimroot Saplings rise every 45 s and crawl to the trunk to heal it; ranged and Rootgrasp kite/kill.
- **Heartwood Exposed** (30%) — bark splits; +100% damage taken but *Dying Rage*: Root Lash hits everyone in melee.
- Resists Nature 50% (Stormcall Druids swap to Starbrand/Lance or heal).

## 2. Pyrraxis, the Cinder Wyrm

**Where:** Pyrraxis' Roost, [Cinderfall Barrens](../05-zones/t4-cinderfall-barrens.md). **What:** a fire-drake matriarch grown fat on Slagborn tribute.

- **Ground phase:** *Flame Breath* (frontal cone), *Tail Sweep* (rear knockback — stand at the flanks), *Wing Buffet* (threat drop on the tank: off-tank taunts — Fighters' Taunt/Cleric's Challenge of Faith).
- **Air phase** (every 90 s, 25 s): she strafes the plateau with fire lines; *Cinderwing Drakes* (3) land and must be tanked. Ranged can hit her in the air at reduced damage.
- **Ashfall** — stacking raid-wide Fire DoT that only clears while standing in the steam of the *Emberwell geysers* (which erupt on a rotation) — the raid moves as one.
- **Immolate the Roost** (20%) — the plateau burns from the edges inward.
- Immune to Fire; Fire Protection Potions and Cinderscale crafted gear are the intended preparation.

## 3. Gorrath, the Rift Maw

**Where:** the Maw-Scar, [the Riftlands](../05-zones/t4-riftlands.md). **What:** a burrowing wyrm as long as a city wall, parent of the raid's Ghul'thar; it breaches at the Maw-Scar to feed when dust-storms come. Fought on terraced rock riddled with its tunnels.

- **Maw** — inhales: everyone is pulled toward its mouth for 6 s (run away; anyone reaching it is swallowed and spat out at 20% health).
- **Coils** — 4 loops of its body erupt from the ground across the field; each pins a player. Killing a coil damages Gorrath for 3% (the fight is mostly won on coils).
- **Landslide** — the upper terrace gives way; players on the lower terraces are swept to the bottom of the Scar (run back up through *Chasm Lurkers*). Climb when the ground settles.
- **Dust Plume** — blinds an area (hit chance −50%); melee move out.
- **Rift Spawn** — young wyrm adds for off-tanks.
- **Going to Ground** (25%) — it begins to burrow away; if not killed within 90 s it escapes and despawns (no loot).

## Why world bosses exist at launch

- A reason for the max-level population to gather outdoors on a schedule that is *not* a schedule.
- An alternative epic source for players without a raid group (pick-up raids are viable).
- A stress test for the server's worst case: 40+ players, heavy effects, one location (see [Architecture](../08-tech/architecture.md#9-performance-targets)).
- A ready-made three-faction flashpoint for the future.
